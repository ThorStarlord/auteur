# Errata to the PR #553 prose and the H1 path

Status: **working / noncanonical.** The construction's prose files and the H1 path document are part of PR #553, and this work must not rewrite them. Each erratum gives the exact text to change, the defect it removes, and the finding behind it. The errata apply to the prose only. The candidate-level changes are in [w3-changes.md](w3-changes.md) (round 1) and [round-2-changes.md](round-2-changes.md) (round 2).

**Numbering.** "01", "02" and "04" refer to PR #553's own file names: `01-causal-construction.md`, `02-finale-alternatives.md` and `04-h1-focused-delta.md`. The last of these is not in the verification packets. "§" refers to the sections of those files.

## E1. 02, Alternative A, mechanism 1 and the Principle (Stage A R1, round 1 F-7, round 2 revised)

- **Mechanism 1, current:** "Each practice's release depends on the Gate schedule the other practice agrees to. A terrace that stops returning labour to the shared channels loses its own release at the next thaw. Nobody issues a penalty. The schedule follows the balance reading, which anyone can check, so a terrace that stops returning labour shows in its own release at the next thaw."
- **Mechanism 1, proposed:** "A terrace that stops maintaining the shared channels can leave them unable to carry the water of every terrace that uses them. That is a physical consequence, not a penalty. A rule that reduces a terrace's release for lacking labour would be an institutional rule, and it would be a cost of A. A does not include that rule."
- **Principle, current:** "Participation holds because each household's own water depends on its neighbours' labour, and the schedule that follows the balance makes that dependence visible in each household's own release."
- **Principle, proposed:** "Participation holds because each household's water runs through shared channels that its neighbours maintain, and the tally shows who maintains them."
- **Paragraph "How it differs from the Balance Stones", first sentence after the Stones, current:** "Mechanisms 1 and 2 turn the same information into dependence."
- **Proposed:** "Mechanism 2 turns the same information into dependence. Mechanism 1 adds no dependence of its own: it is a fact about the shared channels."
- **Why (round 3):** the Principle credited the balance reading with a job that mechanism 4 gives to the tally. The paragraph still described mechanism 1 as a dependence, which the revised mechanism 1 is not.
- **Why:** the first version joined a physical consequence to a scheduling rule and called the result "no penalty". The Principle repeated the coupling. The trace of A uses no such rule, so the comparison is unaffected.

## E2. 02, Alternative A, mechanism 5 (Stage A R2, credible concern)

- **Current:** "The floor is funded by the lineage's Right, and the Gate Table administers it. The Table has no authority over shares or the record."
- **Proposed:** add at the end: "The floor reduces the Right's draw, so its authority over shares is the Right's holders' consent, not the Table's. A does not say who holds that authority if the lineage withholds consent."
- **Why:** the trace shows that the floor works only through the lineage's consent. This records the dependence without choosing a holder, which is an open author choice.

## E3. 02, Alternative B, "What binds" item 2 and the costs (Stage A R3 and R4, verified defect and credible concern; round 2 rewritten)

- **In "What binds", item 2, replace:** "It binds the water, not the labour: the gift practice's labour stays uncounted."
- **With:** "It binds the water for every practice. For the window it also binds the gift practice's labour, because its shares are inside the Long Rota, so its labour is counted for that window. This is a cost of B. The author has not confirmed it."
- **Add to B's costs:** "Deficits from the rota must be recorded by a keeper. The Reading Hall would keep the record, which reintroduces a debt ledger for the window."
- **Why:** the round-1 text said the gift practice's labour "stays uncounted" while also putting its shares inside the rota, which is a contradiction. The round-1 text also said "the author confirms it", which records a decision the packet says has not been made. The round-2 text says the author has not confirmed it.

## E4. 02, Alternative C, the Costs (Stage A R6, verified defect; round 2 adds a note)

## E5. 01, §1 Hollow Gate row and §3 option (b) (Stage A R8, verified defect; round 2 revised)

- **§1, current:** "This construction reads the surfeit as an effect of the release the trial's schedule carried to the lower channels."
- **§1, proposed:** "The Source responds to the balance between draw and return. The construction assumes that the trial's uncounted return exceeded the draw the Turn counted, so the surplus showed in the high cistern. The original does not name this mechanism, and the author may reject it. Release draws the cistern down and cannot raise it."
- **§3, option (b), current:** "(b) Release at need regardless. The high terraces absorb the surplus."
- **§3, option (b), proposed:** "(b) Release at need regardless. The high cistern bears the drawdown."
- **Why:** release from the cistern draws it down, so it cannot cause surfeit, and a dry spring has no surplus to absorb.

## E6. 01, §5 Book 23, the first bullet (Stage A R10, verified defect)

- **Current:** "After two good thaws, a real surplus sits in the high cistern for two reasons. The Table holds water back in dry spells instead of releasing it, and lineage crews have sealed the leaks in the high channels that used to feed wild water (Book 20). The surplus is real and seasonal."
- **Proposed:** "After two good thaws, a real surplus sits in the high cistern. The Table holds water back in dry spells instead of releasing it. The surplus is real and seasonal."
- **Why:** Book 20's repair was to the Lowfen, a lower channel, and Arc IV contains no sealing of the high channels. The second cause had no source, so it is removed rather than invented into Arc IV.

## E8. 01, §1 item 3, "Two proven practices" (round 3, the surfeit logic)

- **Current:** "The counted Turn (high terraces) keeps the high cistern from surfeit."
- **Proposed:** "The counted Turn (high terraces) matches return to draw, which keeps the high cistern from surfeit."
- **Why:** the surfeit logic is the Source's balance: surfeit is too much return, so matching return to draw is what keeps the cistern from it. Labour as such does not hold surfeit off.
