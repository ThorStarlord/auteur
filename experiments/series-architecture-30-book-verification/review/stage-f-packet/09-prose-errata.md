# Errata to the PR #553 prose and the H1 path

Status: **working / noncanonical.** The construction's prose files (01, 02) and the H1 path document (04) are part of PR #553, and this work must not rewrite them. Each erratum below gives the exact text to change, the defect it removes, and the finding behind it. The errata apply to the prose only. The candidate-level changes are in [w3-changes.md](w3-changes.md).

---

## E1. 02, Alternative A, mechanism 1

- **Current:** "Each practice's release depends on the Gate schedule the other practice agrees to. A terrace that stops returning labour to the shared channels loses its own release at the next thaw. Nobody issues a penalty. The schedule follows the balance reading, which anyone can check, so a terrace that stops returning labour shows in its own release at the next thaw."
- **Proposed:** "A terrace that stops maintaining its share of the shared channels cannot carry its own water at the next thaw. That is a physical consequence, not a penalty. A rule that reduces a terrace's release for lacking labour would be an institutional rule, and it would be a cost of A. A does not include that rule."

## E2. 02, Alternative A, mechanism 5

- **Current:** "The floor is funded by the lineage's Right, and the Gate Table administers it. The Table has no authority over shares or the record."
- **Proposed:** add at the end: "The floor reduces the Right's draw, so its authority over shares is the Right's holders' consent, not the Table's. A does not say who holds that authority if the lineage withholds consent."

## E3. 02, Alternative B, "What binds" and "What prevents capture"

- **Add under "What binds":** "The gift practice's shares are inside the window's rota. This is the scenario's assumption and is a cost of B, not a neutral reading. The author confirms it. The Reading Hall keeps the deficit record, which reintroduces a debt ledger for the window."

## E4. 02, Alternative C, the Costs

- **Current:** "The threshold becomes an authority in itself. The surveyor's reading decides whether the rule applies."
- **Proposed:** "The threshold becomes an authority in itself. The Reading Hall's published reading decides whether the rule applies."

## E5. 01, §1 Hollow Gate row and §3 option (b)

- **§1, current:** "This construction reads the surfeit as an effect of the release the trial's schedule carried to the lower channels."
- **§1, proposed:** "This construction assumes that the trial's uncounted return labour fed the high channels beyond the Turn's count, so stored water rose with no draw. Release draws the cistern down and cannot raise it."
- **§3, option (b), current:** "(b) Release at need regardless. The high terraces absorb the surplus."
- **§3, option (b), proposed:** "(b) Release at need regardless. The high cistern bears the drawdown."

## E6. 01, §5 Book 23, the first bullet

- **Current:** "After two good thaws, a real surplus sits in the high cistern for two reasons. The Table holds water back in dry spells instead of releasing it, and lineage crews have sealed the leaks in the high channels that used to feed wild water (Book 20). The surplus is real and seasonal."
- **Proposed:** "After two good thaws, a real surplus sits in the high cistern. The Table holds water back in dry spells instead of releasing it. The surplus is real and seasonal."

## E7. Terminology: "upper" and "high" cistern

- **Add to 01 §1, after the first sentence of the table's introduction:** "The construction uses 'high cistern' throughout. The original's 'upper cistern' (Books 1 and 6) is the same structure. The author may rename it."
