# Reviewer instructions: independent review of a working narrative architecture

You are an independent reviewer. You did not write this architecture, and you have not seen any earlier review of it.

## What to read

Read only the files in this folder. Do not open any other file or folder on this machine, including any repository, worktree, review, synthesis or evidence file. If a file here refers to a document you cannot see, say so in your report and carry on.

## What not to do

Do not modify, create or delete any file. Do not use the network. Return your whole report as your final message.

## Mandate

Evaluate the final corrected 30-Book architecture on its own merits. Determine whether its causal mechanisms, institutional arrangements, physical constraints, and distant narrative payoffs are mutually coherent. Identify consequential contradictions, missing necessities, or unsupported claims. Distinguish deliberate author choices from design defects. Do not assume that the previous designer's preferred mechanism is correct. Do not manufacture defects merely because the architecture remains incomplete.

Review the questions below without presupposing that their suspected problems are real. There is no prose in this packet. Do not judge prose quality, and do not give literary scores.

## Labels in the packet

- **[Fixed]**: the author's fixed brief (file 01).
- **[Working]**: a construction proposal.
- **[Derived]**: follows from a named earlier Book or mechanism.
- **[Open]**: an author choice that is not decided.

The design documents also contain the designer's own characterisations, such as a comparison table, "costs" lists, and notes like "(not chosen)" or "working direction for testing". Treat those as claims to test, not as evidence. Some designer-only passages have been withheld from this packet. Each withholding is marked in the text as "[Withheld by design: ...]".

## Areas

**A. Arc IV.**
- Do the Counted Rota and Two Practices, One Gate arise from established history?
- Do they create genuinely different pressures?
- Do their outcomes establish the material conditions inherited by Books 23 and 30?
- Does the Hollow Gate require an unsupported addition to the original physical system?

**B. Alternative A (noncoercive mutual dependence).**
- The architecture states that nobody loses water merely for lacking labour. It also states that a terrace which stops maintaining shared channels can lose its next release. Determine whether these are coherent statements about different causes, or whether an unacknowledged penalty has been introduced. Distinguish physical consequences, institutional scheduling, and deliberate coercion.
- Examine whether the Gate Table can administer a care floor without possessing authority over shares.

**C. Alternative B (bounded binding arrangement).**
- Determine precisely which obligations bind: water allocation; maintenance labour; temporary deficits; participation in the rota; the Compact's contribution.
- Check that the arrangement does not assert incompatible meanings of "binding", and does not contradict the gift practice without acknowledging the change.

**D. Alternative C (ratified trigger).**
- Does the threshold create an unintended concentration of authority?
- Who interprets the gauge? Who decides whether the threshold has been crossed? Who activates the rule? Who can challenge a mistaken reading?
- Does the proposed separation of record authority and scheduling authority survive activation?

**E. Cross-artifact consistency.**
- Check that the prose construction (files 03 and 04), the candidate (file 05) and the original architecture (file 02) describe compatible events, mechanisms and author choices.
- Do not treat an unchanged identifier as proof of unchanged meaning.

## Classification

Classify each consequential finding as one of:
- **Verified defect**: a contradiction or impossibility you can show from the text. Quote the text.
- **Credible architectural concern**: a consequence the text does not handle, which you judge would matter. Give your reasoning.
- **Deliberate author choice**: the text marks it [Open] or author-reserved. Say what it changes.
- **Unsupported claim**: asserted with no mechanism in the text.
- **Unresolved question**: you cannot decide it from the text.

Distinguish a defect from a choice you would have made differently.

## Report format

1. **Overall assessment** (at most 200 words).
2. **Findings**, most consequential first. For each: an ID (R1, R2, ...), area (A to E), classification, location (file and node, link, phase or section id), a short quotation, your explanation, and what would settle it.
3. **Justified strengths**: the mechanisms you judge sound, and why.
4. **What you could not assess**, and why.
5. **Your context**: anything you know or suspect about this architecture from outside this folder. Write "none" if nothing.
