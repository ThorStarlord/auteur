- Earlier history: W2 is the output of a correction pass over an earlier candidate, and that earlier candidate was reviewed before W2 was produced. That review and the earlier candidate are not in this packet, by design. W2 itself has not been reviewed by anyone outside its author.
- The file hashes are recorded in the coordinator's manifest, not in this folder.

## Status

- Working and noncanonical. Nothing here is accepted Identity, Structure, Series, Book or canon. No author has approved it.
- The labels inside the candidate are the designer's labels. For example, its summary and one node title call Alternative C "the working direction for testing". Those labels record the designer's preference and are not evidence.

## Product constraints on file 05

- It is a schema-v2 architecture candidate. It has nodes (each with a category), links (each with a function of at most 120 characters), phases (each with genres and roles), genre contracts, emotion targets (each with a provenance field), complex emotions, genre interactions, open questions (at most 16), and a summary (at most 3000 characters).
- The candidate has no institution category and no Book node. Book functions are given as free text in the phase narratives and scopes.
- The product checks the structure and identity of the candidate. It does not check whether the meanings in the candidate are consistent with each other. Schema validity is not evidence of semantic consistency or literary quality.

## Constraints on the architecture (from the author's brief, file 01)

- No new villain, no magical rescue, and no unlimited source of labour. The physical world follows its established behaviour: thinning, surfeit, channel labour and water routing.
- Victories change the world. They do not restore the earlier situation, and some conflicts come from earlier successes.
- No escalating antagonists, arbitrary threats or indefinitely postponed revelations. Local closure with continuing long-range questions is allowed.

## Author-reserved decisions (not decided in this packet)

- Whether the shared accounting of care binds, and in what form.
- Whether Alternative A, B or C is the finale direction.
- Whether a Balance Stones threshold exists (C), at what level, and whether its activation is automatic or confirmed.
- Whether the flood costs lives, and which channel takes the Long Return's surge.
- The Compact's fate: whether grain-labour settles its claim, and whether it has a seat.
- Whether reform from within is the Series' stance, and whether the Gate Table models it or warns against it.
- Whether the Old Hand needs intermediate states between Books 8 and 15.
- Whether the lineage's Special Draw Right is revocable or permanent, and what its training condition is.
- Whether the Hollow Gate and the Gate Table are accepted parts of the world.
