# Source identity and product constraints (Stage B)

## Identity

- File 05 is the final corrected candidate (W2) from PR #553. It is the same candidate as in the first review packet. It is working and noncanonical, and no author has approved it.
- File 09 is derived from file 05 by a recorded script (`make_h1_path.py`), which applies a focused delta. It is hypothetical. It is not accepted, and it is not a second complete candidate. Its summary begins with a label that says so.
- File 07 is the premise, as the author proposed it for the counterfactual. File 08 is the revision instruction that was applied to file 05 to produce file 09. Its text describes the original path as "evidence and a coalition from outside". That description is part of the instruction as applied.
- File 10 is generated mechanically by comparing files 05 and 09 by identifier. It records what changed. It is not a judgment about what should change.
- Files 01 to 04 are the same as in the first review packet: the author's fixed brief, the original architecture, the causal construction, and the finale alternatives.

## Product constraints

- The product checks the structure of file 09 (schema v2) and the identity of its elements. It does not check whether the meanings are consistent with each other. Schema validity is not evidence of semantic coherence.
- A preservation check that compares identifiers only would report an element as unchanged even if its meaning had changed.

## Constraints on the architecture

- No new villain, no magical rescue, and no unlimited source of labour. The physical world follows its established behaviour: thinning, surfeit, channel labour and water routing.
- Victories change the world. They do not restore the earlier situation.
- No escalating antagonists, arbitrary threats or indefinitely postponed revelations.

## Author-reserved decisions (not decided in this packet)

- Whether the shared accounting of care binds, and in what form.
- Whether Alternative A, B or C is the finale direction.
- Whether a Balance Stones threshold exists (C), at what level, and whether its activation is automatic or confirmed.
- Whether the flood costs lives, and which channel takes the Long Return's surge.
- The Compact's fate: whether grain-labour settles its claim, and whether it has a seat.
- Whether reform from within is the Series' stance, and whether the Gate Table models it or warns against it.
- Whether the Old Hand needs intermediate states between Books 8 and 15.
- Whether the lineage's Special Draw Right is revocable or permanent, and what its training condition is.
- Whether the Hollow Gate and the Gate Table are accepted parts of the world.
- Whether the premise in file 07 is adopted at all. The premise is an author-proposed counterfactual, and it is not accepted.
