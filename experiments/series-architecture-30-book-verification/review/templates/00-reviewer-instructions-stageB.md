Evaluate on its own merits whether the revised candidate is coherent. ## Labels in the packet

- **[Fixed]**: the author's fixed brief (file 01).
- **[Working]**: a construction proposal.
- **[Derived]**: follows from a named earlier Book or mechanism.
- **[Open]**: an author choice that is not decided.

The design documents also contain the designer's own characterisations, such as a comparison table, "costs" lists, and notes like "(not chosen)" or "working direction for testing". Treat those as claims to test, not as evidence. Some designer-only passages have been withheld from this packet. Each withholding is marked in the text as "[Withheld by design: ...]".

## Classification

Classify each consequential finding as one of:

- **Verified defect**: a contradiction or impossibility you can show from the text. Quote the text.
- **Credible architectural concern**: a consequence the text does not handle, which you judge would matter. Give your reasoning.
- **Deliberate author choice**: the text marks it [Open] or author-reserved. Say what it changes.
- **Unsupported claim**: asserted with no mechanism in the text.
- **Unresolved question**: you cannot decide it from the text.

Distinguish a defect from a choice you would have made differently.

## Report format

1. **Overall assessment** (at most 200 words).
2. **Findings**, most consequential first. For each: an ID (R1, R2, ...), classification, location (file and node, link, phase, element or section id), a short quotation, your explanation, and what would settle it.
3. **Preserved-element table**, as described above.
4. **Justified strengths**: the parts of the revision you judge sound, and why.
5. **What you could not assess**, and why.
6. **Your context**: anything you know or suspect about this architecture from outside this folder. Write "none" if nothing.
