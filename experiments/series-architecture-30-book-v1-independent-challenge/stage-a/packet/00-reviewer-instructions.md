## Mandate

"Evaluate whether this working 30-Book Series architecture can plausibly support the intended reader experience, causal transformations, meaningful Book-level development, and long-range payoffs. Identify its strongest demonstrated mechanisms, its most consequential weaknesses if any, and the smallest level of revision that might improve it. Distinguish intentionally unresolved future details from consequences that the architecture actually needs to establish. Do not assume that every missing Book synopsis is a defect. Do not assume that the intended finale is valid simply because it is described. Support judgments with the supplied architecture."

## Dimensions to assess

- **A. Distinctiveness.** What distinguishes this Series from a generic epic-fantasy outline? Does its distinctive identity emerge from interacting mechanisms, or mainly from thematic language and setting details?
- **B. Causal renewal.** When major conflicts resolve, do their consequences generate meaningfully new pressures? Are later conflicts genuinely different in kind, or variations of earlier conflicts under new names? Can some engines end without making the Series unsustainable?
- **C. Multi-engine interaction.** Do the established engines actively transform one another? Are they causally connected or merely coexisting? Does their interaction produce possibilities that none could generate independently?
- **D. Long-range coordination.** Do distant promises have credible foundations? Which intermediate states are necessary to make the intended future possible? Are those states already established, plausibly open, or contradicted?
- **E. Book-level independence.** Can individual Books plausibly provide satisfying local experiences while contributing to the Series? Does the architecture leave enough room for meaningful local variation? Are any Book functions redundant or artificially prolonged?
- **F. Creative transformation.** Can the Series change institutions, character roles, social conditions, narrative modes, and emotional concerns while remaining recognisably itself? Does the architecture mistake preservation of identity for preservation of the initial status quo?
- **G. Authorial flexibility.** Which future commitments must be fixed now? Which questions should remain provisional? Would more upfront planning increase coherence, or create unnecessary creative constraints?

## Required judgments

- The strongest architectural achievement.
- The strongest consequential weakness, if one exists. You may instead give an explicit **NO REPAIR REQUIRED** verdict, if you can defend it.
- At least one credible counterargument to your own main judgment.
- The level that would own any warranted repair. Choose from: Local Book Structure; Multi-Book Structure; Whole-Series Architecture; Working Story Identity; Realization or Expression; Agent creative-reasoning method; Representation or review capability in Auteur.
- The smallest revision that might improve the architecture.
- What additional evidence could change your assessment.

## Report format

0. **Provenance.** Your model, if you can observe it. Every path you opened, in order. Any material you saw beyond the three packet files.

3. **Judgment.** Achievement; weakness or NO REPAIR REQUIRED with rationale; a counterargument to your main judgment; owning level for any repair; smallest revision; evidence that could change your view.

5. **Self-assessment.** Any passage in the packet that you read as retrospective commentary or self-assessment rather than design. Quote it briefly. If none, say none.

Keep the report under 2,200 words. Do not invent passages. Do not assume a passage exists unless you have read it in the packet.
