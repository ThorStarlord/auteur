## 2. Different kinds of pressure?

Yes. 03 labels A "Distributional and feasibility" and B "Coordination and legitimacy".

- **Conflict A changes labour, water and social relationships.** On labour: "Labour is concentrated. Lineage crews carry channel repair". On water: "a household's hands set part of its draw". On relationships: "lands on the terrace below".
- **Conflict B changes water routing and legitimacy, not labour.** It creates "a coordinating institution", and 03 §6 concedes the Table is "A schedule without labour. It cannot bring hands to the channels."
- **Responsibility shifts in both.** In A, decline "forfeits its water". In B, "Two practices sit at one instrument".

The distinction holds. B alone has no effect on how labour is distributed.

## 3. Book 23: three kinds of abundance

- **Genuine abundance.** The stated source is "two good thaws, the Table's schedule and the lineage repairs have cut leakage on the Lowfen. The high cistern holds a real surplus."
  - The good thaws are exogenous and asserted.
  - Less leakage on a lower channel does not explain a surplus in the *high* cistern. That is a causal gap.
  - Under 01 §2, a stored surplus is the surfeit-and-fever condition, which the text does not address.
- **Unevenly distributed abundance.** "Hand-light houses have drawn less since Book 20". This is derived from Conflict A (c). The claim that high-cistern draw belongs "mostly to the best-handed houses" is asserted.
- **Belief that abundance needs no work.** The cohort "were born into a full cistern and a tally that reads 'in credit'". This contradicts the timeline:
  - 01 §2: Hesper is "born about Book 13".
  - The tally begins in Book 15.
  - The "in credit" reading follows the Book 20 conversion.
  - Books 19–21 have a broken Lowfen and a dry spring.
  - The full cistern comes only after Book 22.

  The belief needs a different source, such as growing up in the cohort's teens rather than being born into it.

All three are kept distinct ("The construction keeps them separate"), but each has a weak link.

## 4. Does each finale mechanism change distribution or reliability?
