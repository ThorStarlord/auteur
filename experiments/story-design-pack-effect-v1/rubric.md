# Frozen blinded rubric

Each artifact receives 1–5 scores for every dimension. Evaluators must write a
brief rationale and mark any failure flags. The midpoint `3` means adequate
but uneven. `1` means absent or actively harmful. `5` means unusually clear,
specific, integrated, and useful for the stated case. Scores are not a claim
of literary quality.

## Story/artifact dimensions

- Intent alignment
- Specificity
- Causal integration
- Stakes
- Actionability
- Avoids unsupported narrowing

## Reasoning/guidance dimensions

- Explains why
- Surfaces tradeoffs
- States a craft principle
- Connects the principle to this story
- Preserves author agency
- Avoids parroting the supplied context

## Failure flags

`generic_amplification`, `pack_parroting`, `mechanically_copied_option`,
`unnecessary_narrowing`, `unsupported_canon`, `authority_tone`, `verbosity`.

Evaluator-facing packets must include this rubric but never reveal whether a
sample came from control or treatment.
