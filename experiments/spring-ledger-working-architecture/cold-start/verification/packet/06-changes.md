# Corrections and their defects

## F1. The governing variable (required)

- **Defect:** the proposal said the governing variable "gains a third term: who *can* return labour". That redefines a whole-Series structure from inside one middle Book.
- **Before (function §7, item 2):** "The governing variable (`th_hands_and_draw`) gains a third term: who *can* return labour, beside who gives it and who is counted for draw."
- **After (function §7, item 2):** "The Ledger engine gains a capability record, which bears on the governing variable (`th_hands_and_draw`) by showing who is able to give return labour. It does not change who is counted for draw."
- **Before (candidate, link `l_b24_mark_variable`):** "adds who can return labour beside who gives it and who is counted"
- **After (candidate, link `l_b24_mark_variable`):** "bears on who can return labour; does not change who is counted for draw"

## F2. Hesper's motive, an author-reserved choice (required)

- **Defect:** the proposal stated that Hesper's motive "does not carry the author's preference for the Turn", which answers an open choice.
- **Before (function §4):** "Her motive is her own, and it does not carry the author's preference for the Turn (open question 10; 01 §8.11)."
- **After (function §4):** "Her motive is designed to be judged against open question 10 (01 §8.11). Whether it is independent of the author's preference remains the author's decision."

## F3. The Reading Hall's refusal (recommended)

- **Defect:** the refusal rested on an institutional principle, and it tilted the Book against Alternative B's deficit record.
- **Before (function §4):** "it refuses to attribute aggregate balance to named hands, because that would make it a ledger-keeper."
- **After (function §4):** "it declines to attribute aggregate balance to named hands, because the gauge does not resolve local return (P3). Whether the Hall should keep any person-level record is not settled here, and B's deficit-record cost (01 §6) is unchanged."
- **Before (candidate, link `l_b24_reading_hall_declines`):** "declines to attribute gauge balance to named hands"
- **After (candidate, link `l_b24_reading_hall_declines`):** "declines to attribute gauge balance to named hands, which the gauge does not resolve"

## F4. The surge route (recommended)

- **Defect:** P10 said the surge route "is avoided", but no surge route is designated in the packet, so the sentence either made an unsupported claim or ruled out the Lowfen.
- **Before (function P10):** "The surge route is author-reserved (01 §8.6), so it is avoided."
- **After (function P10):** "The surge route is author-reserved (01 §8.6). This Book neither designates the Lowfen as the surge route nor excludes it."

## F5. The gauge citation (recommended)

- **Defect:** the proposal said Book 24 does not depend on the Gate, yet it cited a link to the Gate Table for the gauge.
- **Before (function §2):** "**The public gauge** (`eng_source`; `l_reading_separate_schedule`)."
- **After (function §2):** "**The public gauge** (`eng_source`; 01 §2.2). The gauge is the Source's own state, and it does not depend on the Gate."

## F6. Two wording claims (minor)

- **Gift without skill.** Before: "Gift without skill also fails, as the gift reading's physical caveat predicts (01 §2.3)." After: "Gift without skill also fails. This is a new physical assumption (P4), not a consequence of the caveat in 01 §2.3, which concerns gift without return and gift with unequal hands."
- **The granddaughter's act.** Before: "the granddaughter repeats her grandmother's Book 1 act, evidence cut in stone, against her grandmother's institution." After: "the granddaughter, as her grandmother did in Book 1, sets evidence against an institution's record; here the evidence is a craft-mark on the water, not a share-mark."

## K1. Knowledge omission in the architecture (restored, not a function change)

- **Defect:** the compact architecture did not say that Hesper's household is hand-light. The continuation agent had to ask, and the fact is in the original, §5, Book 23.
- **Before (architecture §4, Hesper row):** "Leads Brightwater; takes a Council apprenticeship"
- **After (architecture §4, Hesper row):** "Leads Brightwater; takes a Council apprenticeship. Her household is one of the hand-light houses the Turn burdens (original §5, Book 23)"
