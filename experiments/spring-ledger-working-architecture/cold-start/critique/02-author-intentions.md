## Series identity (author-supplied in the consolidation mission)

> The Spring Ledger is a long-form fantasy Series exploring a shared water commons, the institutions that govern it, the meanings attached to obligation, and the consequences of changing collective practices. Its identity should preserve wonder, discovery, everyday belonging, meaningful relationships, institutional transformation, material consequences, and the possibility of hopeful flourishing. Do not turn it into a generic escalating-villain epic.
