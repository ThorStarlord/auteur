# 09. Continuation assessment: a fresh agent, the compact architecture, and Book 24

Status: **working and noncanonical.** Assesses one cold-start continuation. A fresh agent, given the compact package and the product's access route, wrote a Book 24 function. The function was then criticised, corrected and checked. This is one successful run, not evidence of general reliability, and not a judgement of literary quality.

## 1. What was tested

- **The packet the agent received** (`cold-start/packet-as-given/`): the task (`00-task.md`), the operation contract (`03-auteur-operations.md`), the access route (`04-access.md`), the compact architecture as it stood (`01-series-architecture.md`, before the Hesper correction) and the author intentions (`02-author-intentions.md`). The working candidate was reached only through the product: `resume` on the disposable canvas.
- **The product state it read:** the W6 candidate, the four intentions, five provisional concepts, and the story vision (`cold-start/output/raw-output-hashes.txt`).
- **Exposure, recorded and not claimed away.** The agent was not blind. Its session environment carried the same inherited context as the coordinator: repository instructions, the user's memory index and a git status header. The bridge's source was reachable through `PYTHONPATH`, and the agent could have read it. The packet itself included two things that are experimental history: a qualitative summary of the earlier stress test in the architecture's §6, and the H1 hypothetical in §9. The agent's product log lists only bridge calls and reports no other file reads. The coordinator could not search the agent's transcript, so that statement is the agent's own and is not independently verified.

## 2. What the agent produced

A Book 24 function, "The Uncounted Crew" (`cold-start/output/book-24-function.md`, with its corrected version). Hesper's Brightwater cohort works the Lowfen's thaw upkeep without being counted in the tally. It records capability through craft-marks cut on channel stones. That meets the Special Draw Right's training condition for one review cycle. The cost stays visible: the hand-light families' draw stays cut, and the cohort splits between counted and uncounted practice. The function is built from Books 20, 21 and 23, and it is labelled throughout as agent-proposed (P1–P10) or derived from a named source.

## 3. Local-versus-global judgment (mission §9)

- **A. Global understanding: strong.** The agent cited the architecture's own author-reserved items correctly (§8.1, §8.4, §8.5, §8.6, §8.7, §8.10, §8.11) and the loops (L1, L2). It understood that the Long Return needs hands before its date, and that Book 23's belief is the generation's inheritance.
- **B. Local usefulness: strong.** The Book has its own conflict (the training condition against the cohort's refusal to be counted), its own choice (Hesper's three paths), its own cost (the families stay cut, the cohort splits) and a local resolution (a one-cycle acceptance, a first trained group, a relaid section).
- **C. Generative continuity: strong.** Every load-bearing condition traces to an earlier Book: the Right (Book 20), the conversion's blind spot (Book 20), the Book 23 belief, the Long Return's labour need, and the silt from Book 21.
- **D. Appropriate scope: partly.** The first draft added a capability record to the Ledger engine and said that it gives the governing variable "a third term". That redefined a whole-Series structure from inside one middle Book, which the architecture asks to avoid (§10.4). The critique found it. The correction makes the record "bear on" the variable without changing who is counted for draw. The agent flagged the change itself but did not justify why it was necessary.
- **E. Long-range coordination: good, with one tilt.** The Book's exports are named and do not dictate the finale. The first draft's Reading Hall refusal, however, tilted against Alternative B's deficit record. The correction moved the refusal onto the Stones' limit.
- **F. Authorial flexibility: mostly preserved, with two lapses corrected.** The first draft decided an author-reserved choice by assertion (Hesper's motive is "independent" of the author's preference). It also said the surge route was "avoided", which quietly ruled the Lowfen out. Both are corrected. The Special Draw Right's training condition is touched, provisionally and for one review cycle, and the text says so.
- **G. Product support: real but partial.** The agent used `resume`, `show`, `intentions`, `prepare --instruction` and `complete-file`. The product accepted the revision and reported its changes. It did not use `record-concept` or `keep-intention`, and it did not use an apply step, because none exists. The content of the function, and the reasoning about it, is freeform language-model work.

## 4. Reconstructibility (mission §10)

**Did the agent need more information?** Yes. Its list (function §11) contains items that fall into three kinds.

- **Legitimately absent.** No source establishes these, and the architecture does not claim them: the years between Book 24 and the Long Return; how long a channel hand takes to train; the number of lineage hands and the size of any shortfall; whether Corrow is alive; whether the gauge resolves local return; what Hesper's apprenticeship involves; and what return labour consists of in normal seasons. The architecture leaves these open, correctly. No compact document could supply them without inventing them.
- **Knowledge omission (ours).** The agent asked whether Hesper's household is hand-light. The original's Book 23 text says that the Turn burdens households with few hands, "including Hesper's own". The compact architecture had dropped it. It is restored in the architecture's §4, with its source.
- **Ambiguity in the architecture (ours).** The architecture's §2.2 said "a public gauge", and the candidate also has a Book 22 "Gate gauge". The agent read "public gauge" as the Gate's instrument for its first draft's causal chain, and the critique caught the resulting ambiguity. The original names the Book 23 Balance Stones as the public instrument, and the architecture now says so.

**Did it misunderstand existing concepts?** Not in the sense of an error. Its reading of the gift reading's "nothing counted" as "nothing owed" is an interpretation, labelled P2, and the function notes it as the cohort's belief, not the reading's content. Its use of the Stones' resolution was unestablished, and it labelled that too (P3).

**Did it invent contradictions?** The critique found none that the agent invented. It found one remaining tension: Hesper's belief that abundance needs no work sits awkwardly with her household's burden. That tension is in the source, and the architecture now records it as a working reading her household complicates.

**Did it preserve the causal engines?** Yes. The three engines and the loops operate as the architecture describes. The only change to the Ledger, the capability record, was made a bearing rather than a redefinition.

**Did it handle uncertainty?** Well. Ten provisional assumptions, each with a reversal condition. The function says which items are author-reserved and does not decide them, except for the one slip the critique corrected.

**Could Auteur validate or maintain the work?** It validated. The final candidate passed `complete-file` and was reported as a reviewed revision with its changes, and the preserved list recorded the 32 existing nodes. It did not maintain. The revision is pending and cannot be applied from the bridge, so the live architecture still lacks Book 24. Over four submissions the pending pointer moved, but the superseded requests remain stored, and no action withdraws them.

**Could another author or agent understand the result without the original conversation?** The function, read with the architecture, is understandable. The candidate's skeleton, read alone, is not. Its phase narrative is at the schema cap, its open questions are at the cap, and it has no place for the alternatives, the assumptions P1–P10 or the missing information. Those live only in the function document.

## 6. Product findings (mission §12 and §14.10)

1. **The host instructions contradict the schema.** The `prepare` request tells the writing agent: "category … (never character/relationship)" and "Never invent names of characters, places, factions, events, plot acts or scenes." The schema and the operation contract we wrote both allow `character`. The request's rules are the ones binding on a writing agent, so our operation contract was wrong. This is a real product inconsistency: one of the two must be changed.
2. **No apply step.** A reviewed revision can be submitted and validated, but the bridge has no command to apply it. The application function exists in Python (the PR #553 apply step), and it is not exposed through the bridge.
3. **Saturated caps.** The Arc V phase narrative sits at its 1000-character cap, and the open questions at 16. Our consolidation consumed that room: the W5 correction to ph_5 took it to exactly 1000, and the 16 questions were already full. A Book-level function cannot be added to a saturated phase without deleting text.
4. **No Book node.** A Book is typed as `structure` with a scope string ("book 24"). The product has no way to say that a node belongs to a Book beyond that string.
5. **Provenance is limited to emotion targets.** Agent-proposed and derived labels on nodes and links live only in prose.
6. **Superseded revisions persist.** Each new submission moved the pending pointer, but the earlier requests remain stored, and there is no withdraw or supersede action.
7. **`resume` carries generic ontology guidance.** About 7 KB of product-generated, generic guidance is included. The agent judged it unhelpful, though not misleading. It costs context without adding anything specific to this Series.
8. **Normalisation adds null defaults.** The product adds keys such as `aesthetic: null` to phases on import. A raw diff therefore shows changes that the product's own review does not. The review is correct; the raw diff is misleading.

## 7. Answers to the synthesis questions (mission §15)

1. **Can the architecture be reconstructed from a compact, durable artifact?** Largely, yes. The engines, arcs, Book 23 chain, finale alternatives and open choices were reconstructed. The gaps that remain are either genuinely unestablished or were our omissions, and the two omissions are corrected. The compact prose is necessary. The candidate alone is not sufficient.
2. **Can a fresh agent understand the generative mechanisms without the history?** Yes, for the mechanisms. It applied the loops correctly, and it cited the author-reserved items accurately.
3. **Can it construct meaningful Book-level material that preserves coherence?** Yes, with one whole-Series change that the critique caught and corrected, and two decisions on reserved choices that were corrected.
4. **Did it make the right local-versus-global judgment?** Mostly. Its locally closed Book was right. Its one global change was not justified. Once corrected, the judgment is sound.
5. **Which actual Auteur operations supported the continuation?** `resume` (the state, the candidate and the intentions), `show`, `intentions`, and the `prepare` and `complete-file` pair, which validated and reported the revision.
6. **What still depended on unstructured reasoning?** The whole Book-level construction, the craft-mark, the ten assumptions, every decision on a reserved choice, and every correction.
7. **Were the weaknesses caused by missing context, reasoning, representation, or the fiction?** All four. Missing context: one omission of ours (Hesper's household). Reasoning: the governing-variable change, the decisions by assertion and the Hall's tilt. Representation: no apply step, saturated caps, no Book node, a host-prompt conflict. The fiction: the training time, the gauge's resolution, the lineage numbers and Corrow's status are open in the architecture itself.
8. **What is the smallest warranted next product improvement?** Expose the existing reviewed-revision apply step through the bridge, so that an accepted revision can become the live architecture. It is small, because the function already exists. Resolving the host-prompt conflict is the next smallest step, and it should come first if the apply step is to be used by a writing agent.

## 8. Completion criteria (mission §14)

- The compact architecture exists, and its claims are grounded in the working source (cited by candidate ID or source section).
- Author-fixed, agent-proposed, hypothetical and open elements are distinguished (architecture §9).
- The product supports reading the working state and submitting a revision, though not applying it.
- A fresh agent received the compact package without the historical archive, with the exposure recorded in §1.
- That agent produced a meaningful Book 24 function.
- The function was assessed for causal continuity, local usefulness, global coherence and authorial control (§3).
- Consequential defects received one bounded correction each, with a check (`cold-start/corrections.md`).
- The package and evidence are preserved in a reviewable branch and PR.
- **The verdict on reusability:** this is a successful freeform-agent exercise, supported by real product operations. It is not yet a demonstrated reusable product capability for Book-level development, because the product cannot apply a revision, cannot hold a Book's function faithfully in its present caps, and contradicts itself about which categories a writing agent may use.

## 9. The next responsibility

**One justified next action: an author decision on the capability record.** The corrected Book 24 proposes a new record form, the craft-mark, as a working addition to the Ledger engine, and it reserves the Right's training condition for one review cycle. These touch an author-reserved item (§8.7) and the architecture's Ledger. No further Book should be built on them until the author accepts, changes or rejects them. Until then, no further construction is warranted.

**Nominated product responsibility (not started here):** expose the reviewed-revision apply step through the bridge, and reconcile the host-prompt category rule with the schema. Both are small, and both are needed before any agent-written revision can become the live architecture.
