## What the qualified journey proves

The deterministic Archive of Lies fixture and
`tests/test_series_vertical_slice_e2e.py` exercise the complete beginner
journey:

1. A sparse ongoing `SeriesDirection` is proposed and explicitly accepted.
   It contains no future `BookPlan` entries. Proposal generation alone creates
   no authority.
2. A local Book 1 direction is proposed with an explicit accepted-Series
   commitment reference and then explicitly accepted. Accepting it does not
   rewrite Series Direction.
3. A bounded Book 1 realization is proposed. Canonical State remains unchanged
   until the author accepts the realization bundle. After acceptance, the
   accepted transition is projected into Canonical State with its source
   reference.
4. Book 2 planning is entered explicitly after a fresh service/store reload.
   The derived context surfaces the relevant prior commitment and realized
   state change, explains why each matters now, and excludes an unrelated
   recent datum. Deleting and rebuilding the derived context preserves the
   same semantic result and does not alter authority or state history.
5. A bounded next-decision proposal presents a recommendation, alternatives,
   rationale, and tradeoffs. `choose_recommended`, `choose_other` for an
   option in that exact proposal, and `defer` are persisted as workflow history
   only. None creates Book 2 Direction or Book 2 Canonical State.
6. The CLI Map and Focus surfaces expose the established context, why it
   matters now, and the next useful decision. Default output remains
   beginner-readable; source/revision detail is available only through the
   deeper inspection path.

These behaviors are implemented behind the narrow vertical-slice boundary;
the existing full-Series commands continue to use the legacy Series model and
were included in the complete regression suite.

## Deferred machinery

The following remain intentionally outside this qualification:

- free-form author-authored Book 2 Direction;
- generalized episode support or Book/Episode unification (a narrow,
  bounded exception has since been proposed and evaluated separately: a
  first-class Episode 1 Direction workflow for an explicitly episodic Series,
  scoped to Episode 1 only, with no Episode realization or canonical state and
  no change to `SeriesDirection`; see
  [Bounded Episode 1 Direction Qualification](series-episode-one-direction-qualification-v1.md),
  which as of this writing states that qualification evidence for that
  bounded capability is complete, while explicitly not asserting anything
  about this repository's separate, downstream Feature Factory Validator
  rerun or CHECKPOINT 3 gate — it does not reopen or supersede this
  document's original deferral of the general case);
- universal Direction inheritance and generalized revision propagation;
- universal dependency inference or a generalized Author Decision system;
- replacement of the existing StoryBible/full-Series workflow;
- a final browser, TUI, or editor presentation for Map and Focus;
- a complete multi-book roadmap or exhaustive upfront Series planning.

## Qualification history and issue classification

An earlier evidence invocation used only `src` on `PYTHONPATH` and produced
four test-module collection errors. That was an execution-environment/import
path issue, not product evidence; rerunning with both the checkout root and
`src` resolved it and produced the complete-suite result above.

An earlier candidate also exposed a real compatibility regression: recording
acceptance timestamps unconditionally changed hashes for legacy accepted
artifacts. The fix makes timestamp recording opt-in for the new vertical-slice
acceptance paths while preserving deterministic legacy artifact acceptance.
The final candidate was rerun after that source change; the legacy dogfood
test and the complete suite pass. No current candidate failures or collection
errors remain.

`CONTEXT.md` was not changed as part of this qualification. The bounded slice
does not justify revising Domain Model V1 or claiming that Series architecture
is complete.
