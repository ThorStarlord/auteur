# Erotic Fiction Base Pack Guide

## Overview

The **Erotic Fiction Base Pack** (`erotic_fiction` v0.1.0) provides versioned genre knowledge for stories in which erotic desire, intimacy, or erotic transformation is a central organizing force rather than incidental content.

## Core Audience Promises

1. `erotic_desire_is_structurally_central`: Erotic desire materially drives character choices and structural progression.
2. `intimacy_or_erotic_experience_changes_state`: Intimate scenes perform narrative work, transforming character, relationship, identity, power, knowledge, or stakes.
3. `resolution_addresses_erotic_arc`: The final act confronts and resolves the established erotic arc.

## Subgenre Profiles

1. **Erotic Romance (`erotic_romance`)**:
   * Focuses on desire, affection, trust, and relational vulnerability.
   * Culminates in a satisfying relationship resolution.
2. **Erotic Psychological Drama (`erotic_psychological_drama`)**:
   * Focuses on desire, ambivalence, identity conflict, and psychological transformation.
   * Explores the breakdown of identity facades.
3. **Erotic Horror (`erotic_horror`)**:
   * Blends desire with dread, bodily destabilization, transgression, and terrifying fascination.
   * Explores dangerous surrender or fatal revelation.

## Key Evaluation Rules

* `desire_affects_decisions`: Erotic desire materially shapes key character choices.
* `intimate_scenes_change_state`: Every intimate scene modifies character, power, or relational state.
* `scene_function_diversity`: Consecutive intimate scenes utilize distinct scene functions.
* `resolution_addresses_erotic_arc`: Act 3 climax directly resolves the accepted erotic commitment.
