**Where the trajectory reading is better**

1. Book 25 repeats Book 24's elements verbatim (E14) with no development of its own. That is the repetition the author fears, already present in the drafts, and the inquiry arm could not have found it.
2. Per-stage "becomes_available" and "changes" fields, plus the standalone test for Book 24, are the right discipline for a multi-Book arc.
3. Alternative 1 argues from order: training first makes the record "a test of the Right's condition rather than a register of existing repairers, so it does not repeat the lineage rota in a new form."
4. The craft-standard opportunity ("can meet the lineage's craft standard instead of ending it") turns the lineage's fear into something the story can dramatise. Exercise D dropped it.

**Where it is worse or merely more elaborate**

1. The load-bearing dependency (s3 to s5, now s2 to s4) conflicts with E8's present tense: the cohort is "now practising both". E4 already records the split within one generation. D keeps the dependency and defends it with "no other route is recorded", an argument from absence.
2. Stage 1 keeps the basis "recorded" though its content is agent-proposed. D adds the attribution in the text but leaves the label.
3. The most consequential stage is built on a record it never saw. Its cohort "standing in how the valley coordinates channel work" sits beside roles the candidate already assigns to the Terrace Council and the Gate Table.
4. It is elaborate for what it yields: four relation types, three paths and three opportunities for three proposed stages. Opportunity 3 ("a change of register") restates stage 3.
5. The inquiry has real faults too. Its 21-30 frame re-plans recorded Books 21-22. Its Book 23-26 and 24-26 proposals may collide with the drafted Book 24 record it never saw, and its reader-experience reasoning is thin.

**Exercise D**

- It changed the judgements the criticism targeted. c1 (Book 25 as a stage) is correctly accepted and the stage dropped. c2 is correctly partly accepted: "who can repair rather than who" does not show that the count goes away.
- It made changes it does not disclose. changes_from_prior lists four items, none about alternatives, opportunities or viability. In fact all three alternatives were replaced. "Standing without training" moved from "weakened" to "closed", on "Closed on the current record", which contradicts D's own open decision about who grants standing. Two of three opportunities were dropped, along with the reader-experience arc. The alternative lost is the one that named the question's concern: keeping the lineage rota is "the repetition the question asks to avoid". The new stop-at-Book-24 path is a real improvement.
- It missed the load-bearing error. The criticism tested only c1 and c2, so the dependency passed unchallenged. A narrow criticism passing says little about the rest of the output.
- Net: the targeted fixes are right and the "closed" upgrade is wrong. The rest is partly cautious and partly undisclosed hardening, which breaks the contract to state what changed.

**Fair test for the next run:** give the inquiry arm the same Book 24-25 excerpts. Only then can the trajectory form's own contribution be separated from evidence access.
