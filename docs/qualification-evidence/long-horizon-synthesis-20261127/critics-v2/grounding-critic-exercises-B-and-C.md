**Three most serious problems**
1. Agent-proposed Working Book text is cited as record: C's S1 is labelled "recorded" on E10 and E12, and B's A3 says "the record says" about E4, which marks itself an agent inference.
2. Conditionals become events: B's S2 drops "if it is credited," B's S3 turns E11's hypothetical into "now practising," and C's standalone value has the steward accept a gap where E5 says only that accepting "means" one.
3. Relations and opportunities outrun their premises: C's prerequisite rests on a link its own reason calls "inferred, not stated," B's O1 assigns hired labour to "the same channel repair" that E24 does not give, and B's O2 and C's R6 pair accurate quotes with a different claim.
