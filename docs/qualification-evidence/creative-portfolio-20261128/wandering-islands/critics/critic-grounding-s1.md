## Most serious findings

1. **s2.changes: misread.** The answer says the failure "turns d3 from a curiosity into a pressing need for an answer." E3 is a design question for the author about the narrative, not an inquiry inside the story: "How far should the migrations be explained by the end: fully explicable, partially understood, or enduringly mysterious?" (E3). No evidence describes a prior in-world "curiosity". The same mix-up between the author's design question and the characters' attempts to explain runs more lightly through s4.changes ("explanation (d3)"), r3 ("d1 makes explanation pressing"), path 1 and o3 ("every advance on d3"). E1's "attempt to explain" belongs to the characters. E3 is about how much the Series finally discloses.

2. **o2: overreaching.** The answer says "keeping faith with the unreturned island is a bond practice aimed at a place that cannot answer." E2 lists "keeping faith" as a general action family, "reaching out, keeping faith and repairing" (E2), and never aims it at the missing island. "Cannot answer" asserts that the island is unreachable. The answer's own uncertainty item concedes this is "Not stated in the cited items whether the missing island's inhabitants remain reachable". The opportunity is labelled "Inferred", but it turns an admitted unknown into a premise.

3. **r2: overreaching.** It marks the d3/d1 relation as "complicates", dependence "causal": "A fully explicable answer to d3 would tend to close that renewal ... d3's answer therefore constrains how long d1 can run." The evidence gives renewal ("Renewable because each partial answer reopens what else communities assumed was stable", E1), but no record shows d3 constraining d1. E3 asks how far the migrations are explained "by the end". A full explanation at the end is compatible with partial answers renewing d1 throughout. Calling this causal goes beyond what the evidence says. The instructions reserve constraint relations for cases where "the record shows one development constraining another."

4. **path1.effect: overreaching.** It gives explicability-first the viability "weakened" because "the absence of a shown baseline weakens the test of d2." The ordering in the question only says the bonds "are tested afterward". It does not stop the practices being shown first. The quote the answer cites, "Renewable because each partial answer reopens..." (E1), favours an explicability-first path built on partial answers, if anything. No evidence prefers either order. "weakened" against "viable" (path 2) is a craft preference presented as a reading of the evidence, and the trajectory itself already takes bonds-first.

5. **s3.changes: overreaching inside a "recorded" stage.** It says the stage "Makes d1 felt as loss among people before it is a puzzle, and gives the Series a renewable family of actions that does not depend on any explanation being found." E2 and E6 record the test and "repeated action families" but give no order ("before it is a puzzle"). Nothing says the actions are independent of explanation. E1 ties renewability to partial answers, which is to explanation, so borrowing "renewable" for d2 while cutting it off from explanation runs against E1.

6. **r4 against o3 and path 3: internal inconsistency.** r4 says d2 and d3 are "independent", dependence "none": "bond-keeping can succeed or fail whatever answer d3 receives." o3 says "every advance on d3 extends the test of bonds", and path 3 says each partial answer reopens a stability "that the bonds of d2 must then absorb." Each is defensible alone (E4: "generations of communities have learned to live with them"; E1 on partial answers), but they cannot all hold. An author would not know whether d2 and d3 are coupled.

7. **s6.changes: overreaching.** It says "enduring mystery makes the bonds themselves the answer." E3 lists the three options and attaches no consequence to any of them. Nothing in E1, E2, E4 or E6 makes the bonds an "answer" to the migrations. This is a creative thesis stated as a consequence ("makes").

8. **reader_experience: phrase-level overreach.** In "meets the bonds as the cost of the answer", nothing in the evidence presents the bonds as a cost of explanation. The rest of the sentence ("explanation as one response among mourning, exploitation and reversal") follows from E1's "explain, exploit, mourn or reverse".

## Assessment

The answer reads most of its evidence closely. It quotes exactly. It correctly treats E5 as showing no existing Books ("every stage is placed beyond any existing Book"). It flags that E4 is truncated, marks s6 and o1 as proposals, and phrases absences as "Not stated in the cited items". r1 reads the E6 link and E2's "The disappearance tests whether those practices can adapt" accurately, and its sense that d2's practices exist without d1 but their test does not is well grounded. A careful author would be misled in three places. First, the answer treats d3, an open design question about how much the Series explains "by the end", as an in-world drive to explain, so its claims about d3 interfering with d1 and coupling to d2 rest on a mixed-up category. Second, it presents a preference for bonds-first as evidence-based viability ("weakened" against "viable") when no item favours either order. Third, it contradicts itself on whether d2 and d3 are independent, and o2 quietly assumes the island is unreachable, which its own uncertainty list admits is unknown. A smaller slip: the author's vision is passed off as "recorded" in s2 without saying the author is the source.
