# Auteur — Stage 4J Authorial Direction

## The bounded correction

**Original sentence (frozen Stage 4H text):**

> Nemi thought of the crowd that had greeted their earlier Book 5 news of Larkhome.

**Corrected sentence (Stage 4J derivative):**

> Nemi thought of the crowd that had greeted them when they brought word that Larkhome was alive.

The phrase “Book 5” belongs to the series' structural documentation, not to Nemi's interior life. The Stage 4I fresh reader identified it spontaneously as an immersion break before seeing the Stage 4H assessment. The replacement preserves Nemi's earlier journey, their role in carrying verified news of Larkhome's survival, their complicated relationship with public gratitude, the psychological motivation for accepting limited letter custody, and the surrounding chronology. It removes only the external book numbering.

## What remains open

The excerpt is coherent, humane, and rich in everyday detail, but its emotional experience may be quieter than the intended Book 11 experience. The fresh reader found it “a quietly good excerpt rather than a spectacular one,” admired more than felt. That is not a defect to fix with another sentence. It is a genuine authorial choice about the kind of emotional intensity Book 11 should primarily pursue.

The directions below are alternatives, not a recommendation to adopt all of them. They are not necessarily mutually exclusive, but a substantial revision needs a primary emphasis.

## Direction A — Restorative everyday fantasy

Prioritize belonging, craftsmanship, community life, mutual discovery, and emotionally satisfying ordinary achievements.

**Merits:** This is the excerpt's strongest demonstrated mode. The opening's hanging gardens, Ivo and Marra's collaborative repair, Pera's unsentimental homecoming, and the wooden bird all work through tactile, domestic means. The Story Vision explicitly values wonder, warmth, friendship, belonging, and earned hope; this direction protects those qualities. It also gives the series a humane register that can distinguish it from adventure-fantasy defaults.

**Risks:** Restrained conflict may leave readers admiring the narrative more than emotionally investing in its outcomes. The fresh reader's “admiring more than aching” is direct evidence of this risk. A whole Book 11 built only on ordinary repair may feel small against the series' growing scale, especially late in a twelve-Book arc.

**Implication for later revision:** Keep the quiet stakes but make the local goal matter more. Deepen the consequences of the one-collar choice, the window bed, and the letter without adding spectacle. The revision would need to earn emotional weight inside the everyday mode rather than import it from a larger plot.

## Direction B — Hopeful adventure with stronger dramatic pressure

Retain warmth and generosity while increasing suspense, wonder, difficult choices, and the emotional consequences of the crossing and encounter.

**Merits:** This direction directly addresses the fresh reader's sense that the crossing felt procedural and the excerpt's emotional intensity was limited. It serves the Story Vision's wonder, mystery, exploration, consequential heroism, and growing scale. A more pressure-filled Book 11 could give the series late-arc momentum without abandoning its optimistic identity.

**Risks:** Artificial danger or manufactured catastrophe could undermine the established world and tonal vision. The migrations are already a source of real uncertainty; inventing a villain or a spectacular disaster would risk making the story generic. Stronger pressure must arise from the existing causal material—the moving island, the one-time passage, the letter's uncertain destination, the unequal access to observations—not from added spectacle.

**Implication for later revision:** Strengthen the crossing's physical and emotional stakes through the pilots' decisions and the cost of being wrong. Let the letter create a consequence that cannot be resolved in one conversation. The revision would need to preserve the excerpt's restraint while allowing at least one sequence to feel genuinely dangerous.

## Direction C — Deeper interpersonal and social consequences

Develop Nemi's internal contradictions, Ivo's grievances, Pell's competing economic interests, and the costs of relationships that cannot be immediately reconciled.

**Merits:** This direction addresses the fresh reader's sense that Nemi was thinner than Ivo and Pera, and that the chart dispute became discursive. It serves the Story Vision's complicated choices, intimate human concerns, and earned hope. It could increase emotional transformation without requiring spectacular external danger, and it would make the near-side encounter matter beyond the single trade.

**Risks:** Excessive argument or unresolved friction could make the story heavier or more didactic. The excerpt's warmth depends on characters who disagree without becoming cruel; a revision must preserve that generosity. If every relationship becomes a debate, the domestic texture that made the opening strong could be crowded out.

**Implication for later revision:** Let the existing conflicts leave residue. Nemi's jealousy, Ivo's grievance about the gardens, Pell's unequal-access argument, and Tava's caution should change what the next scene can do. The revision would need to show consequences across scenes rather than resolving each position inside one workshop conversation.

## A possible combination

The most natural hybrid suggested by the evidence is **A with C, and B used sparingly**: preserve the restorative everyday texture and deepen the interpersonal/social consequences, while allowing the crossing and the letter to carry genuine dramatic pressure. This would keep the excerpt's strongest qualities and address its two most credible weaknesses—thin late-series momentum and Nemi's comparative underdevelopment—without turning the book into a different genre.

This is an option, not a decision. A+B+C with B subordinate is also coherent; so is a deliberate A-only late-series breather. The author's intent, not the fresh reader's preference, should determine which emphasis governs.

## Book 11 within the twelve-Book series

By Book 11, the series likely needs a distinct late-arc function rather than another interchangeable excursion. A quiet restorative book can work as a deliberate breather, but it still needs to advance a series-scale thread and justify its place this late. The Larkhome letter is the clearest existing hook for that function; the excerpt leaves it undelivered, with a blank space beneath the custody entry. The authorial choice is not merely “more plot” versus “more warmth.” It is what Book 11 is for: recovery, consequence, revelation, relationship, or some combination.

The excerpt's local success does not prove it can carry a full Book 11's series-scale expectations. The Stage 4H assessment's “strong local extended generativity, not full-Book sufficiency” remains the correct evidence ceiling.

## The precise decision remaining open

**Which emotional intensity should be Book 11's primary mode: restorative everyday fantasy, hopeful adventure with stronger dramatic pressure, deeper interpersonal and social consequences, or an explicit hybrid with a named primary emphasis?**

Subordinate choices follow from that decision: whether the excerpt is intended to stand alone or as a series chapter; how much of Book 11 should be organized around the undelivered Larkhome letter; and whether Nemi's comparative thinness is a deliberate late-series choice or a revision priority.

This brief does not choose among the directions. It exposes the choice for the author.
