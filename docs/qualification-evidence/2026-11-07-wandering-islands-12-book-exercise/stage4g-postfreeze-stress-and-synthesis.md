## A. What the completed story establishes and what it does not

**Fictional occurrence [A].** Stage 4F narrates *Mothwake* leaving the outer inhabited ferry, reaching the observed Lacebank refuge, finding an occupied near-side repair dock, requesting trade, receiving an independently willing near-side pilot, sounding approaches that **actually overlap for a small cutter**, making a one-time Rillturn berth, completing a craft bargain, taking limited custody of the original sealed letter and returning to its outer ferry under refreshed checks. That entire series of events happened **within the preserved fictional version**. No historical source is altered by judging its warrant.

**Internal possibility [B].** The fixed world permits local sailing, cargo exchange, lived care and historically contingent contacts, while it forbids treating unpredictable migrations or Book 2's missing third bearing as a deterministic global navigation technique. An independent later near/outer contact in a bounded current window is not logically incompatible with those rules. Jessa's earlier unsounded stop concerns an **earlier observation state and unverified vessel**, not a statement that all future passages are forever closed.

**Causal warrant [C].** Stage 4F does not rely only on “Nemi arrived because the plot needed it”: Tava checks water, the near-side pilot separately checks and consents, the narration explicitly joins the two observed reaches, Lacebank and Rillturn are available refuges, and return is freshly confirmed. These are **story-level supporting mechanisms**. Their weakness is that the source gives no specific sounding extent, intervening current description, time/freshness boundary, cutter turning margin, signaling protocol or quantitatively validated reserve. The decisive overlap itself is a **NEW_P4F_ASSUMPTION**, asserted by the construction rather than established by prior series facts.

**Generalized capability [D].** **Not demonstrated.** One locally witnessed visit does not generate a route schedule, forecast island migration, map the whole outer gyre, prove a repeatable crossing, guarantee a future rescue or bring Larkhome within reach.

**The proper threshold:** At the level of a working fantasy architecture and one short craft scene, the conjunction of **currently checked continuous small-craft water + willing pilots + bounded stores + shelter + refreshed return checks** is a plausible condition for one encounter. Detailed engineering data are not required simply to justify a character entering a harbor in a fantasy story. But for use as an **essential long-horizon Series dependency**, the coincidence and the physical crossing should become legible *through actual narrative action*, rather than a bare sentence that the needed pieces aligned.

## C. Outbound and return warrant: strongest argument for and against

**Strongest argument for the one-time encounter:** This is not a hidden universal map. A motivated outer crew travels to a separately observed refuge, detects an actual near-side trade opportunity, sounds toward it without forfeiting retreat, receives an independently checked and **voluntary** near-side pilot, and in this specific episode the checked reaches are stated to meet **for the cutter**. The crew can shelter at Rillturn if the return changes; the pilots refresh the return check; the cutter actually completes the visit. The small-craft scale, bounded stocks, near-side welcome, historical fact of some possible intercommunity travel and independent barter make one successful, nonrepeating episode credible within the established genre.

**Strongest argument against its causal warrant:** The exact reason this successful crossing is possible—the compatibility of current observations **across the unknown cut**—arrives at the decisive point as an **authoring assertion**. We see no differentiated bottom/current obstacles, shared survey boundary, timing constraint or dangerous but controlled choice during the actual crossing. *Mothwake*'s draft/turning room and the source of Rillturn's prolonged refuge capacity are similarly not shown. The destination is, conveniently, **the one dock where the prior letter waits**, and the encounter falls inside Eda's finite custody period. The narrative lists all appropriate kinds of precaution but does not yet fully *dramatize* the material mechanism that converts sighting and partial soundings into reliable access. A reader can reasonably feel the plot made the sea cooperate precisely once.

**Adjudication:** The positive Stage 4F outcome is **conditionally credible as one newly constructed fictional visit**. The gap is **dramatic warrant and contingency explanation**, not a demonstrated contradiction or missing genre-wide travel technology. An indispensable Series climax would require a more textured enactment of the observation window, host refusal risk and residual cost; the next research step should not be another pseudo-engineering worksheet merely to certify it.

## D. Earned, convenient or contradictory contingency?

**Operationally earned:** Tava's choice of a small rather than inhabited vessel, protected return stores, local outer refuge, independently willing near-side pilot, incremental observations and refreshed return checks make the team ready to exploit an opportunity **if it appears**. Ivo's ownership limits and Rillturn's finite collar stock also make the encounter an actual transaction rather than an unconditional welcome.

**Artistically convenient:** The particular opening is available near **Rillturn**, in the same short Eda-custody period, with willing people and an independently checked overlap. Those are new Stage 4F events, not natural consequences compelled by the original Book 11 premise. The decisive water conditions are asserted more than evoked. The crew did not deliberately aim for the letter, so the correspondence handoff is genuinely incidental to their motive; that improves causal independence, but does not remove the conspicuous coincidence.

**Not contradictory:** No fixed author rule says islands cannot temporarily approach, no missing Book 2 measurement is restored, no Book 5 survival fact is reversed, no compelled rescue is invented, and the unknown Stage 4E sail is not retconned into Nemi. A new actual current observation can legitimately differ from an earlier failed observation without changing the earlier outcome.

**Best description:** **Earned response to a convenient but permissible contingency.** If forced to classify the *precision of the exact meeting* as earned or convenient, choose **convenient contingency**; if classifying the *crew's decision to use one observed opening*, choose **earned contingency**. The weakness concerns coincidence and incomplete material showing, not impossibility.

## F. Evidence disposition and rival classifications

**Selected: CONTACT_WINDOW_WARRANT_SUPPORTED_CONDITIONALLY.** At an appropriate fiction-outline level, the original source names all major *types* of conditions and explicitly records their being met **for this small cutter and one contemporary visit**: checked outer refuge, independently current near-side observations, successful overlap, voluntary reception, resources, refuge and fresh return. It is reasonable to accept the one-time passage *conditionally* within the provisional fictional version without demanding fabricated bathymetric precision.

**Credible narrower rival: CONTACT_WINDOW_WARRANT_PARTIAL.** If the claim target is not “Could this happen under narrated conditions?” but “Do the textual scenes sufficiently **earn** the crossing before the author says the soundings overlapped?”, then much of the crux is only asserted and no spatial/temporal margins are demonstrated. This is a serious artistic limitation and **must not be glossed over**. It is recorded as a **dramatic-warrant shortfall**, even though the selected category treats the missing granularity as nonfatal at the current research abstraction.

**Why not CONTACT_WINDOW_UNDERDETERMINED?** The core propositions needed for a single bounded passage are *not wholly absent*: the source affirmatively states independently checked, overlapping current water and rechecked successful return. More detail is missing about their **persuasiveness**, not about whether the fictional text specifies that they occurred. A stricter characterization of causal demonstration could reasonably disagree; this assessment does not claim certainty or an independent external confirmation.

**Why not CONTACT_WINDOW_CONTRADICTION?** No fixed rule or earlier observed event states that a newly checked small-cutter crossing at this later time is impossible. The unexplained observation margins do not logically negate the narrated result, and the earlier stopped Jessa voyage is not rewritten.

**What remains unchanged:** Stage 4F's **OUTER_RECIPROCAL_CONTACT_DEMONSTRATED_LOCALLY** still describes the single fictional meeting, not a validated repeatable access capability. Stage 4E's **CONTACT_PARTIAL_OR_DEFERRED** still describes the earlier failed onward approach and finite custody. Neither is revised.

## H. Research maturity: is another micro-probe worth its cost?

**Repeated uncertainty:** Later work repeatedly exposes a similar gap at the next scale. A plausible premise becomes a well-organized chain; a concrete scene tests it; the story still needs lived pressure, time, material specificity and independent character responses to make the chain feel earned. Stages 4D and 4F show this is often **solved by construction of a meaningful scene**, not by more abstract tables. Stage 4G can always demand one more current sounding, one more contingency, one more reserve entry. Without a defined decision that such an additional detail would change, micro-analysis becomes a treadmill.

**What the current result allows:** The contact-window conditions are **bounded and noncontradictory** as fiction. The remaining question is whether actual prose can show Tava's difficult real-time choice, the near-side pilot's independently changing information, the moment the two can or cannot commit, and the consequences of not automatically returning to the same home. The existing frozen craft scene begins *after* the crossing, so the story has not earned that physical transition emotionally and perceptually on the page.

**What further micro-analysis would not establish:** It would not qualify the whole Book 11, determine whether Nemi/Ivo matter outside the trade, give Yael agency, make the Book 12 meeting possible, or demonstrate ontology causality. More nautical worksheets might decrease uncertainty about a local crossing while doing little to test what readers would actually experience.

**Author decision boundary:** The author has specified twelve Books and general emotional commitments, not accepted the particular Nemi/Rillturn sequence. The author may ultimately reject or reshape the one-time trade. No need to force that choice **before** further noncanonical realization within the established experimental authority, but any promotion to canon or definitive Series structure would require author judgment.
