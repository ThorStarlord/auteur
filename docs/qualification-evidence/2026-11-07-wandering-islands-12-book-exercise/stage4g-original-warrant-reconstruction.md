**Four claim labels used exclusively for warrant entries:**

- **FROZEN_OBSERVATION** — An action, state or reported result stated as occurring *in the frozen Stage 4F/4E fictional record*. This identifies what the worker **wrote**, not independent verification of its plausibility.
- **NEW_P4F_ASSUMPTION** — A favorable support condition explicitly introduced in Stage 4F rather than warranted by the older Stage 3B/4E state; it may itself be directly narrated, but its provenance is new.
- **SUPPORTED_INFERENCE** — A logical consequence of a directly narrated event, named with its supporting connection and no fabricated measurement.
- **UNSPECIFIED** — A possibly consequential premise, observation detail or operating margin for which these frozen sources supply no usable particulars.

The distinction matters: **FROZEN_OBSERVATION is not automatically an independently established causal warrant**; **NEW_P4F_ASSUMPTION is not automatically a contradiction**; **UNSPECIFIED is not an assertion that a condition was absent or impossible**.
