# Series working architecture W6: nodes (id | category | title | summary)

- th_core_question | theme | Central question | Who gives back the water, and must giving be counted? The question changes meaning as the Old Hand readings change what a return is, and it is answered differently by each finale mechanism.
- th_hands_and_draw | thread | Hands and draw | Who gives return labour, and who is counted for draw. This is the series' governing variable. Until Book 19 draw follows shares alone. From Book 20 it partly follows hands owed. Each finale mechanism answers the question differently.
- th_custody | thread | Custody of the record | Who holds the Ledger and on whose authority. Custody is the Stone Hall's source of legitimacy until Book 5. Public access to the credit record begins in Book 5. Custody of the master record is contested until Book 15 and plural after the Reading Hall is founded.
- th_maintenance | thread | Keeping the channels | Who performs the return labour that keeps the spring balanced, and at what cost to families with few hands. Old lineages hold the repair skill from Books 9-11. From Book 20 lineage crews carry channel repair under a Special Draw Right, and households that can meet the rota carry the rest.
- th_reading | thread | Reading the First Stones | Who may read the oldest script and which reading becomes binding. Book 1 reads the share-marks as evidence, not the script. Partial readings of the script are Books 8 and 12, and the full reading is Book 15.
- eng_ledger | engine | Ledger engine | Recorded shares set the draw. Credit can be falsified or favoured (Book 1), opened to public access (Book 5), traded (Book 6), re-read as gift or contract (Book 15), and from Book 20 partly set by hands owed. Each change alters who must return labour.
- eng_source | engine | Source engine (physical commons) | The spring's balance follows the gap between draw and return. Thinning leaves channels dry and floods wild water below; surfeit brings stagnant pools and fever. It responds to balance rather than to any character's intention. Its deep-store pulse, the Long Return, is predicted from Book 23 and arrives in Book 30.
- eng_old_hand | engine | Old Hand engine (interpretive legitimacy) | Readings of the First Stones change what a share is: debt (Stone Hall), gift (Terrace Council) or contract (Mill Compact). A reading changes whether returns are recorded and therefore the balance on the Source.
- str_seasonal_turn | structure | The seasonal turn | The rhythm of return labour across the terraces, repeated each season with changing participants. It makes belonging visible and provides the recurring scene texture across Books.
- str_measured_turn | structure | The counted Turn (high terraces) | Labour matched to shares in the high terraces, run by the Council since the Turn in Book 6: each share returns labour by rota, and draw still follows shares. The counted Turn matches return to draw, which keeps the high cistern from surfeit. The stewardship tally, from Book 15, records that labour as a record of care for this practice; it does not hold the water.
- str_unmeasured_return | structure | The gift practice (Lower Ammen) | Labour given without a count, from the one-season Unmeasured Return in Book 15 onward. It keeps the lower channels working by release at need, and it depends on the Gate for that release.
- str_hollow_gate | structure | The Hollow Gate | The sluice that divides the high cistern's release between the upper and lower channels. The Council has set its schedule since the Turn. In the Book 15 trial the high cistern showed surfeit. The Source responds to the balance between draw and return, and surfeit is too much return, or stored water with no draw. The construction assumes that the trial's uncounted return exceeded the draw the Turn counted, so the surplus showed in the high cistern. The original does not name this mechanism, and the author may reject it. Release draws the cistern down and cannot raise it. The schedule is the object of the Book 21 conflict.
- str_lowfen_channel | structure | The Lowfen channel | The lower channel that carries Lower Ammen's wells. It breaks in the thaw of Book 19 and is rebuilt by lineage crews in Book 20. Its lowest terrace is where a declined share runs wild.
- str_declined_share | structure | A declined share | A household's exit from the rota in Book 21. It forfeits its share, so its water goes wild and runs down the lowest Lowfen terrace. This is a physical cost of declining, not the act of a villain.
- str_long_return | structure | The Long Return (pulse) | A deep-store pulse in Book 30, predicted by the Balance Stones cut in Book 23. It needs channel labour before its date and a Gate schedule that routes it. The routing decides which channel takes the surge, and that choice belongs to the valley.
- rx_wonder | reader_experience | Wonder at a responsive order | The reader's sense that the valley's order is larger and more responsive than any institution assumed.
- rx_belonging | reader_experience | Everyday belonging | The warmth of shared labour and meals, which the series must keep specific across generations.
- rx_consequence | reader_experience | Consequential loss | Grief and anger that follow from changed standing: those once credited lose standing, and those once uncounted gain it. From Book 20, those whose draw is cut for hands they cannot return also lose standing.
- rx_hope | reader_experience | Hopeful flourishing | The earned sense that a shared practice can outlast the institution that began it. The finale must show the cost.
- inst_stone_hall | other | Stone Hall (Reckoners) | Hereditary keepers of the Ledger. Under this working path the Hall splits in Book 5, as the original has it, and a remnant under Keeper Corrow continues as a dissenting institution. Typed as other because v2 has no institution category.
- inst_terrace_council | other | Terrace Council | Formed in Book 6 from families who take the Turn. Runs the counted Turn in the high terraces, keeps the stewardship tally from Book 15, and sets the Hollow Gate schedule until the Gate Table takes it in Book 22. Holds the gift reading as legitimate.
- inst_mill_compact | other | Mill Compact | Lowland grain-buyers who purchase shares once records open. Their purchased draw carries no return labour. They pursue contract-debt and trade interests from outside the valley and have their own Book 27-29 bid.
- inst_reading_hall | other | Reading Hall | Founded after the full reading in Book 15 to make the First Stones publicly readable. Keeps the public record and its readings. Publishes the Gate gauge reading (Book 22) and the Balance Stones (Book 23). It has no authority over the schedule.
- inst_lineage_crews | other | Maintenance lineages | Old maintenance lineages (Ysa Trant's) who carry channel-maintenance skill from Books 9-11. From Book 20 they hold a Special Draw Right, conditional on training apprentices from outside the lineage. Working design: revocable by the Council. Whether it stays revocable is open question 5.
- inst_gate_table | other | Gate Table | A rotating inter-community body formed in Book 22 to set the Hollow Gate schedule from the gauge reading. Seats for each practice, and one seat drawn by lot from households without lineage craft. It holds power over the schedule only, with no authority over the record, the shares or any practice.
- gn_epic_fantasy | genre | Epic fantasy | Governing genre: the quest is to understand a physical commons that answers choices, not to defeat a rival power.
- gn_pastoral_belonging | genre | Pastoral belonging | Supporting genre: belonging produced by shared return labour and eroded by unshared draw.
- gn_political_institutional | genre | Political-institutional | Supporting genre: institutions compete for legitimacy over custody and distribution of the record and of the Gate schedule. Governs Arc 2 at phase level.
- gn_mystery_discovery | genre | Mystery and discovery | Tertiary genre: partial knowledge of the record and the spring changes choices; readings are staged so discovery advances by Book.
- alt_a_reciprocal | structure | Finale Alternative A (not chosen) | Noncoercive coordination by mutual dependence: gate dependence, pair-hands, a care floor, and a Gate Table with no record authority. Draw is decoupled from hands; care is counted and nothing is owed. Failure modes: correlated shock, lowland draw left unhanded, and surveillance of the tally.
- alt_b_allotted | structure | Finale Alternative B (not chosen) | An allotted binding rota for the rebuild window, with lot-drawn one-cycle Table seats, a sunset, a care floor, and grain-labour from the Compact. Hands are owed in proportion to draw, and inside the window a shortfall is a deficit to repay, not a cut in water. Failure modes: standing power through schedule, and the Hall's custody returning under another name.
- alt_c_ratified_trigger | structure | Finale Alternative C (not chosen) | Ratified in Books 27-29: normal seasons follow Alternative A, and a published Balance Stones threshold activates Alternative B's rota for a fixed window. The valley chooses the rule before the threshold is crossed. Failure modes: the threshold becomes an authority, activation by the Reading Hall's reading may be unchecked, ratification fails in calm years, and the gauge is disputed.

# Open questions (index: text)

0: Does the spring respond to intent, or only to measured balance? Book 15 gives a partial answer; the full ontology is deliberately not promised.
1: What do the lowland towns owe Ammen, and what does Ammen owe them?
2: Which finale mechanism, A, B, C or a third, does the author choose for who gives return labour and who is counted for it? Not decided.
3: Does the shared accounting of care bind, and in what form? Not decided.
4: Is the lineage Special Draw Right revocable or permanent, what training condition does it carry, and who carries the Turn when the maintenance lineages end? Not decided.
5: Is the Book 20 hands-to-draw conversion a lasting settlement, or a stage the finale must revisit?
6: Does the Compact accept grain-labour in exchange for settling its claim, and does the Gate Table's schedule reach its lowland draw? Not decided.
7: Which channel takes the Long Return's surge, does any household flood, and does the flood cost lives or lasting losses? These are author choices and are not decided.
8: Does a Balance Stones threshold exist (Alternative C), who reads it, and which body ratifies it? Not decided.
9: Is Hesper's generational motive independent of the author's preference for the Turn's sustainability? Not yet tested.
10: Do the Old Hand's readings need intermediate states between Books 8 and 15? Not decided.
11: How many Brightwater shares may be drawn before the Balance Stones restrain them?
12: Does reform from within remain the series' stance, and does the Gate Table model it or warn against it? Not decided.
13: Will any terrace keep a measured ledger in Book 30, and is plurality of practice the intended end state? Not decided.
14: Are the Hollow Gate and the Gate Table accepted parts of the world? Not decided; both are construction additions.
15: What can the high cistern physically store above its tolerance? Not established; Alternatives B and C depend on it.
