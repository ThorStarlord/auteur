The record supports a narrower answer than the question assumes. Only one development is supplied, the mechanism "Keeping the channels" (E1), and the record reads only two existing Books for it, Books 24 and 25 (E14). Eleven elements cite it. Book 25 repeats five of Book 24's six citing elements word for word, and the one Book 24 element it does not repeat is the cohort's possible standing in coordinating channel work (E8). Repetition is therefore already present in the draft's wording, though the record does not show whether it marks a condition that recurs or is a drafting duplicate. A development plan has to settle that first.

What the record establishes about the mechanism itself is a sequence of changes in who carries the labour. Old lineages have held the repair skill from Books 9 to 11, and from Book 20 lineage crews carry channel repair under a Special Draw Right, while "households that can meet the rota carry the rest" (E1). A generation that came of age after Book 20 "has never seen the labour" (E6, a derived element). E1 also names the cost, which falls on "families with few hands". That cost is the part of the mechanism the record leaves most open.

I infer that the mechanism avoids repetition only if each stage changes the question it answers rather than the quantity of labour. The record already moves through four questions: who holds the skill, who holds the Right, whether the Right is still conditional on training, and whether the labour can be recorded as capability rather than as debt. A later Book that asks one of these again would repeat the draft. The training condition is the strongest seam. E3 says the maintenance lineages "need the condition demonstrated or the Right is exposed", so the condition is a prerequisite for keeping the Right, not merely a theme. E7 notes that the source gives a reversal for the review point, but the cited items do not say what that reversal is, so I cannot rely on its content.

I propose a sequence of stages after Book 25, none numbered, since they lie beyond the existing Books. The first tests the training condition by evidence rather than by the lineages' own assertion, using the public capability record that E5 introduces, "a public capability record, separate from debt, tally and aggregate balance". Showing the condition is a causal step for the Right, because the lineages' stake depends on it (E3). The second asks who grants standing. E8 says the cohort may claim standing in how the valley coordinates channel work, yet Book 24 "names no body that would grant that standing and creates none." This question is new to the draft and is the natural next step, since Book 25 does not repeat that element (E14). The third reverses or lapses the Right, so the labour falls back on households on the rota and the cost E1 names becomes the centre of the Book. The author's own constraint applies here: "Major victories change the world rather than restore the initial situation" (E2). A reversal should leave the rota and the record changed, not restore the arrangement before Book 20. The fourth brings the generation that never saw the labour (E6) to the record, with the split between "counted and uncounted practice" (E4) as its material. E4 carries an unknown legacy status, so this stage rests on a claim the record has not established.

Two alternative orders are weaker on the record. Publishing the capability record before the condition is demonstrated is weakened, because E3 says the condition must be shown first. Ending the Right early is unresolved, because the reversal's content is not in the cited items. On tone, E2 warns against a "generic escalating-villain epic", so the conflict should be the cost of returning labour to people, not a rising antagonist.

Open decisions for the author: what the reversal is (E7); whether Book 25's repeated wording is intended (E14); who grants standing, if anyone (E8); and whether the capability record is exported at all (E5).

Uncertainty: these are excerpts, and E5 is truncated mid-sentence. Most claims rest on agent-proposed items (E3, E5, E7), not author-fixed ones. With one development, any opportunity must come from the mechanism's own parts rather than from its interaction with other developments. The author named no culmination, so I offer none.
