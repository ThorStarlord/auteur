AXIS 2: CREATIVE SUBSTANCE

Answer P
1. The training test as a dilemma with two outcomes. Under E3 and E7 the Right continues if the condition is shown and is exposed if it is not. This gives each later Book a verdict to reach rather than another round of the same labour.
2. Standing for the uncounted cohort. E8 leaves a claim with no grantor. That gives the split practice in E4 a political claim and a question of its own for a later Book.
3. A lapsed Right with the cost at the centre. If the Right reverses, households on the rota carry the labour (E1). E2's "rather than restore the initial situation" requires a changed rota and record, and families with few hands become the protagonists of that Book.
Most serious weakness: the test is framed as evidence-gathering with no internal opposition. E3's fear that craft standards will be diluted by untrained gift-hands is absent, so the chain of test, record, standing and reversal reads as a tidy plan with little friction.

Answer Q
1. Dilution fear set against the outside apprentices the test needs (relation 2, citing E3). Ysa Trant's lineage guards craft standards while the training condition requires outsiders. This is a dilemma inside the lineage rather than a villain, which matches E2's warning against a generic escalating-villain epic.
2. A capability record offered before the condition is tested (alternative 3). It raises a legitimacy question: whether a record can show capability the Right has not certified. The answer is left to the author.
3. Uncounted practice made visible without being counted (opportunity 2, citing E4 and E5). It gives the cohort recognition without the debt-and-tally logic, and makes the split and the record mean something neither does alone.
Most serious weakness: the cost named in E1, falling on families with few hands, appears only in reader experience and never as a stage. The trajectory develops institutional mechanics (conditions, records, standing) while the human stake stays undeveloped. Its prerequisite and enables relations also make the path more determined than the record supports.

WHICH TO DEVELOP

I would give the author P. On grounding, P has no misreadings and four overreaches; Q has two misreadings, one of them a false claim about E5, and six overreaches. On creative value, Q has the sharper dilemma (its relation 2) and more alternatives, but P offers a developable arc in which the cost of labour becomes the centre of a stage. Add Q's dilution tension to P's test stage before developing it.
