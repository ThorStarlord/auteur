# Human-author walkthrough protocol — Connected Creative Thread

**Status:** Protocol prepared, **zero participants recruited or observed**, **no results recorded**.
**Target:** Auteur experimental Studio after its intention-aware development candidate passes code-bearing L1/L2 checks.
**Participants:** Prefer 2–3 volunteers able to author creative material. Do not describe this as a representative population or proof of literary quality.

## Preparation

Use a disposable local project with a clear Story Vision and a rich source-backed ArchitectureCandidate. Preserve prior accepted state; do not use private manuscripts without permission. Freeze the executable code SHA. Prepare one familiar relationship or emotional target and one documented pending revision. Give participants a brief neutral introduction distinguishing working material from accepted canon. Do not coach the participant on where to click during scored tasks.

## Tasks and observable success

1. **Explore and decide:** Select a narrative relationship, ask for alternatives, compare mechanisms, choose or reject one. Observe whether alternative differences are understood rather than merely labeled.
2. **Keep and reconsider intention:** Record an active and a provisional direction, request a conflict challenge, explicitly supersede one and withdraw the other. Observe status comprehension and whether a user mistakenly thinks a working direction is canon.
3. **Critique before apply:** Ask about the narrative impact of an unapplied holistic revision. Observe whether recorded differences are distinguished from model interpretation and whether the author understands that critique does not apply changes.
4. **Realize and recover:** Carry a selected emotional/relational meaning into Story Realization; re-enter in a fresh session and identify the currently active direction, historical choices, and the next safe author action.

## Evidence recording template

For each participant record (with their informed agreement): environment/SHA, authoring task, success or failure, visible blockers, unprompted comments, time or step count only if genuinely observed, concrete interface misunderstandings, and one useful creative outcome. Note when the participant could not complete an operation without developer intervention.

Separate in the report:

- Deterministic code behavior.
- Same-agent creative output.
- Independent model critique.
- Author comments and choices.
- Evaluator interpretation.
- Unobserved / unavailable evidence.

No numerical creativity score is required. A participant saying an answer feels useful is a report of preference, not an objective correctness metric.

## Decision implications

- Technical regression found: repair the smallest owning layer and rerun the affected task.
- Repeated comprehension friction: simplify the author-facing control and retest.
- Creative reasoning remains cosmetic: improve source-bound reasoning obligations or critical evaluation rather than adding persistence.
- Participants succeed with low friction: retain the bounded experience, then separately review whether default promotion is warranted.
- Participants unavailable: keep experimental and record `HUMAN_EVIDENCE_NOT_OBTAINED`, never `PASS`.

## Results

**Not conducted as of 2026-11-06.** No human usability, preference or creative-effectiveness claims are supported by this protocol alone.
