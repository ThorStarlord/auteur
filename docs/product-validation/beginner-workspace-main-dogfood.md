# Beginner Workspace Main Dogfood Observation

**Date:** 2026-09-17  
**Repository candidate:** `87971e57d186d1559e20aebce3403b311c8a6f2f`  
**Evidence type:** agent-observed workflow probe; not independent human evidence.

## Scenario

The probe used a long-form Mystery premise:

> A disgraced museum conservator discovers that a recently donated painting
> contains a hidden layer painted over an unsolved portrait theft. When the
> donor is found dead before the restoration can begin, the conservator must
> determine whether the painting reveals a family secret, a forged provenance,
> or a crime still in progress—while deciding whether exposing the truth will
> destroy the person who gave her a second chance.

## Observed path

The Beginner Workspace successfully supported:

```text
premise → Discover → Story Direction
→ Story Identity → canonical Identity
→ Whole-Story Structure → canonical Structure
```

The projection exposed the expected working/canonical distinction, readable
decision labels, Mystery guidance, milestone reviews, and explicit acceptance.

## First material friction

After Whole-Story Structure acceptance, the workspace offered no coherent
handoff into the existing outline/planning and chapter-drafting workflows. The
workspace correctly displayed the accepted foundation, but the next available
actions were revision controls only. The author would have to discover and
reconstruct the Cartographer/Bard workflow outside the Beginner Workspace.

This is a **workflow-continuity gap**, not evidence that the existing outline
or drafting engines are missing. The next product package should define the
smallest authoritative handoff from the accepted Beginner foundation into the
existing blueprint, Cartographer, chapter-contract, and drafting services. It
must preserve existing authority boundaries and must not introduce a second
outline or drafting engine.

## Qualification boundary

This probe does not replace a real-author dogfood session. Human usability of
the merged Beginner Workspace remains separately qualified from the earlier
independent human transfer run. A real author should validate the complete
post-foundation path before a continuation package is selected for production
implementation.
