# Beginner Story Development Continuation

This milestone completes the forward beginner path from accepted foundation to
first-draft handoff:

```text
accepted StoryIdentity + accepted whole-story Structure
  → derived whole-story outline proposal
  → accepted outline
  → Chapter 1 plan
  → candidate scene plans
  → accepted draft handoff
  → story navigator orientation
```

The continuation reuses the existing Cartographer, chapter-contract, scene, and
Bard/Critics capabilities. It adds orchestration and beginner-facing review
surfaces; it does not add another outline engine or semantic layer.

## Authority boundary

Outline and chapter plans are derived proposals until the author explicitly
accepts them. Scene plans sit on the Structure → Realization boundary: they name
purpose, POV, entry state, goal, conflict, change, exit state, and continuity
constraints, but they do not silently become realized canonical state. Drafting receives
the accepted identity, structure, outline, chapter plan, and scene plan as
inputs; the workspace records a handoff and does not invoke an LLM implicitly.

L3 verification and human qualification remain separate evidence gates for this
milestone.
