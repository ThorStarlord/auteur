# Decision-Oriented Tutor V1

The Tutor presents one derived Decision Card at a time. A card explains the
decision, its craft principle, recommendation, alternatives, trade-offs,
beginner trap, evidence, and downstream consequence. Card output is
`DERIVED / NOT CANON`.

Tutor depth (`recommend`, `explain`, `teach`, `challenge`, `quiz`) changes
presentation only. `tutor next` may persist an atomic local session; `tutor
choose` records an explicit response but does not accept Story Identity or any
other canonical artifact.
