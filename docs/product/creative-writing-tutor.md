# Creative Writing Tutor V1

Auteur helps creative beginners learn narrative craft while creating their own
stories. Its role is a guided, opinionated, learning-by-doing creative writing
tutor.

Success has two dimensions: artifact value (a stronger, coherent story
decision) and learning value (the author understands why it works and knows
the next useful decision).

Story Design Packs are curated knowledge, not story canon. Tutor Guidance can
teach, compare, recommend, diagnose, and challenge, but it cannot silently
rewrite or canonize an author's choice. Pack knowledge is not a
recommendation, and a recommendation is not accepted `StoryIdentity` state.

## Tutor loop

1. Orient the author to one decision.
2. Teach one craft concept in plain language.
3. Apply it to the author's story.
4. Recommend one choice with reasons.
5. Compare alternatives and trade-offs.
6. Ask the author to decide, optionally with a comprehension check.

Guidance is a derived artifact labelled `DERIVED / NOT CANON`. Identity-level
mutation remains behind the existing explicit proposal and ratification paths.

V1 supplies Superhero, Anti-Hero, Hard Determinism, and Corporate Superhuman
Metropolis packs. The existing Erotic Fiction Genre Pack remains supported by
its original compatibility API.
