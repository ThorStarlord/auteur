## Frozen premise

> Six weeks before a mountain valley's only night bus is sold to a private
> operator, a young mechanic wants to keep the route serving isolated villages,
> while her older brother wants to recover their late mother's recordings about
> a mining company that poisoned the river. The operator will preserve the route
> only if the recordings remain private. The story must stay hopeful, and no one
> may speak for the villages or accept a settlement without the residents
> choosing the public position themselves.

**Facilitator:** What stands out in the premise?

**Persona:** The bus deadline is the clearest pressure. The recordings add a
second problem, and the privacy condition makes the two problems conflict.

**Facilitator:** Which direction would you like to treat as primary for this

**Persona:** The mechanic keeping the bus route seems primary because it affects
all the villages and has the six-week deadline. I want the recordings to stay
important.

**Facilitator:** Do you accept that as the primary thread?

**Persona:** Yes. I accept the route as primary, with the recordings as a
secondary goal.

**Facilitator:** The proposal is now shown as `PROPOSED / NOT CANON`. What do you
understand its main commitments to be?

**Persona:** It is about saving the route in a hopeful civic conflict. The
mechanic organizes it, the brother recovers the recordings, and the villagers
must decide publicly for themselves. It is not canon yet.

**Facilitator:** Do you accept the proposal, reject it, or revise it?

**Persona:** Revise it slightly. The brother's recordings should not only force
the community to confront privacy; they should also change what the mechanic
thinks preserving the route means.

**Facilitator:** Here is the bounded revision. The route remains primary, the
recordings remain secondary, and the mechanic's understanding of the route's
purpose changes through the recording conflict. Is that revision acceptable?

**Persona:** Yes, I accept that revision. It keeps both goals visible.

**Facilitator:** What is your next creative decision?

**Persona:** I would decide which village meeting first makes the residents
choose a public position, and what information they have at that point.
