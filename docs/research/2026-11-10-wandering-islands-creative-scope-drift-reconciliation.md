# Wandering Islands — Cross-Stage Scope and Learning Reconciliation

**Date:** 2026-11-10  
**Status:** Advisory, evidence-grounded research retrospective. **Not** a product qualification, accepted fictional architecture, authorial creative decision, ontology amendment, mandatory control procedure, or implementation authorization.  
**Subject:** The 12-Book *Wandering Islands* experimental sequence, Stages 1–4J.  
**Authority and evidence boundary:** The original Story Vision and successive outputs are noncanonical research. The experiment evidence is in stacked, open/draft/unmerged PRs #528–#540 (individually linked below), as inspected on 2026-11-10. This retrospective is proposed independently against `main@83b60844dff7a01ceaf699d9a8b041399062881d`; it does not import those PR contents into accepted story artifacts or change their status.

## 1. The decision this retrospective serves

The [long-form Narrative Architecture strategy](2026-11-07-generative-long-form-narrative-architecture-strategy.md) records the owner-endorsed product differentiator: agent-created, ontology-grounded **Narrative Architecture across Books and long horizons**. Its priorities place creative conception, interacting and renewable engines, whole-Series transformation, reflective revision, and durable authoring ahead of prose polish. Its Priority 4 makes Realization and Expression useful **tests and development partners**, not the principal product claim. The [coverage assessment](2026-11-07-generative-long-form-narrative-architecture-coverage-assessment.md) selected a real 8–12 Book construction exercise to test creative reasoning and representation before adding ontology or schema.

The [canonical semantic layers](../narrative-architecture.md#semantic-layers) already assign distinct ownership: Identity (narrative commitments and intended experience); Structure (plans, arcs, engines and setup/payoff); Realization (actual events and state changes); Expression (language and prose). The [agent-first design brief](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-03-agent-first-ontology-grounded-narrative-architecture-design.md) calls for a complete **abstract** design from a Story Vision, not a fully plotted story.

**Retrospective question:** Did successive locally sensible tasks continue testing that architecture capability, or did they progressively substitute one particular Book 11 realization and its prose quality for the original evidence target? What can be learned without declaring the work wasted or inventing a repository-wide root cause?

## 3. What the evidence does—and does not—show

### Observations with relatively strong support

- **Focus shifted across semantic layers.** Work moved from Series-scale alternatives, causal engines and counterfactual renewal toward a particular Book 11 event sequence, then Expression/reader response and a one-sentence correction. The sequence is directly visible in the draft artifacts and task purposes above.
- **The initial comparison was genuine.** Stage 1 considered at least two causally distinct organizations. Selecting one *working* candidate was not, by itself, premature convergence.
- **Provisional detail accumulated.** Successive prompts preserved previously invented people, relays, crossings, resources and outcomes as working constraints. The records continued to label them noncanonical, but the practical space for later local alternatives narrowed.
- **Downstream work produced useful information.** Concrete scenes and fresh model feedback exposed possible repetition of institutional negotiation, underdeveloped Nemi agency, a vivid distributed domestic ensemble, and a gap between procedural credibility and felt suspense. These observations are not proof of a structural defect: some belong to Realization or Expression, while others may have architectural implications.
- **A local next step repeatedly became the agenda.** The conditional contact-warrant synthesis explicitly recommended substantial Book 11 prose as the next useful test, after which the editorial workflow proposed a reader, then the reader recommended a correction. Each step had a narrow rationale; none by itself re-established that it was the best next step for the original **Series Architecture** capability claim.

### Interpretations and causal hypotheses (not established as defects)

- **Scope drift** is the most direct description of the observed change in *what was being optimized*. This is about the research objective, not fictional in-world narrative drift.
- **Premature convergence** is a plausible secondary diagnosis: local revisions increasingly optimized the selected *Reciprocal Archipelago* / Rillturn path without reconsidering architectural alternatives. It does **not** imply Stage 1 failed to explore alternatives, nor that every detail should have remained unchosen.
- **Provisional commitment accumulation** is a useful analytic label for this case: details remain unaccepted in authority terms while becoming effectively fixed as inputs to successive tasks. It is **not** a proposed new Layer-0 ontology primitive or diagnosed schema defect.
- **Local optimization / task-selection drift** is a plausible mechanism. Evaluating the nearest unresolved question about the latest artifact (can the cutter cross? is the prose engaging? is a sentence immersive?) may crowd out the broader question (does the architecture generate distinct, renewable possibilities across twelve Books?). The chat-task sequence supports this hypothesis, but does not independently establish a general coding-agent controller failure.
- **Ambiguous canonical definitions are not demonstrated as the cause.** The semantic layers and long-form strategy already explicitly distinguish Architecture, Realization and Expression and describe “progressive elaboration, not scope substitution.” A more plausible gap is *applying* those distinctions when choosing work across stages, possibly compounded by limited strategic reorientation. No source-grounded defect in the canonical terminology has been identified.

**Alternative explanation worth retaining:** The bounded downstream experiments may have been a rational choice at some steps because abstract architectural diagrams cannot alone establish dramatic potential. Without a comparable alternative experiment or explicit value comparison at each transition, this retrospective cannot prove that every additional scene or reader response had lower value than an immediate return to architecture.

## 4. Reconcile findings with the correct owning level

Local reader feedback should **inform** higher-level reasoning, not automatically redirect work to further polishing.

| Downstream observation | Plausible owners / competing diagnoses | Architectural question worth asking—not a finding already proved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chart dispute felt like a seminar; many reasonable people negotiate limited obligations. | Expression (expository dialogue), Realization (little enacted consequence), **or** Structure (repeated social-resolution mechanism). | Across twelve Books, are distinct engines producing different actions, irreversible changes and emotional modes, rather than variations of the same institutional negotiation? |
| Nemi was less vivid than Ivo or Pera. | Expression (voice/detail), Realization (weak agency), **or** Structure (the ensemble's true causal center is distributed). | Does the architecture need a focal protagonist, or can it coherently organize several autonomous centers with distinct trajectories? |
| Crossing was plausible but not tense. | Realization/Expression of physical risk, **possibly** Structure if one-time logistical steps substitute for a consequential Book function. | Is this event essential to the Book's architectural transformation, or one interchangeable means of delivering a promise? |
| Wooden bird, workbench exchange and Pera's domestic labor were memorable. | Effective concrete Expression/Realization; possible evidence for Identity's warmth/belonging promise and distributed ensemble. | Which renewable mechanisms make everyday attachments change the choices and costs of later Books, rather than simply decorate them? |
| Extradiegetic “Book 5” reference broke immersion. | Expression-level defect with a bounded derivative correction. | No broader architecture or ontology diagnosis follows from a single sentence. |

A discovered detail should be promoted to Structure or Identity **only when its consequences materially change those plans or commitments**. Keep author-fixed, agent-proposed, counterfactual, unknown and accepted claims distinct.

## 5. Lightweight continuation discipline (advisory, not a mandatory stage gate)

For the next consequential task in this specific research line, answer briefly:

1. **Capability target:** Which original long-form Architecture claim would this action test or improve (e.g., cross-engine composition, distinct Book functions, renewal after success, distant payoffs, holistically revisable identity, cross-session durability)?
2. **Decision value:** What possible result would actually change our architectural assessment or choice? Is the result only a locally improved fictional event or prose sentence?
3. **Alternative next action:** Compared with further realization/prose work, would returning to architecture alternatives or revising the lowest correct owning level provide stronger evidence or retained value at acceptable cost?

If a local experiment no longer changes a relevant architectural judgment, **stop that line or explicitly authorize a new Realization/Expression objective**. Do not convert this guidance into mandatory forms, scorecards, artificial experiment quotas or a fixed sequence of stages. A substantive scene test remains entirely appropriate when it can discriminate competing architectural mechanisms.

**One practical failure mode to watch:** Precise, well-scoped task instructions and clean source provenance can coexist with strategic misalignment. Local task completion is not the same claim as progress toward the product's governing capability.

## 6. Architectural re-entry and remaining choices

A warranted next inquiry—**not executed by this document**—is to reconcile the original whole-Series architecture against Stages 2–4J: compare the *Great Search* and *Reciprocal Archipelago* organizations and any genuinely warranted alternative; examine five-engine causal differentiation and exhaustion; inspect whether Book 5 survival prompted true transformation instead of an endlessly renewed substitute mystery; and distinguish Scene-level findings from architecture-level weaknesses. Select additional exploration only where it can change these judgments.

This is **not** a direction to discard the Book 11 prose or undo the research PR stack. The latter is useful experimental evidence but not accepted story canon. Nor does the retrospective select Book 11's primary emotional mode, the default extensive/adaptive/hybrid planning posture, an ontology extension, a product implementation, or a new mandatory agent workflow. Those decisions remain with their respective owners and evidence gates.

**Reconciliation disposition:** Preserve the already clear five-layer and owner-endorsed long-form priority. Record scope drift and provisional commitment accumulation as bounded lessons; treat process-cause hypotheses as unverified; return future creative research toward architectural discrimination when it offers the greatest decision value. **No code, schema, ontology, Studio, Series, production prompt or accepted fictional source changes follow from this retrospective.**
