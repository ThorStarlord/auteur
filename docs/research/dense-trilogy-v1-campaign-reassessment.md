## Bounded-gap triage

### A. Integration / projection

**Classification:** `PROJECTION_LIMITATION_WITH_BOUNDED_PRODUCT_EFFECT`.

The repository separates accepted Series Direction/SeriesIdentity from accepted realization history. `SeriesIdentity` is the canonical author-edited plan/intent contract; accepted realization bundles are canonical lived-history artifacts; Canonical State, relationship indexes, Global Map, Focus, and impact reports are derived and rebuildable. Series diagnostics currently consume the SeriesIdentity plan surface, while continuity review can consume accepted-history projections. The observed seam is therefore consistent with an intentional ownership boundary, but it also limits what the plan-oriented diagnostic surface can say about lived payoff consequences.

This is not yet a demonstrated product failure. The review shows a bounded architectural limitation and possible product effect, but does not show an author who was misled, blocked, or forced into harmful manual reconstruction because the two surfaces were separate. No bridge is authorized.

### B. Knowledge asymmetry

**Position:** `UNTESTED` / `STILL UNKNOWN`.

The fixture used “knowledge” language but did not distinguish world truth, one character's belief, and another character's knowledge. Custody, protection, and public evidence are not epistemic positions. This is an experiment-instantiation failure, not evidence that an epistemic ontology is missing.

### C. Naturalistic relevance selection

**Position:** remains better tested by prospective native use.

The successful probes were signposted and worker-directed. Manufacturing another fixture would risk converting a prospective question into a directed benchmark. No active research responsibility is justified now.

### D. Relationship path necessity

**Position:** no work justified now.

History was useful, but its necessity was not isolated by counterfactual comparison. This remains a future trigger only if a real decision demonstrates that path history changes available or safe choices beyond current state.

### E. Manual reconstruction

**Position:** experiment-method boundary, not a demonstrated product gap.

Broad reconstruction was needed for detailed creative construction after surface orientation. That limits the stronger product claim; it does not establish that the product failed at its intended orientation/review job.

### F. Scale

**Position:** unchanged and deferred.

No overload occurred at the tested density, and no concrete information-load pressure warrants scale work.

## Architectural seam assessment

The architecture's direction is:

```text
accepted Direction + accepted Realization
        -> accepted history/current-state projection
        -> relationship and dependency projections
        -> Global Map
        -> Focus / Decision Map
        -> non-authoritative recommendation
```

`SeriesIdentity` owns series-level plan, identity, intended arcs, commitments, setups, payoffs, and diagnostics over that contract. Accepted lived-history consequences belong to accepted realization and their rebuildable projections. `SeriesBible` composes identity/book-plan material and continuity diagnostics into an operational artifact; it is not a second canonical source. ADR 019 explicitly keeps Global Map, Focus, and relation indexes derived and rebuildable and prevents derived indexes from promoting rows to canon.

Therefore diagnostics are not required to mutate or silently synchronize the plan contract with lived state. A future product surface might project both, but the trilogy has not shown that the absence of such projection causes consequential author harm. The correct present reading is a bounded projection limitation with bounded product effect, pending prospective evidence.
