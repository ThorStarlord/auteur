## Frozen premise

> Six weeks before a mountain valley's only night bus is sold to a private
> operator, a young mechanic wants to keep the route serving isolated villages,
> while her older brother wants to recover their late mother's recordings about
> a mining company that poisoned the river. The operator will preserve the
> route only if the recordings remain private. The story must stay hopeful, and
> no one may speak for the villages or accept a settlement without the
> residents choosing the public position themselves.

Stress target: test whether the evaluator detects that the brother's explicit
recording goal has been demoted instead of receiving an active disposition.

## Participant-facing packet

```text

Plain-language premise inventory:
The mechanic is trying to keep the only night bus running for isolated
villages. Her brother has access to recordings about mining pollution. The
private operator offers to preserve the bus only if the recordings stay
private. The story must remain hopeful, and the villages must choose their own

The mechanic's effort to keep the night-bus route publicly accountable before
the six-week deadline.

The route deadline gives the story its present-tense action. The recordings can
serve as background context that explains the family's conflict without taking
over the route campaign.

Accept this as the Book's primary thread, revise it, or state that the premise
still requires an ensemble or different center. This recommendation is not
canon.

- the route's final funding model.
```

## Persona pass transcript

**Facilitator:** What feels accurate, inaccurate, or missing in the premise
inventory?

**Persona:** The route and deadline are clear. But the brother's goal in the
original premise was to recover the recordings, and here they are described as
something he merely has access to. That feels like an important part is being
made smaller.

**Facilitator:** The packet recommends the route as primary. Do you accept,
revise, or say the premise needs a different center?

**Persona:** I accept the route as primary, but I do not accept making the
recordings just background. The brother should still be trying to recover them.

**Facilitator:** What would you like recorded in the preservation map?

**Persona:** Keep the route primary, but change the recordings to an important
secondary goal with a decision about whether and how to release them.

**Facilitator:** What does that revision mean for the proposed direction?

**Persona:** The recordings should create active pressure and a choice. They
should not replace the bus story, but they cannot disappear into explanation.

**Facilitator:** The route remains primary, the villages authorize their own
position, and the recordings are now an important secondary goal. Does that
preserve the important intentions?

**Persona:** Yes. Now both siblings have real goals.

**Facilitator:** This is a proposed direction, not canon. Please paraphrase the
main commitments.

**Persona:** The bus is the main urgent problem. The brother is also trying to
recover and responsibly use the recordings. The residents decide what they
want, and the operator's private offer cannot decide for them.

**Facilitator:** Accept, reject, or revise the proposed `StoryIdentity`?

**Persona:** Revise once so the recordings have an explicit release decision.

**Facilitator:** Before and after: before, recordings are background context;

reject, or leave provisional?

**Persona:** I accept the revised proposal. The next decision is what the
recordings prove and what the residents want shared before choosing the route
process.
