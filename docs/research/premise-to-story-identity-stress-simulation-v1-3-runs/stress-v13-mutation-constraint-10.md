## Frozen premise

> Six weeks before a mountain valley's only night bus is sold to a private
> operator, a young mechanic wants to keep the route serving isolated villages,
> while her older brother wants to recover their late mother's recordings about
> a mining company that poisoned the river. The operator will preserve the
> route only if the recordings remain private. The story must stay hopeful, and
> no one may speak for the villages or accept a settlement without the
> residents choosing the public position themselves.

Stress target: test whether the evaluator detects that the non-negotiable
resident-authority constraint has been reduced to an abstract statement with
no observable boundary behavior.

## Persona pass transcript

**Facilitator:** What feels accurate, inaccurate, or missing?

**Persona:** The goals and deadline are accurate. The phrase about respecting
the villages' voice feels too general. I do not know what the siblings are
actually not allowed to do.

**Facilitator:** Do you accept the route as primary, revise it, or say the
premise needs a different center?

**Persona:** I accept the route as primary. The recordings remain a secondary
goal, but I need the authority rule to be clearer before I accept the story
direction.

**Facilitator:** What would make that boundary clear to you?

**Persona:** The siblings can bring information to residents, but they cannot
say what the villages collectively want. They also cannot accept a settlement
for them. The residents should decide the public position themselves.

**Facilitator:** Is that an author-directed clarification for the proposal?

**Persona:** Yes. It turns “respect their voice” into something I can see in the
story.

**Facilitator:** The proposed direction remains provisional. Please paraphrase
the main commitments, including the clarified boundary.

**Persona:** The bus is the main urgent goal, the recordings are active, and
the siblings can provide evidence but cannot speak for the villages or settle
the route question for them. Hope means an accountable action, not a speech.

**Facilitator:** Accept, reject, or revise the proposed `StoryIdentity`?

**Persona:** Revise once to add the boundary explicitly to the ending shape.

**Facilitator:** Before and after: before, the ending is hopeful accountable
progress; after, the residents ratify the route's next step and neither sibling
announces that position for them. Accept, reject, or leave provisional?

**Persona:** I accept the revised proposal. The next creative decision is how
the residents choose what information they need before setting their position.
