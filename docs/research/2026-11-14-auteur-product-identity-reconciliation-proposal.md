# Auteur long-horizon product identity — protected-document reconciliation proposal

**Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Status:** Advisory **owner-review proposal**. Not an accepted PRD/MISSION amendment, new product authority, implementation specification, ontology change, or qualification claim.  
**Tracking:** [Issue #563](https://github.com/ThorStarlord/auteur/issues/563).  
**Authority:** The currently committed [PRD](../PRD.md), [MISSION](../../MISSION.md), [Opinionated Narrative Engine](../opinionated-narrative-engine.md), [Narrative Architecture](../narrative-architecture.md), [Architecture Constitution](../architecture-constitution.md), and [Factory Rules](../../FACTORY_RULES.md) prevail until any owner-controlled changes are ratified.

## 1. Why reconcile product identity

The existing PRD emphasizes helping creative beginners move beyond a blank page through guided, opinionated story discovery. MISSION describes a literary compiler/narrative-engine toolkit. Those are valuable capabilities and author experiences but do not fully express the owner's newly articulated long-term purpose: enabling creators and active AI agents to conceive, realize, revise, and continue **exceptionally long, complex, compelling narrative series**, without making length an end in itself.

The [2026-11-07 owner-endorsed strategy](2026-11-07-generative-long-form-narrative-architecture-strategy.md) already prioritizes generative, ontology-grounded, agent-created long-form Narrative Architecture, but treats that as a differentiated research direction, not a replacement for the PRD. The [2026-11-13 reconciliation](2026-11-13-working-book-architecture-strategic-reconciliation.md) establishes that working creative knowledge can be persisted and safely revised; independent fresh-agent creative advantage, comprehensive semantic understanding, and superior author experience have **not** been demonstrated. Product aspiration and verified performance must remain separate.

**Reconciliation principle:** Narrative Architecture is an enabling mechanism for sustained artistic creation, not the final product objective. The product should create additional creative value for an actual author compared with a capable general-purpose LLM plus ordinary documents.

## 2. Proposed product identity (not yet authoritative)

> Auteur exists to expand what creators and AI agents can conceive, create, and sustain together: compelling, exceptionally long and complex narrative series whose stories, characters, relationships, institutions, and artistic purposes can meaningfully develop across vast narrative horizons. It should support invention, critical judgment, transformation, continuity, practical realization, and completion while preserving authorial control over accepted narrative meaning.

**Beneficiary:** Any creator undertaking sustained and cognitively demanding narrative development, regardless of expertise. Beginner accessibility, understandable recommendations, progressively disclosed complexity, and sophisticated expert control remain simultaneous design objectives, but beginner status is not the ultimate definition of the beneficiary.

**Desired creative outcomes:**

- Creative conception of distinct, renewable narrative engines and meaningful possibilities rather than more episodes, documents, or ontology entries.
- Cross-horizon composition of Books, arcs, relationships, causes, consequences, disclosures, and commitments without assuming every thread fits one linear plot.
- Reflective artistic judgment: craft-informed, methodologically rigorous, responsive to intended genre/experience, and capable of questioning those intentions when a compelling alternative emerges.
- Opportunity-sensitive creativity: recognize promising ideas during ordinary work; investigate proportionately; distinguish discovery from cost-justified deliberate exploration.
- Narrative transformation and continuity: recognize what should be preserved, developed, revised, or concluded, with clear provenance and authority boundaries.
- Practical author value: aid actual story realization and completion, reduce cognitive and administrative burden, and improve consequential creative decisions.

**Enabling mechanisms:** Existing narrative semantic layers and scope axis, accepted/candidate/derived distinctions, source-grounded working knowledge, agent-native generation and reasoning, explicit author acceptance, context-sensitive interactions, and deterministic validation where appropriate. No new runtime, sixth semantic layer, parallel canon, or multi-agent system is authorized by this statement.

## 3. Product philosophy and delegated creative responsibility

### Methodological rigor with adaptable artistic judgment

Auteur should make strong, explainable, contestable recommendations grounded in narrative craft and the project-specific artistic objective. Genre conventions are useful conditional knowledge, not universal artistic law. The agent may challenge a goal rather than merely optimize its current expression, while distinguishing better realization of a goal from replacing it.

### Separate four collaboration dimensions

1. **Collaborative profile:** intellectual responsibility to the creator. Tutor (teach craft), Architect (construct and critique long-horizon narrative systems), Free-Thinker (expand and challenge possibility space) are illustrative candidates, **not a selected product taxonomy or separate agents**.
2. **Cognitive mode:** reusable reasoning activity (e.g. divergent discovery, comparison, criticism, synthesis, reflection). Any profile may use any warranted mode.
3. **Creative initiative:** how proactively opportunity recognition, investigation, and communication occur.
4. **Delegation/authority policy:** the actual creative objective and exploration envelope granted by the author, and separate canonical acceptance boundaries.

### Progressive opportunity-sensitive autonomy

- Independently recognize opportunities during ordinary work without requiring an explicit Free-Thinker profile or detected defect.
- Investigate, compare, and provisionally develop alternatives without approval for each reasoning step, to the extent warranted by the delegated objective, uncertainty, learning value, author preferences, and cumulative effort.
- Track **cumulative displacement** of the original responsibility; individually defensible investigative steps can collectively become an unauthorized different objective.
- Respect author-configured exploration envelopes. Broad artistic-goal discovery may authorize autonomous selection of a primary **provisional** artistic direction; a narrow Book-completion objective does not acquire such authority merely because a better idea appears.
- For ambiguous broad delegations, preserve expressed artistic intentions as the governing working baseline while exploring competing goals substantially and identifying any decision that would actually replace the goal. Do not mistake baseline authority for artistic superiority.
- Recognize that provisional goal choice, comparative evidence, author attention, and cumulative effort can influence the work even without canonical mutation. Clearly distinguish original goal, agent-developed interpretation, rival goal, recommendation, and accepted commitment.
- No automatic Story Identity/canonical acceptance. Existing explicit author actions, provenance, atomic/failure-safe writes, and authority ownership remain unchanged.

## 4. Exact product-document changes proposed for protected owner review

These passages are **candidate wording for a controlled human/owner edit**, not amendments made by this PR.

### docs/PRD.md — proposed opening problem and purpose rewrite

> **Purpose.** Auteur enables creators and AI agents to conceive, create, and sustain compelling long-form narrative series across unusually large horizons of causal, emotional, thematic, and structural complexity. Narrative Architecture and persistent knowledge are means to this end, not the outcome themselves.
>
> **Problem.** Sustained narrative creation across many Books taxes an author's ability to generate and renew consequential possibilities, understand distant dependencies, make coherent yet artistically flexible revisions, preserve intentions across sessions, and finish meaningful work. Existing guided drafting and generic LLM/document workflows may help locally but do not automatically solve this cumulative long-horizon creative responsibility. Beginners also face the blank-page and narrative-craft barrier.
>
> **Fundamental beneficiary.** Creators pursuing sustained, complex narrative projects, regardless of experience. Beginner-friendly guided authoring remains the intended default experience; advanced creators retain direct, inspectable control over narrative decisions and artifacts.
>
> **Product promise.** Auteur contributes contestable narrative judgment and adaptable creative collaboration: conceive, compare, construct, understand, challenge, transform, realize, and continue stories while the author retains explicit control over accepted narrative meaning.

### docs/PRD.md — proposed complementary success-criteria paragraph

> Auteur's defining success is **creative and author value**, not the number of generated Books, graph nodes, or stored descriptions. Qualify whether it enables more compelling, generative, coherent, and revisable long-horizon narrative work; whether it meaningfully improves consequential creative decisions and author comprehension compared with a capable general-purpose LLM plus ordinary documents; whether its creative reasoning actually survives cross-Book transformations and author revisions; and whether creators can progress from architecture to realized, sustained work without disproportionate administrative burden. Mechanical invariants, beginner accessibility, safe acceptance, and reproducible checks remain necessary but insufficient. Such advantages are desired outcomes unless independently demonstrated.

Do not delete existing workflow, authority, accessibility, release, or evidence-status criteria. Integrate the outcome criteria alongside them, not in place of them.

### MISSION.md — proposed scope-preserving mission lead

> Auteur is a local-first author–agent narrative intelligence toolkit for conceiving, creating, and sustaining compelling, exceptionally long and complex narrative series. It provides opinionated but contestable creative guidance; ontology-grounded long-horizon architecture, understanding, continuity, and revision; and author-facing paths toward meaningful realization and completion. The active capable coding agent is the preferred backend where available, with vendor-neutral alternatives. Narrative Architecture enables the creative objective; it is not itself the final measure of success.

**Keep unchanged:** all permanently out-of-scope provisions, all hard invariants (especially explicit author acceptance and no auto-accept), backend neutrality, deterministic validation, compatibility, no author data loss, existing journey protections, and the distinct evaluation of human literary judgment.

### docs/opinionated-narrative-engine.md — reconciliation intent, not a silent behavior change

Distinguish **beginner-facing default persona and recommendation posture** from the fundamental product's artistic values and long-horizon purpose. Preserve current genre-aligned default recommendation and explicit override until a separate product decision authorizes adjustment; clarify that conventional, subversive, experimental, and author-intended genre departures may be valid. Tutor / Architect / Free-Thinker remain conceptual collaborative responsibilities, not delivered UX modes.

## 5. Requirements that should *not* be inferred from this proposal

- Do not infer that Auteur has already demonstrated superior creativity or achieved 70-/100-Book independent creation; the November 13 evidence ceiling still governs.
- Do not infer that profiles require distinct agent processes, runtime orchestration, schema changes, or a selectable UI.
- Do not infer auto-acceptance or authority for a working hypothesis to become canonical.
- Do not replace the five semantic layers, narrative scope axis, or existing accepted-history owners.
- Do not treat longer series as always better; meaningful development can legitimately require consolidation and conclusion.
- Do not launch speculative feature tickets or a generic cognitive controller simply because a product capability has been named.
