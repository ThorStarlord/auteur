# Story Design Pack Tutor V2 Evaluation Rubric

Score each completed response independently from 0 to 4:

- **Artifact value:** specificity and actionability of the selected story decision.
- **Learning value:** accurate explanation of the governing craft principle and
  recognition of at least one relevant trade-off.
- **Transfer:** independent application of the same principle to a novel case.

Also record completion, evaluator uncertainty, author disagreement, and whether
the response relies on unsupported claims. Do not average artifact and learning
value into one product score. Incomplete cases remain visible in the report and
are not treated as zero-quality completed responses.
