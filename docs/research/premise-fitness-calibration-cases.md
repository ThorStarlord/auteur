# Premise Fitness Calibration Cases

**Status:** documentation-only calibration evidence  
**Scope:** qualitative stress test of the Premise Fitness vocabulary  
**Authority:** noncanonical; not a runtime benchmark and not audience evidence

## Purpose

This document tests whether the Premise Fitness vocabulary produces useful,
nontrivial distinctions across deliberately different narrative jobs.

It does **not** attempt to prove that any premise is artistically good, popular,
or commercially viable.

The calibration question is:

> Does the model distinguish premise affordance, scope fit, efficiency, runway,
> renewability, expansion, and genre posture without collapsing them into one
> quality score?

The cases are synthetic craft examples. Their value is conceptual
discrimination, not empirical audience validation.

---

## 1. Calibration decisions

The following decisions are selected from the calibration exercise.

### 1.1 Core premise and target environment are separate

Use:

    CORE PREMISE
    the generative dramatic proposition

    TARGET ENVIRONMENT
    genre / genre mix
    target experience
    narrative horizon
    architecture complexity
    genre posture
    audience intent

    WORKING PREMISE
    core premise + currently declared constraints

Premise Fitness evaluates:

    core premise
    x target environment

It should not force genre, word count, market intent, and every design constraint
into one premise sentence.

### 1.2 Unknown scope produces conditional analysis

If the author has not selected a horizon, do not invent one.

Instead compare plausible environments such as:

    short form
    novel
    multi-book / series

and show how fitness changes.

### 1.3 Runway anatomy has four useful shapes

**Finite engine**  
The governing problem naturally terminates after one concentrated resolution.

**Renewable engine**  
Resolving one instance creates or permits another meaningful instance of the
same underlying story system.

**Expanding engine**  
Resolution opens a deeper/larger domain while retaining the premise identity.

**Multi-domain engine**  
Several interacting domains can independently continue producing consequences,
for example politics + relationships + progression + exploration.

These are descriptive patterns, not a rank order.

### 1.4 Runway, renewability, and expansion capacity remain distinct

- **Runway:** how much meaningful story the premise can sustain.
- **Renewability:** whether resolving pressure naturally creates or permits new
  pressure through the existing engine.
- **Expansion capacity:** whether the story can enter new scales/domains while
  remaining recognizably the same narrative proposition.

A procedural can have high renewability but low expansion capacity. A conspiracy
thriller can have finite renewability but high expansion capacity.

### 1.5 Exact word count is secondary to narrative horizon

Prefer coarse horizon classes first:

    short
    novella
    standard novel
    long novel
    multi-book
    long-running series

Exact word count may be an additional constraint, but Premise Fitness should not
pretend to derive an ideal number from the premise alone.

### 1.6 Multi-genre hierarchy is declared when known, explored when unknown

If the author declares the hierarchy, evaluate it.

If not, Premise Fitness may identify hierarchy ambiguity, but materially
different primary-engine arrangements belong in Story Discovery as distinct
story architectures.

### 1.7 Deconstruction needs genre legibility, not a universal retention threshold

A deconstructive story needs enough of the genre relationship to make the target
of deconstruction legible, but there is no universal percentage or checklist.

Genre Pack rule strength can help distinguish:

    genre-defining promise
    strong default
    common pattern
    optional technique
    intentional subversion point

Deliberate violation of defaults is not automatically genre failure.

### 1.8 No Genre Pack means lower-specificity reasoning, not refusal

When a relevant Genre Pack exists:

    use explicit pack knowledge

When no pack exists:

    use bounded generic craft reasoning
    + mark genre-specific claims as heuristic / incomplete

Unknown or unsupported genre knowledge must not be fabricated.

### 1.9 Market demand remains external to Premise Fitness

Premise Fitness asks whether a premise can perform its intended narrative job.

Current market demand, popularity, and audience breadth are separate evidence.
They may influence Story Opportunity Discovery or later product reasoning, but
must not silently become fitness dimensions.

### 1.10 Repair classes stay advisory

When Premise Fitness identifies a mismatch, proposed responses should be grouped
as:

- **scope repair** — keep the premise, change the intended horizon;
- **engine repair** — keep the horizon, strengthen the mechanism that generates
  consequence;
- **complexity repair** — keep premise/horizon, reduce unnecessary activation or
  maintenance burden;
- **hierarchy repair** — change which genre/engine is primary or how secondary
  engines attach.

No repair is automatically applied.

### 1.11 Premise Fitness may eventually be two-pass, but only the upstream pass is selected now

Possible future composition:

    candidate premise
    -> LIGHT PREMISE FITNESS
    -> Story Discovery
    -> candidate architecture
    -> ARCHITECTURE-AWARE FITNESS REASSESSMENT

The second pass is conceptually useful but is not selected for runtime
implementation by this calibration.

### 1.12 Premise and governing engine are not identical

A premise may be evocative but insufficiently causal.

Use a finding such as:

    EVOCATIVE PREMISE
    +
    GOVERNING ENGINE UNESTABLISHED

rather than inventing runway from setting or concept alone.

# 2. Case A — Compact horror short story

## Premise

> Every lie a woman tells appears as a bruise on the person she lied to.

## Target environment

- primary genre: horror / moral supernatural fiction
- horizon: short story
- target experience: escalating guilt, dread, irreversible consequence
- posture: conventional-to-literary
- complexity preference: focused

## Calibration

**Genre efficacy:** supported.  
The supernatural rule directly converts ordinary social behavior into visible
harm.

**Engine strength:** supported.  
Every attempt to conceal, protect, manipulate, or avoid truth can worsen the
central problem.

**Activation:** strong.  
The rule can be demonstrated almost immediately.

**Complexity cost:** low.  
Few entities or world rules are required.

**Runway:** finite.  
The premise is naturally driven toward confession, catastrophic lying, or a
choice about truth.

**Renewability:** low-to-moderate.  
Additional lies create more incidents, but repeated use risks becoming a pattern
rather than deepening consequence.

**Expansion capacity:** low.  
Large-scale institutional expansion is possible, but not necessary to fulfill
the core promise.

**Scope fit:** strong for short form.

## Calibration lesson

Low runway is not a defect when the declared horizon is concentrated.

Trying to "repair" this premise into a ten-book series would likely add machinery
unrelated to the original value.

# 3. Case B — Romance novel

## Premise

> Two rival restoration architects are forced to co-lead the reconstruction of
> a historic theater that both families claim was stolen from them.

## Target environment

- primary genre: romance
- horizon: standard novel
- target experience: rivalry -> admiration -> vulnerability -> commitment
- posture: conventional
- secondary engine: family conflict

## Calibration

**Genre efficacy:** supported.  
The premise naturally creates repeated proximity, conflict, competence
recognition, and emotionally consequential cooperation.

**Engine strength:** supported.  
Professional decisions can repeatedly expose values and force relational
choices.

**Activation:** strong.  
The rivalry and shared project activate immediately.

**Runway:** moderate and appropriate.  
The project supplies escalating stages without requiring indefinite repetition.

**Renewability:** finite.  
Once the relationship and theater conflict resolve, the primary engine should
largely close.

**Expansion capacity:** moderate.  
Family history and ownership can deepen the romance, but a long series would
need a new organizing principle.

**Payoff density:** supported.  
The restoration stages can provide intermediate relational payoffs.

**Scope fit:** strong for one novel.

## Calibration lesson

A finite engine can be ideal for a novel. Long-series renewability would not
automatically improve the romance.

# 4. Case C — Mystery/thriller novel

## Premise

> A forensic linguist receives transcripts of murders twenty-four hours before
> they happen, but each transcript is altered after she prevents an event.

## Target environment

- primary genre: mystery/thriller
- horizon: long novel
- target experience: predictive tension, uncertainty, recontextualization
- posture: conventional with speculative mechanism

## Calibration

**Genre efficacy:** strong.  
The premise naturally creates evidence, hypotheses, anticipation, and revision
of prior interpretations.

**Engine strength:** strong.  
Action changes later evidence, coupling protagonist choice to the mystery.

**Activation:** strong.  
The first transcript can establish the promise quickly.

**Runway:** finite-expanding.  
Several prevented crimes can escalate toward the origin/purpose of the
transcripts.

**Renewability:** moderate.  
Repeated transcript cases work temporarily, but indefinite repetition risks
procedural stasis.

**Expansion capacity:** strong within one book.  
Local crimes can expose a larger system or sender without abandoning the
premise.

**Complexity cost:** moderate.  
Causal bookkeeping becomes important because interventions alter evidence.

**Scope fit:** strong for a novel; uncertain for a long-running series.

## Calibration lesson

High expansion capacity does not imply endless renewability.

# 5. Case D — Progression-fantasy long series

## Premise

> Every ability a courier learns opens routes through previously unreachable
> regions, but using a route permanently changes the political value of the
> places it connects.

## Target environment

- primary genre: progression fantasy
- secondary engine: political adventure
- horizon: long-running series
- target experience: competence growth, discovery, strategic consequence
- posture: conventional
- complexity preference: layered

## Calibration

**Genre efficacy:** strong.  
Advancement visibly changes what the protagonist can do.

**Engine strength:** strong.  
New capability creates new access, and new access changes faction incentives.

**Activation:** moderate.  
The route/ability relationship must be taught but can be demonstrated early.

**Runway:** high.

**Renewability:** high.  
Each new connection changes the political state and creates new route problems.

**Expansion capacity:** high.  
New regions, institutions, route types, and capability domains can widen the
story without abandoning the governing engine.

**Complexity cost:** moderate-to-high.  
Long-term route state and political consequences create continuity burden.

**Marginal story cost:** relatively low after the system is understood.

**Scope fit:** strong for a long series.

## Calibration lesson

High startup/continuity cost can be efficient at series scale when the engine
reuses that investment.

# 6. Case E — Maximalist multi-genre series

## Premise

> A hidden civilization recruits ordinary humans whose memories contain keys to
> abandoned planetary infrastructure; every recovered system changes both the
> balance among rival civilizations and the recruit's relationships on Earth.

## Target environment

- primary engine: civilizational political progression
- secondary engine: relationship drama
- tertiary engine: mystery
- local mode: cosmic horror
- horizon: multi-book series
- target experience: awe, discovery, escalating consequence, attachment
- complexity preference: maximalist

## Calibration

**Genre efficacy:** supported but hierarchy-sensitive.  
The premise can support several genres, but not all should be equal.

**Engine strength:** strong.  
Recovery actions alter power, relationships, and knowledge.

**Activation:** tension.  
Hidden civilizations, memory keys, planetary infrastructure, and Earth
relationships can create front-loaded cognitive burden.

**Runway:** high.

**Renewability:** high.  
Recovered systems create new geopolitical and relational states.

**Expansion capacity:** very high.

**Complexity cost:** high.

**Multi-engine compatibility:** potentially strong.  
The same recovery event can serve progression, politics, mystery, horror, and
relationships if consequences are integrated rather than parallel.

**Scope fit:** strong for a long series; poor for a short story without radical
compression.

## Calibration lesson

Maximalism is not automatically inefficiency. The relevant question is whether
multiple engines share causal events or demand separate story machinery.

# 7. Case F — Deconstructive romance

## Premise

> Two people who are extraordinarily compatible discover that every act of
> increased intimacy makes them less capable of functioning independently, and
> both must decide whether romantic completion is actually the relationship they
> want.

## Target environment

- primary genre relationship: romance
- horizon: novel
- posture: deconstructive
- target experience: attraction, tenderness, unease, examination of romantic
  completion
- complexity preference: focused

## Calibration

**Genre efficacy:** supported for a deconstructive target.  
Attraction, intimacy, vulnerability, and relationship stakes remain central.

**Conventional alignment:** intentionally partial.  
Increasing intimacy is not automatically treated as unambiguous progress.

**Genre legibility:** supported.  
The story must preserve enough romantic expectation for its critique to have an
object.

**Engine strength:** supported.  
Every step toward closeness changes the cost of autonomy.

**Runway:** finite and appropriate.

**Scope fit:** strong for a novel.

**Genre Pack relationship:** useful.  
A Romance Pack can identify expected promises/defaults and distinguish deliberate
subversion from accidental failure.

## Calibration lesson

Genre deviation cannot be evaluated without declared posture. Conventional
noncompliance is not the same as deconstructive unfitness.

# 8. Case G — Excellent hook, insufficient declared runway

## Premise

> For one hour each year, everyone in a city can hear the private thoughts of
> the person they love most.

## Target environment A

- horizon: short story / novella
- genre: relationship drama / speculative fiction

### Result

Strong activation, strong emotional efficacy, finite engine, strong scope fit.

## Target environment B

- horizon: twelve-book continuous series
- same governing mechanism only

### Result

**Scope fit:** tension / unsupported.

The annual event can recur, but repetition alone does not establish meaningful
renewability.

A long series would need additional causal machinery such as:

- changed social institutions;
- evolving legal/political consequences;
- persistent relationship-state transformations;
- increasingly different uses of the phenomenon.

## Calibration lesson

Repetition is not renewability.

This is a canonical example of:

    strong premise / wrong declared scope

rather than "bad premise."

# 9. Case H — Enormous runway, poor activation

## Premise

> Seven interdependent empires administer different physical laws, and a
> diplomat's marriage treaty can redistribute which laws apply in contested
> territories.

## Target environment

- primary genre: political speculative epic
- horizon: multi-book
- posture: conventional
- complexity preference: layered rather than maximalist
- audience intent: relatively broad entry despite deep worldbuilding

## Calibration

**Engine strength:** potentially strong.

**Runway:** high.

**Renewability:** high.

**Expansion capacity:** very high.

**Activation:** major tension.  
The audience may need to understand empires, physical-law jurisdictions,
marriage politics, and contested territories before appreciating the hook.

**Entity cost:** high.

**Causal complexity cost:** high.

**Scope fit:** potentially strong for a series.

**Audience-entry fit:** tension relative to the declared broad-entry target.

## Candidate repairs

**Complexity repair:** start with one treaty and two law systems, reveal the rest
progressively.

**Hierarchy repair:** keep political marriage primary; defer cosmological detail
until it changes a visible choice.

**Scope repair:** not necessary unless the author wants a much shorter work.

## Calibration lesson

High runway does not compensate automatically for excessive activation cost.

# 10. Case I — Evocative world premise, governing engine unestablished

## Premise

> A magical university exists beneath Sao Paulo.

## Target environment

- horizon: five-book fantasy series
- genre: fantasy
- target experience: discovery, belonging, escalating competence

## Calibration

**Evocative premise:** supported.

**Genre atmosphere:** plausible.

**Governing engine:** unestablished.

The statement supplies a setting proposition but does not yet establish:

- what the protagonist repeatedly does;
- what resists them;
- what choices alter state;
- what creates escalation;
- why Book 2 follows from Book 1.

Premise Fitness should therefore refuse to invent high runway from the existence
of a large setting.

A more causal version might be:

> A scholarship student must earn magical citizenship beneath Sao Paulo while
> every exam permanently transfers one of her memories to the rival assigned to
> grade her.

Now there is a recurring action/cost/relationship engine to evaluate.

## Calibration lesson

World size is not narrative runway.

# 11. Case J — Unknown scope

## Premise

> A girl discovers that every dream she forgets becomes a real person.

## Declared information

- genre: speculative fantasy
- target experience: wonder, grief, identity uncertainty
- horizon: unknown

## Conditional analysis

### Short form

Supported.

One forgotten dream-person can force a concentrated identity/emotional choice.

### Novel

Supported if several dream-people create cumulative consequences and the
protagonist's forgetting becomes an active pressure rather than a one-time
reveal.

### Long series

Unestablished.

A series would need renewable or expanding machinery beyond repeatedly spawning
new dream-people.

Possible series-strengthening mechanisms include:

- dream-people form institutions;
- forgetting alters reality itself;
- the protagonist gains choices over what can be remembered;
- other dreamers create competing populations.

## Calibration lesson

When horizon is unknown, Premise Fitness should show conditional fit instead of
choosing a scope for the author.

# 12. Cross-case findings

The calibration cases support the current conceptual model.

### Finding 1 — Scope changes the judgment materially

The same premise can be highly fit for one horizon and poorly fit for another.

### Finding 2 — Runway is not enough

Cases C and H show that expansion/runway can be high while activation or
complexity remains problematic.

### Finding 3 — Repetition is not renewability

Case G demonstrates that recurring events do not automatically create meaningful
new state.

### Finding 4 — Multi-engine stories need causal integration

Case E works best when one event advances several engines.

Parallel unrelated subplots would increase cost without equivalent value.

### Finding 5 — Deconstruction requires declared posture

Case F would look conventionally noncompliant if posture were ignored.

### Finding 6 — Setting scale is not engine strength

Case I shows why Premise Fitness must distinguish evocative concept from causal
engine.

### Finding 7 — Genre Pack absence should lower specificity, not block analysis

Most dimensions remain analyzable through generic craft reasoning, while
genre-specific claims should become more tentative.

---

# 13. Calibration result

**Disposition:** REAFFIRM with terminology refinement.

The cases do not expose a need for a numeric score, canonical Premise Fitness
artifact, or runtime implementation.

They do justify these durable refinements:

- separate core premise from target environment;
- conditional analysis when scope is unknown;
- four runway shapes: finite, renewable, expanding, multi-domain;
- explicit distinction among runway, renewability, and expansion capacity;
- horizon bands before exact word count;
- no-Pack heuristic fallback;
- repair classes;
- premise-vs-engine distinction;
- future two-pass analysis remains optional/deferred.

No further conceptual expansion is selected by this calibration.

The next evidence question is practical usefulness:

> Does repeated real or agent-assisted use reveal that this documented method
> needs durable runtime support?

Until that need appears, the documentation is sufficient.
