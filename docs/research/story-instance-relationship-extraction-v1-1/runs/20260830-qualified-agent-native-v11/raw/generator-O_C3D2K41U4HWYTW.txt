I recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

The **monastery testimony preserved** can return lived memory to public attention without exposing the originals that sustain the **treaty-protected archive** as the only evidentiary chain. Authentication also connects the testimony to the established conflict: the **founding record was forged**, while the **council retracted its admission that the archive was falsified**. This directly serves the series commitment to contested history.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Verification postpones public release, reducing immediate pressure on the council. That cost is preferable to revealing the strongest archive records through a protected hearing and risking the evidentiary chain Book 3 sought to preserve.

I excluded the **repaired archive lantern** because its condition does not determine how testimony can safely enter public memory. I also excluded new claims about who authenticates the testimony, how publication works, or how the public responds, since those facts have not been accepted.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation. It establishes no Book Direction or Canonical State.
