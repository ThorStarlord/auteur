I recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

It best serves the Series commitment to contested history: the monastery testimony returns lived memory to public view while the protected archive preserves the original evidentiary chain. This matters because the founding record was forged, the monastery testimony is preserved, the evidence identifies the falsifier, and the council retracted its admission that the archive was falsified. Publication can challenge that retraction without exposing the archive’s strongest original records at a hearing.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Authentication postpones public release, giving the council’s retracted position more time to stand uncontested. In return, Book 4 avoids risking the treaty protections that make the archive the only evidentiary chain.

I excluded the lantern’s repaired condition because it does not determine how testimony can safely re-enter public memory. I also excluded chapter-level execution, scene design, and any new political actors or authentication methods because none are established by the accepted facts.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
