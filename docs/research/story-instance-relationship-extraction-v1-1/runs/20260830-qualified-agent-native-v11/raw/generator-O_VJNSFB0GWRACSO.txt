Recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

This option best fits the planning intent because monastery testimony can re-enter public memory through authenticated publication while the treaty-protected archive keeps the originals secure. It preserves the archive as the only evidentiary chain, respects Book 3 Direction to protect the archive after the council retracted its admission, and advances the Series commitment to expose conflict between official history and lived memory. That conflict remains active because the founding record was forged, monastery testimony was preserved, and the council admission was retracted.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Authentication postpones public release and gives the retracted official account more time to stand uncontested. Even so, that cost is safer than a stage-protected-hearing, which would create immediate pressure by revealing the strongest archive records and could weaken the protection the treaty was meant to secure.

I deliberately excluded the archive lantern being broken and repaired because its condition does not affect how testimony should enter public memory. I also excluded the evidence identifying the falsifier because it supports the larger historical case but does not decide between publication and a protected hearing.

This recommendation is non-authoritative. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
