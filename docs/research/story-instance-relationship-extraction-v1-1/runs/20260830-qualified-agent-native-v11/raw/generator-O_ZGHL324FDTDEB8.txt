Recommend `publish-verified-testimony`.

The Monastery Testimony is preserved, so Book 4 can authenticate and publish it while the Treaty-Protected Archive retains the originals. This restores lived memory to public circulation without disturbing the Archive’s role as the Only Evidentiary Chain. It also follows Book 3 Direction to protect the Archive after the Council Retracted Admission. Because the Archive Evidence Identifies the Falsifier and the Founding Record Was Forged, exposing selected high-value records through a protected hearing carries the greater risk.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Verification postpones public release while the Council’s Retracted Admission remains the official position.

I deliberately excluded the Repaired Archive Lantern because its condition does not decide how testimony should enter public memory. I also excluded unaccepted details about witnesses, authentication procedures, public reaction, or council countermeasures.

This recommendation is non-authoritative. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
