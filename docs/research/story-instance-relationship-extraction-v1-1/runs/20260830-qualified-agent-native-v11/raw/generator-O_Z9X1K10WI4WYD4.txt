Recommend **publish-witness-account**.

The **monastery testimony** preserves an account independent of the council. After the **public admission** and **council retraction**, forcing a hearing would return control of the response to the institution that already reversed its position. Publishing the witness’s account creates a record the council cannot retract while preserving the witness as the source of testimony, rather than using the **named falsifier** to make the council the final arbiter.

The principal tradeoff is exposure to retaliation. The witness’s authority is preserved publicly, but their safety may be weakened.

I excluded the **broken lantern** because it does not bear on who should control the response to the retraction. I also excluded new claims about the witness’s identity, motives, consent, or protection because none are accepted facts.

This recommendation is non-authoritative. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
