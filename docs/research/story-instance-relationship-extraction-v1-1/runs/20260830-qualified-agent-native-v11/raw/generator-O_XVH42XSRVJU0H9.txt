I recommend **publish-witness-account**.

The **council's retracted admission** shows that council-controlled acknowledgment is reversible. A **public witness account** would create an independent record whose authority comes from the witness, not from council approval. This also fits the established **monastery testimony**, while the **named falsifier** gives the account concrete relevance without requiring the council to validate it.

The principal tradeoff is exposure. Publication preserves the witness's authority over the truth, but increases the risk of retaliation against the witness. By contrast, **force-council-hearing** makes accountability explicit but leaves the council controlling the forum and timing, repeating the same institutional dependence that allowed the admission to be retracted.

I excluded the **broken lantern** because it does not affect this decision. I also excluded unsupported claims about the witness's identity, the falsifier's motives, the council's reasons for retracting, and the form retaliation might take.

This recommendation is non-authoritative. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
