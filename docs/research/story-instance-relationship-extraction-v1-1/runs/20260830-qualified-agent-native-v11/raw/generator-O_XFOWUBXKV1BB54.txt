Recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

Authentication and controlled publication restore the **preserved monastery testimony** to public memory while respecting the **treaty-protected archive** as the only evidentiary chain. This directly serves the series commitment to **contested history**: the testimony can challenge the **forged founding record** and the council’s **retracted admission** without destroying the evidence needed to substantiate that challenge. It also continues Book 3’s Direction to **protect the archive after the retraction**.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Verification postpones public release and may reduce its immediate impact, but preserves credibility and future proof. By contrast, **burn-archive** would make the testimony unavoidable only by destroying the evidentiary chain that can authenticate it, contradicting the current planning intent.

Deliberately excluded as irrelevant: the **repaired archive lantern**, because its condition does not determine how testimony can be authenticated or published without breaching archive protection.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation; it establishes no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
