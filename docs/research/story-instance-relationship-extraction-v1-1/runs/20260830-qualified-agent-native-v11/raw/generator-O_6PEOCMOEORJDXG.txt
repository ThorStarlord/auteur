Recommend **publish-witness-account**.

The **monastery preserves a testimony**, while the **council publicly admitted the archive record was falsified and later retracted that admission**. Because the **evidence identifies the person who falsified the record**, submitting the matter to a council-controlled hearing would let the council control timing and forum after it has already reversed its public position. An independent public record preserves the witness’s authority outside that unstable process.

The principal tradeoff is exposure: publication protects the testimony’s authority but exposes the witness to retaliation.

I excluded the **lantern broken during the archive search** because it does not affect who controls the witness’s account or how Book 3 should answer the retraction. I also excluded any specific publication method, retaliation, or protection plan because none is established.

This recommendation is non-authoritative. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
