Recommend **publish-witness-account**.

The council publicly admitted the archive record was falsified and later retracted that admission. Because the founding record was forged, the falsifier was named, and the monastery preserves a testimony, Book 3 should establish the witness’s account as an independent public record. This preserves the witness’s authority instead of placing it inside a hearing whose forum and timing the council controls.

The principal tradeoff is exposure: publication protects the testimony’s independence but may expose the witness to retaliation.

I deliberately excluded the broken lantern because it does not affect how Book 3 should answer the retraction or preserve witness authority. I also excluded any assumptions about the witness’s identity, motives, safety arrangements, or the council’s next actions because none are accepted facts.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation; it creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
