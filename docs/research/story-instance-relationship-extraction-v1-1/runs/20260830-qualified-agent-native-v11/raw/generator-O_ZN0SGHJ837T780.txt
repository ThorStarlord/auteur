Recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

The monastery testimony should be authenticated and published while the originals remain secured within the treaty-protected archive. This restores lived memory to public view without weakening the only evidentiary chain supporting it. That matters because **monastery testimony preserved**, **archive protection treaty protected**, and **treaty protects archive as only evidentiary chain**. It also answers the renewed danger created when **council archive position retracted admission**, while advancing the series commitment to **contested history**.

The principal tradeoff is delay: verification postpones public release and gives the retracted official history more time to hold ground. In return, Book 4 avoids exposing the archive’s strongest records or creating an opening to challenge their custody.

I deliberately excluded the **repaired archive lantern**, because its condition does not determine how testimony can safely re-enter public memory. I also excluded renewed proof of the forgery or falsifier: **founding record forged** and **evidence identifies the falsifier** are already accepted facts, while the present choice concerns publication without breaking the evidentiary chain.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation only; it establishes no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
