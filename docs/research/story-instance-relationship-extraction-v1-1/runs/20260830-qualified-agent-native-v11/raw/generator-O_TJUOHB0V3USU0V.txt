Recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

The **monastery testimony is preserved**, so Book 4 can authenticate and publish it without altering the **treaty-protected archive**. This keeps the testimony connected to the only evidentiary chain while renewing the series commitment to **contested history**, especially after the **council retracted its admission** that the **founding record was forged**.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Verification postpones public release and gives the retracted official account more time to hold ground. Burning the archive would create urgency, but it would destroy the evidence needed to prove the testimony rather than merely assert it.

I excluded the **repaired archive lantern** and the details of its earlier damage because its present condition does not affect testimony authentication, publication, or preservation of the evidentiary chain.

This recommendation is advisory only. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
