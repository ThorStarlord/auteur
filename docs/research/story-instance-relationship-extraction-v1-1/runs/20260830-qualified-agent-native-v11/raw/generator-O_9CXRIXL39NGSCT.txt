Recommend **publish-witness-account**.

The council publicly admitted the archive record was falsified and later retracted that admission. A council-controlled hearing would therefore place the response inside a forum whose authority has already proved reversible. Publishing the testimony preserved by the monastery creates an independent public record that the council cannot retract. It also keeps the witness's account central, supported by the accepted facts that the founding record was forged and the falsifier was named.

The principal tradeoff is exposure. Publication protects the witness's authority over the testimony but may expose the witness to retaliation.

I deliberately excluded the broken lantern because it does not affect this choice. I also excluded the identity, methods, and likely retaliation of any party because those facts are not accepted history.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
