I recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

The **monastery testimony is preserved**, so Book 4 can authenticate copies and prepare them for publication without removing or altering originals. The **archive is treaty protected** and remains the **only evidentiary chain**, which makes preserving its custody more important than producing immediate public pressure. Publication also answers the series commitment to **contested history** by putting lived memory against the **council’s retracted admission** and the **forged founding record**.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Authentication postpones the public confrontation and gives the council time to shape its response. In return, Book 4 avoids exposing the archive’s strongest records or weakening the treaty-protected chain.

I excluded the **repaired archive lantern** because its condition does not affect authentication, publication, or evidentiary custody. I also excluded chapter-level staging and prose choices because the question concerns whole-book direction.

This recommendation is advisory only. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
