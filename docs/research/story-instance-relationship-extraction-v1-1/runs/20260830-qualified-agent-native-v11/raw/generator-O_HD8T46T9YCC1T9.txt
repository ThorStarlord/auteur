Recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

It best serves the Series commitment to contested history by returning lived memory to public view while preserving the protected originals. Monastery testimony is preserved, archive protection is treaty protected, and the treaty protects the archive as the only evidentiary chain. Authentication followed by publication lets the testimony challenge the council’s retracted admission without exposing or disturbing the archive’s strongest original records.

The principal tradeoff is delay: verification postpones public pressure and may give the council’s retracted position more time to hold.

I deliberately excluded the repaired archive lantern and the identity of the falsifier because neither determines how testimony can re-enter public memory without damaging provenance. I also excluded chapter-level staging and prose execution as beyond this Book-level decision.

This recommendation is non-authoritative and creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
