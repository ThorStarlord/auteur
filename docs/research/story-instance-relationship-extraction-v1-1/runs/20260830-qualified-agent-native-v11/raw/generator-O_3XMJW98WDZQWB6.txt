Recommend **publish-verified-testimony**.

It best fits the Series commitment to contested history by returning lived memory to public view while preserving the official record it contests. The monastery testimony is preserved, the council retracted its admission that the archive was falsified, and the treaty protects the archive as the only evidentiary chain. Authenticating the testimony before publication lets the protected archive retain the originals and avoids exposing selected records through a hearing.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Verification postpones public release and immediate pressure on the council, but it does not risk the evidentiary chain that Book 3 Direction sought to protect.

I excluded the repaired lantern because its condition does not affect testimony authentication or archive custody. I also excluded new revelations, political actors, and testimony contents because none are accepted facts.

This recommendation is non-authoritative. It creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
