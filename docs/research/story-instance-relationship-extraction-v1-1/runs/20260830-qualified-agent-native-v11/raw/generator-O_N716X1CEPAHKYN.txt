Recommendation: **publish-verified-testimony**.

Authenticate the preserved **monastery testimony** against the treaty-protected archive, then publish it without altering or removing the archive. This restores lived memory to public view while preserving the archive as the **only evidentiary chain**. It also serves the Series commitment to **contested history**: the testimony can publicly challenge the **council’s retracted admission** and the **forged founding record**, with the underlying evidence remaining available for scrutiny.

The principal tradeoff is delay. Verification postpones publication and gives the official account more time to dominate, but destroying the archive would permanently sacrifice the evidence needed to substantiate the testimony. Therefore, **burn-archive** is incompatible with the stated intent to avoid breaking the protected archive.

Deliberately excluded as irrelevant: the **archive lantern’s repaired condition** and the circumstances of its earlier breakage. They do not determine how testimony should re-enter public memory while preserving the evidentiary chain.

This is a non-authoritative recommendation only; it creates no canon, Book Direction, or Canonical State.
