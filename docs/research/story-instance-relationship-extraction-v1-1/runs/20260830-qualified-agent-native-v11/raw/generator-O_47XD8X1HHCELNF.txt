Recommend **publish-witness-account**.

The council has already **publicly admitted the archive record was falsified** and then **retracted that admission**. Because **archive.founding_record=forged**, **archive.falsifier=named**, and **the monastery preserves a testimony**, an independent witness account creates a durable public record whose authority does not depend on the council’s shifting position. By contrast, **force-council-hearing** would place the response inside a forum whose timing and legitimacy the retracting council controls.

The principal tradeoff is exposure: publication best preserves the witness’s independent authority, but risks retaliation against the witness.

Deliberately excluded as irrelevant: **a lantern was broken during the archive search**. It does not materially affect this choice. I also exclude any assumptions about the witness’s identity, motives, safety arrangements, or the council’s reasons for retracting, because none are accepted facts.
