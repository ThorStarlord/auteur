**Recommendation: witness-account**

**Why:** The Series pressure ("every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness") and the active commitment contested-history ("every Book must expose conflict between official history and lived memory") both require lived memory to be an active, embodied presence in Book 2, not just an institutional backdrop. The current fact founding-record (forged) already establishes the fraud institutionally; centering witness-account is what actually activates contested-history's demand for conflict between official history and lived memory, rather than restating the institutional side of that conflict a second time. It also keeps faith with the still-unresolved commitment commitment-falsifier by putting a person — the witness — at risk in a way that sets up future stakes for identifying who falsified the record.

**Principal tradeoff:** witness-account exposes the witness early, accelerating the danger implied by the Series pressure (correcting the record invites erasure) — a real cost, but one that is thematically load-bearing rather than accidental, since it dramatizes the Series open question ("can truth survive without becoming instrument of power?") through a person, not an institution.

**Deliberately excluded:** monastery-testimony and broken-lantern are dormant and not referenced by this planning intent, so they carry no weight here. burn-archive and ally-militia are proposed but not accepted and are excluded entirely. cover-up-trace is a viable alternative but was not preferred, since it delays the lived-memory witness that contested-history requires to be active now, and keeps the conflict institutional rather than personal.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create Book Direction, does not resolve commitment-falsifier, and does not modify Canonical State.
