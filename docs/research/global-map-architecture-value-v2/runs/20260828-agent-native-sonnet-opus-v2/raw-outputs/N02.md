Recommendation: publish-witness-account

Why: The series commitment "contested-history" requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — a hearing that the council controls the forum/timing of risks becoming another site of retraction, echoing the pattern already established when the council admitted the archive record was falsified and then retracted that admission. Since the council has already shown it will retract an admission once made, force-council-hearing places the witness's authority back inside the same institutional channel that already failed to hold — the council's public admission. An independent public record for the witness, drawing on the monastery's preserved testimony and the fact that the falsifier is already named and the founding record's forgery is established, secures the witness's authority outside the council's reach, consistent with the series open question of whether truth can survive without becoming an instrument of power (an independent record does not depend on the council's continued cooperation to remain true).

Principal tradeoff: Publishing the witness's account protects their authority independent of the council, but exposes the witness to retaliation — a real and unresolved risk given the series pressure that "every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness."

Excluded as not relevant: The broken lantern from Book 1 is an accepted fact but has no bearing on this response choice and was not used. I also did not treat the council's earlier admission as still standing — the current state is explicitly the retraction, not the admission — and did not assume any resolution to the series open question, since that remains open by design.

This is a non-authoritative planning recommendation; it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
