Recommendation: **publish-verified-testimony**

Why: The active constraint at this horizon is the grouped pressure cluster combining monastery testimony (preserved) with archive-protected (treaty protected). Because archive-protected is a current, active constraint — and is explicitly incompatible with anything that would compromise the archive (as with the excluded burn-archive proposal) — any option must keep the evidentiary chain fully intact, not merely "protected in principle." Publish-verified-testimony authenticates and releases the testimony while leaving the original evidence secured under the treaty's protection, directly satisfying the planning intent to return the testimony to public memory without breaking the archive. This also serves the interpretive thread that future payoff depends on preserving the evidentiary chain to authenticate the testimony — though that thread is context, not a deciding constraint on its own.

Principal tradeoff: Preserving the chain requires delaying release until verification is complete — public memory is served more slowly, but without exposure risk to the protected archive.

Why not stage-protected-hearing: presenting testimony beside selected archive evidence reveals which records carry the strongest evidence. That selective exposure sits in tension with treaty protection as a current constraint — it trades chain integrity for immediacy, which runs counter to the standing pressure that any public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness. It is not necessarily excluded as a matter of strict incompatibility (unlike burn-archive, which is a hard-excluded proposal), but it carries materially more risk against the active commitment and is not the recommended choice here.

Deliberately excluded as not relevant: founding-record (dormant, grouped supporting history only); admission-retracted (dormant, grouped causal history explaining archive-protected, not a separate active peer); public-admission (superseded); both lantern states (neither supports current continuity or the decision, including the recent-but-irrelevant repaired-lantern); commitment-falsifier (resolved history); burn-archive and ally-militia (never accepted, excluded from the Decision Map entirely).
