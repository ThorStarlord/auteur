**Recommendation: force-council-hearing**

**Why:** The active constraint carried into Book 3 is contested-history — the ongoing struggle over who controls the historical record, not merely whether the witness has a personal platform. The current fact is admission-retracted: the council already made a public admission (public-admission) and then walked it back, making the council's own credibility and process the live issue. The named-falsifier commitment was already resolved in Book 2 — that work exists and is available to use, not to re-litigate. Compelling the council to answer publicly, using the already-established named falsifier, directly presses on the contested-history pressure (visibility forces accountability, which risks provoking further erasure) and treats the retraction as what it is: a council credibility failure to be exposed on the record, rather than a gap to be filled by a separate witness channel.

**Principal tradeoff:** Keeps accountability centered on the council and its retraction, but cedes control of forum and timing to the council — they can stall, control framing, or manage the hearing's terms.

**Excluded as not relevant:** founding-record and monastery-testimony (dormant, not referenced by current Book 3 intent); broken-lantern (dormant/irrelevant); ST-P1/ST-P2 (unaccepted, cannot be treated as canon); public-admission as a current state (it is superseded history, not the active fact — admission-retracted is current). publish-witness-account was considered but not preferred here because it sidesteps the council rather than confronting the retraction, and introduces retaliation exposure without directly engaging the pressure that's actually active.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
