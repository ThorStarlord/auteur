**Recommendation: witness-account**

**Why:** The active constraint contested-history requires every Book to expose a consequential conflict between official history and lived memory, and commitment-falsifier requires the founding-record falsifier to be identified — both carried forward as accepted from Book 1 direction history. The current planning intent is to make the forged founding-record fact (founding-record, forged) matter to lived memory specifically, not merely to institutional record-keeping. witness-account puts the living witness's account in direct conflict with the forged record, which most directly satisfies the lived-memory side of contested-history and keeps the series pressure (exposure risk to hidden archivists) active and consequential.

**Principal tradeoff:** Centering the witness advances lived memory now but exposes the witness early, raising the stakes named in the series pressure — a public correction (the witness's account surfacing) gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness.

**What I excluded:** cover-up-trace, because while it could still serve commitment-falsifier (institutional trace could lead to the falsifier), it delays the lived-memory witness and so is weaker against contested-history's explicit lived-memory requirement given the current planning intent. I did not rely on monastery-testimony or broken-lantern — both are dormant and not referenced by Book 2 planning, so they carry no weight here. I have not treated this choice as canon; it creates no Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
