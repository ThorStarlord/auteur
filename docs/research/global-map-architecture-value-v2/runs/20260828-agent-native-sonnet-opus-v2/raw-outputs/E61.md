Recommendation: force-council-hearing

Why: The series commitment `contested-history` requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — a compelled public hearing keeps that conflict visible and active, whereas publishing the witness's account independently risks resolving/sidelining it rather than exposing it further. The named falsifier (Book 2 Realization) is the concrete lever making a hearing possible now — that fact only exists because of Book 2's identification work, so using it continues the accountability arc already established through the council's admission and retraction (Book 2 Realizations). It also keeps pressure aimed at the council itself, consistent with the series pressure that public correction provokes hidden archivists to erase witnesses — a hearing forces the council's own agents into exposure rather than pushing exposure risk onto the witness directly.

Principal tradeoff: The council controls the forum and timing of the hearing, so accountability stays central but on the council's terms — it can stage, delay, or limit the hearing's scope, unlike publish-witness-account, which secures irretractable authority for the witness but does so by directly exposing the witness to the retaliation the series pressure already anticipates.

Excluded: The lantern broken during the archive search, the monastery's preservation of testimony, and the forged founding record itself were not treated as relevant — they establish the archive's material history but bear on evidentiary provenance, not on how to respond to the retraction or protect witness authority, which is what this decision turns on.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
