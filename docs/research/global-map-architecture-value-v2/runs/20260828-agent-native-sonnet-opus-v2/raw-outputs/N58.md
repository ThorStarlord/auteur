**Recommendation: witness-account**

**Why:** The series commitment `contested-history` requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — witness-account is the only option that directly stages that conflict, since it centers the living witness's testimony against the forged record. The Book 1 realization that the monastery preserves a testimony gives this option a concrete, already-established resource to draw on, and the current state that the founding record is forged gives the witness's account something specific to contest. This also serves the series commitment `commitment-falsifier`: putting the witness's account in direct tension with the forged record is what will surface who falsified it, whereas tracing the cover-up's institutional trail is a slower, more indirect route to that identification.

**Principal tradeoff:** Centering the witness advances lived memory and the falsifier question fastest, but it exposes the witness early — which runs against the Book 1 Direction's established resistance (custodians erase witnesses) and stakes (publishing too soon destroys witnesses). Choosing this option means Book 2 must reckon with that exposure risk rather than deferring it.

**What I excluded as not relevant:** The archive lantern's broken condition — no accepted fact ties it to either option's tradeoff, so I did not treat it as bearing on this choice. I also did not treat the series open question ("can truth survive without becoming instrument of power") as deciding between the options, since neither option's stated tradeoff engages it directly; it's a thematic frame, not a fact this choice turns on.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
