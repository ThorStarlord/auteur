Recommendation: **witness-account**

Why: The active commitment `contested-history` requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — `witness-account` directly satisfies this by centering lived memory against the forged record. It also builds on `founding-record` (forged, active), the current constraint the planning intent explicitly targets, and sets up eventual resolution of `commitment-falsifier` (the still-unresolved requirement to identify who falsified the record) by putting a witness on record who can be set against institutional denial. `cover-up-trace` keeps the institutional history central and delays the lived-memory witness, which sits against the grain of `contested-history` rather than fulfilling it at this horizon.

Principal tradeoff: Centering the witness's account surfaces lived memory as required, but exposes the witness early — raising their narrative and story risk before the falsifier is identified, which may complicate how `commitment-falsifier` gets resolved later.

Deliberately excluded: `monastery-testimony` (dormant, not referenced by this planning intent) and `broken-lantern` (dormant/irrelevant, never supports active continuity) — neither is a current constraint. `burn-archive` and `ally-militia` are excluded as proposed-not-accepted and were not treated as available options.
