Recommendation: publish-verified-testimony.

Why: The active commitment requiring every Book to expose a consequential conflict between official history and lived memory (grouped under contested-history, carried by accepted Book 1 and Book 3 directions) is served either way, but the controlling constraint is that archive.protection is treaty protected — an active fact, not dormant or superseded — and monastery.testimony is preserved and reactivated for Book 4. Publish-verified-testimony keeps the protected archive's evidentiary chain fully intact while still returning the testimony to public memory, honoring both the treaty protection and the preservation of the testimony without forcing a conflict between them.

Principal tradeoff: it preserves the evidentiary chain but delays public release until verification is complete, which sits in tension with the series pressure that every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness — a slower, verified release reduces exposure risk during the delay window compared to an immediate hearing.

Excluded: stage-protected-hearing, because presenting testimony beside selected archive evidence risks revealing which records carry the strongest evidence, directly threatening the treaty-protected status of archive.protection (active) even though staged under the treaty's nominal protections — the exposure of relative evidentiary strength is a chain-integrity risk the active protection constraint doesn't clearly permit.

I also deliberately excluded from consideration: the falsifier's identity (resolved, historical continuity only), archive.founding_record being forged (dormant, not referenced by Book 4 planning), archive.falsifier being named (dormant), the superseded facts (broken-lantern, superseded by repaired-lantern; public-admission, superseded by admission-retracted), and council.archive_position/retracted admission and archive_lantern.condition/repaired (both dormant/irrelevant per the map) — none of these bear on the specific tension between testimony reactivation and archive protection that this decision turns on.

This is a non-authoritative planning recommendation only; it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
