**Recommendation: force-council-hearing**

**Why:** The active constraint is contested-history, carried forward from accepted Book 1–2 direction — the series pressure ("every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness") means any response that pushes the truth further into public, uncontrolled visibility raises witness-erasure risk. The current fact is admission-retracted (not public-admission, which is superseded) — the council has already shown it can walk back a public admission, meaning the fight is not about getting information out but about compelling accountability that can't be quietly retracted. The resolved commitment-falsifier thread (closed by named-falsifier in Book 2) gives Book 3 a concrete, already-established instrument — the named falsifier — to compel the council to answer, rather than needing a new mechanism. This keeps the series' central tension (accountability vs. erasure) live and lets the open question ("can truth survive without becoming instrument of power?") stay in play, since forcing a hearing tests whether power can be made to answer rather than sidestepping power via unilateral publication.

**Principal tradeoff:** The council controls the forum and timing of the hearing, so the witness's authority is asserted through a process the antagonist party still partially governs — accountability is centered, but not fully secured on the witness's own terms.

**Excluded as not relevant:**
- founding-record, monastery-testimony, broken-lantern — all dormant/irrelevant to Book 3 intent, not cited.
- public-admission — accepted history but superseded by the current retraction; not the operative fact.
- ST-P1, ST-P2 — unaccepted, not treated as canon or considered.
- publish-witness-account was weighed but not recommended: it protects authority more directly but exposes the witness to retaliation, which directly triggers the series pressure clause about giving hidden archivists reason to erase another witness — a risk force-council-hearing manages better given the already-resolved named-falsifier commitment.

This is a planning recommendation only; it does not constitute Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
