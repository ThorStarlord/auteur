**Recommendation: force-council-hearing**

**Why:** The active current fact is the council's retracted admission — the council previously made a public admission of fraud (public-admission), but that has been superseded by their current stance of retraction (admission-retracted), which is what Book 3 must respond to. This retraction sits within the still-active commitment established across Book 1 and Book 2: contested-history — the pattern of history being actively disputed rather than settled. Critically, the commitment-falsifier question is already resolved (by named-falsifier in Book 2) — the identity of who falsified the record is settled, closed history, not something Book 3 needs to relitigate. That resolution is exactly what makes force-council-hearing viable now: the named falsifier already exists as leverage to compel the council into a public forum, directly answering the retraction with accountability the council cannot simply wave away privately.

**Principal tradeoff:** force-council-hearing keeps accountability and the contest over truth centered on the council's own public record — consistent with contested-history as an ongoing pattern — but the council retains control over the forum and timing of that hearing, meaning they could shape or blunt the confrontation on their own terms.

**Excluded:** publish-witness-account is not recommended as the primary path — it bypasses the council's retraction rather than confronting it, and its tradeoff (exposing the witness to retaliation) sits poorly against the series pressure that public correction invites erasure of witnesses. I deliberately excluded dormant/irrelevant material: founding-record, monastery-testimony, and broken-lantern, none of which are referenced by current Book 3 intent. I also excluded the unaccepted material (the two excluded ST-P items) as non-canonical and not usable for this recommendation.

This is a planning-level recommendation only — it does not constitute Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
