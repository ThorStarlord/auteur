**Recommendation: publish-verified-testimony**

**Why:** The series commitment `contested-history` requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — destroying the archive (burn-archive) would eliminate the official-history side of that conflict entirely, not expose it. The Book 3 Direction "The Protected Archive" established want-level investment in protecting the archive as the only evidentiary chain, and Book 3 Realization confirms a treaty now protects the archive on that basis. Burning the archive directly reverses that treaty-protected status with no accepted fact showing the treaty lapsed or was overridden. Meanwhile the monastery's testimony has been preserved since Book 1 Realization and remains available to authenticate rather than requiring destruction of the archive to surface it. Given the council's retracted admission (Book 2 Realization) as current state, verification before publication also matters: an unauthenticated testimony reentering public memory against a council that already retracted once invites the same erasure/discrediting dynamic the series pressure describes ("every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness") — verification hardens the testimony against that.

**Principal tradeoff:** Authentication takes time, delaying when the testimony reaches public memory — but this preserves the treaty-protected evidentiary chain intact.

**Excluded as not relevant:** The forged founding record (Book 1) and the identification of who falsified it (Book 2) — these bear on why the archive's credibility was ever in question, not on how to reconcile publishing new testimony with the existing protection treaty. The repaired lantern (Book 1/Book 3) is a physical-object fact with no bearing on evidentiary or public-memory questions. The series open question ("Can truth survive without becoming instrument of power?") is thematic framing, not a fact or commitment to cite as grounds.

This is a non-authoritative planning recommendation only — it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
