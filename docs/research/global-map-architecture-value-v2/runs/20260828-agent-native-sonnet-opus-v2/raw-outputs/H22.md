**Recommendation:** publish-verified-testimony

**Why:** The series commitment `contested-history` requires exposing conflict between official history and lived memory, which authenticating and publishing the monastery testimony satisfies directly. The archive is currently treaty-protected (Book 3), and the council's public position is a retracted admission — meaning official history is actively contested and unstable. Introducing the testimony through a verification process that keeps the original evidence secure preserves the treaty's protection of the archive as the sole evidentiary chain, honoring the Book 3 want to protect the archive after the retraction. It also aligns with the founding record being forged: verification matters because the council has already retracted once, and unverified material would be an easy new target for erasure per the series pressure (hidden archivists erasing witnesses in response to public correction).

**Principal tradeoff:** Verification delays public release of the testimony, leaving the corrective truth unavailable to the public for a period — but this delay is the cost of not exposing or fragmenting the protected evidentiary chain.

**Deliberately excluded:** stage-protected-hearing — while it also uses the treaty's protections, presenting the testimony beside selected archive evidence necessarily reveals which records carry the strongest evidence. Given the series pressure (corrections give hidden archivists reason to erase witnesses) and the council's history of retracting admissions under pressure, exposing the relative strength of records creates a targeting map for exactly the erasure dynamic the series is built on — a risk to the evidentiary chain the accepted facts don't show any commitment has mitigated. I did not treat the archive lantern's repaired condition as relevant to this evidentiary/testimony question, and I did not invent any resolution to the series open question about truth surviving power — that remains open.

This is a planning recommendation only; it does not create Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
