**Recommendation: witness-account**

**Why:** The series commitment `contested-history` requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — witness-account puts that conflict directly on stage, whereas cover-up-trace keeps the focus on institutional mechanics and only implies the lived-memory side. Book 1's Direction "The Missing Ledger" already established the core tension as authenticating the record while choosing whether to expose witnesses, and the Book 1 Realization that the monastery preserves a testimony gives Book 2 a concrete witness account to center — this option is the direct continuation of that unresolved tension rather than a new thread. It also advances the series open question ("Can truth survive without becoming instrument of power?") more sharply, since a living witness's account testing itself against a forged record dramatizes what "truth" costs a person, not just an institution.

**Principal tradeoff:** Centering the witness advances lived memory and the series' central conflict, but exposes the witness early — which sits in direct tension with the Book 1 resistance fact that custodians erase witnesses. Book 2 would need to reckon with that danger rather than sidestep it.

**What I deliberately excluded as not relevant:** The archive lantern's broken condition — it's an accepted fact but nothing in the question or options ties it to lived memory versus institutional history, so it doesn't bear on this choice. I also didn't treat `commitment-falsifier` (identifying the forger) as decisive here, since both options are compatible with that commitment; it doesn't discriminate between them.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
