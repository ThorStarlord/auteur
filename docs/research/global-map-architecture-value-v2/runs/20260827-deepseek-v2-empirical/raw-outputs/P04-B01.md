### 1. **Recommendation Summary**
**Recommended Option:** **Publish (Controlled Disclosure)** — with a conditional escalation to **Selective Redaction** if treaty constraints bind.

**Rationale (one line):** The archive is treaty-protected and cannot be burned; publishing via a contested-history lens is the only legally viable path that advances the reactivated monastery-testimony trigger without violating current-state constraints.

### 3. **Reasoned Justification**

**Primary constraint:** The archive is treaty-protected. Burning is categorically off the table — not merely risky, but *incompatible with the current state evidence*. Any narrative that ends in destruction fails logical consistency.

**Why Publish over Redact as primary:** The `reactivated monastery-testimony` trigger implies the testimony is *ready* and *intended* for public reckoning. The `contested-history` group (as in P03) operates on challenging official narratives — a full publish aligns with their identity. Redaction, while safer, would introduce a new layer of "what are they hiding?" that undermines the testimony's moral force.

**Why not Delay:** The horizon is Book 4; the testimony has been reactivated. Deferral wastes the narrative investment and leaves the archive-protected state as a static obstacle rather than a dynamic tension.

**Conditional caveat:** If treaty terms explicitly forbid *any* disclosure (not just destruction), then **Selective Redaction** becomes the ceiling — publish only the monastery-testimony portions that are not treaty-covered, and frame the redaction as a *deliberate ethical choice* rather than a defeat.

---

### 4. **Boundary Conditions & Caveats**

- **Treaty interpretation:** Verify whether "protected" means *no access* or *no destruction*. If it means no access, Option B fails; pivot to C.
- **Source safety:** Full publish must include a vetting step for named witnesses in `monastery-testimony` — if exposure endangers them, redact names even under Option B.
- **Narrative coherence:** The `contested-history` group must frame the publish as *evidence*, not *revenge* — otherwise the archive becomes a weapon, not a testimony.
- **Escalation trigger:** If publishing causes immediate treaty enforcement action (per `archive-protected` ref), the story must show the group *anticipating* this and having a legal/ethical counter-response ready.

---

### 5. **Decision-Ready Recommendation**

**Proceed with Option B (Publish Full Archive), contingent on:**
1. Confirming treaty allows disclosure (not just non-destruction).
2. Pre-clearing witness safety protocols.
3. Framing the publish as the culmination of `monastery-testimony` within the `contested-history` arc.

**If any condition fails → Fallback to Option C (Selective Redaction),** explicitly narrativized as a *constrained choice*, not a compromise.

**Do not** pursue Option A (Burn) or Option D (Delay) under any current-state scenario.
