# H62 — P01 rep 2

**Recommendation Analysis: P01 — Making the Forged Founding Record Matter to Lived Memory**

---

### 1. **Decision Context (Bounded)**
- **Active Dispositions (Book2):** Contested-history (DIR-SC1) and founding-record forged (ST-F1) are the only operative threads. Commitment-falsifier (DIR-SC2) is unresolved but *not* yet triggered—it awaits Book2 resolution, so it does not constrain this decision beyond requiring that P01 not prematurely resolve it. Monastery-testimony (ST-F2) is dormant (Book4 setup) and must not be advanced. Broken-lantern (ST-I1) is irrelevant and excluded.
- **Trigger Watch:** DIR-INT2 activates founding-record. This is the only trigger relevant to P01. If P01 is the activation point, the forged record must become *visible* to characters and readers as a contested object—not merely a background fact.
- **Pressure Trajectory (REL-01):** Contested-history carried Book1 and continues to exert pressure. P01 must escalate or complicate this pressure, not relieve it. The forged record should *intensify* the lived-memory conflict, not resolve it.
- **Founding-Record Setup (REL-02):** The forged record was set up in Book1 as a latent falsifier. P01 must make it *matter* to lived memory—meaning it must collide with characters’ personal or communal recollections, forcing a choice between official record and lived experience.

---

### 3. **Recommendation (Non-Authoritative)**
**Adopt A1, with a backfire twist (merge A1 + A4).**

- **Why:** A1 gives the forged record immediate, personal stakes (a character’s discovery), which is the most direct way to make it matter to *lived* memory. The backfire twist (A4) ensures the discovery does not resolve the conflict—instead, the community or authority rejects the discovery, forcing the character to choose between the official record and their own lived experience. This escalates REL-01 (contested-history pressure) without touching DIR-SC2 (falsifier remains unresolved).
- **Why-Now:** Book1 established contested-history as the pressure trajectory. Book2 must *activate* the founding-record (ST-F1) to give that pressure a concrete object. P01 is the natural activation point—the discovery is the first time the forged record enters lived memory as a *problem*, not a background fact.
- **Boundary Guard:** Do not let the character’s discovery lead to a full resolution of the falsifier (DIR-SC2). The backfire ensures the falsifier remains active. Do not reference monastery-testimony (ST-F2) or broken-lantern (ST-I1) in this scene—they are dormant/irrelevant and would dilute the focus.

The character is left isolated, but their lived memory now *contradicts* the official story—this is the pressure point.
