# O34 — P01 rep 2

**Recommendation: Option A — witness-account**

**Why (citing past commitments/facts):**  
- The series promise is that *every recovered account reveals who profits when history is controlled*. Book 1 already exposed the *founding record* as *forged* and preserved the *monastery.testimony*. Option A directly activates that testimony as a living counter-account, making the fraud personal and immediate.  
- The commitment *contested-history* requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory. Option A centers that conflict by placing the *living witness* against the *forged record* — the most direct expression of lived memory versus official history.  
- The *commitment-falsifier* (identify the person who falsified the founding record) is not yet fulfilled. Option A forces the witness to name or point toward that falsifier, advancing that commitment without waiting for a slower institutional trace.  
- The *pressure* (every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness) makes delay costly. Option A acknowledges this by making the witness’s exposure a deliberate, high-stakes choice — not an accident. Book 1’s *broken lantern* and *monastery.testimony* are already set pieces; Option A uses them as the emotional and physical locus of the witness’s account.

**Principal tradeoff:**  
Exposing the *living witness* early risks their erasure by the *hidden archivists* — the very pressure the series runs on. This forces the narrative to balance the witness’s safety against the truth’s urgency, creating immediate tension but shortening the runway for a slow-burn institutional reveal. The tradeoff is acceptable because the series’ pressure is already explicit; a protected witness who never speaks would betray the *contested-history* commitment.

**Deliberately excluded as not relevant:**  
- Option B (*cover-up-trace*) is excluded because it delays the lived-memory witness, undercutting the *contested-history* commitment and the *pressure* that demands timely correction. While tracing the cover-up is useful, it can be woven into Option A as background detail, not as the primary engine.  
- I did not consider the *broken lantern* as a plot device beyond its role as a physical artifact tied to the witness’s account — its symbolic weight is secondary to the witness’s testimony.  
- I did not speculate on the falsifier’s identity or motive, as no accepted facts support that yet. The recommendation only uses what is established: *founding record = forged*, *monastery.testimony = preserved*, *archive_lantern.condition = broken*.
