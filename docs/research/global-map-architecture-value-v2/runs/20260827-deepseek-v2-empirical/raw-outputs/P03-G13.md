# G13 — P03 rep 1

**Recommendation Analysis (Bounded, Non-Authoritative)**

**Context Summary:** Book 4 planning requires revisiting the monastery testimony (reactivated) while respecting the archive-protected treaty constraint (active). The contested-history group (founding-record + admission-retracted + archive-protected) carries a commitment from Books 1-3. The protected archive is treaty-bound, meaning any reference to it must not expose or alter its contents.

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with indirect, corroborative reference to monastery testimony — do not quote, cite, or re-open the testimony itself. Use it as a narrative trigger for character action, not as a plot device that requires archive access.**

**Reasoning (Bounded):**
- The monastery testimony is reactivated as a *relevant fact* for Book 4 planning, but the archive-protected constraint is active and treaty-bound. Direct engagement with the testimony (e.g., retrieving, verifying, or challenging it) would risk breaching the archive protection.
- The contested-history group is stable across Books 1-3; introducing a new direct link to the testimony would destabilize that group and force a re-litigation of the founding-record/admission-retracted dynamic, which is not the planning intent.
- The intent is to "return to" the testimony, not to "use" it as evidence. A return can be thematic, emotional, or investigative (e.g., a character recalls the testimony, or a new character references it secondhand) without touching the protected archive.

**Boundary Conditions:**
- Do not have any character access, quote, or paraphrase the monastery testimony verbatim.
- Do not imply that the archive contains the testimony or that the testimony is part of the protected record.
- Do not create a scene where the archive is opened, consulted, or threatened.
- The testimony may be referenced as a *memory*, a *rumor*, or a *prior statement* that a character carries, but its source must remain external to the archive.

**Alternative (Not Recommended):** If the testimony must be directly engaged, it must be framed as *already public knowledge* (superseded public-admission) and explicitly disconnected from the archive. However, this risks re-activating the resolved falsifier history and is therefore higher-risk.

**Non-Authoritative Note:** This recommendation preserves the treaty protection while honoring the reactivated relevance of the testimony. It allows Book 4 to "return" narratively without violating the archive constraint.
