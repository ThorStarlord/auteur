# V58 — P01 rep 1

**Recommendation Analysis: P01 — Making the Forged Founding Record Matter to Lived Memory**

---

### 1. **Decision Context (Bounded)**
- **Active Dispositions (Book2):**  
  - **Contested-History (DIR-SC1):** Carried from Book1; public corrections pressure every narrative choice.  
  - **Commitment-Falsifier (DIR-SC2):** Unresolved until Book2; must be advanced, not deferred.  
  - **Founding-Record Forged (ST-F1):** Active constraint; the forged record is a live object, not background.  
- **Excluded (Why-Now):**  
  - **Monastery-Testimony Preserved (ST-F2):** Dormant; setup for Book4, not current. Do not activate.  
  - **Broken-Lantern (ST-I1):** Irrelevant; never supports continuity. Ignore.  
- **Trigger:** **DIR-INT2** activates the founding-record — meaning the forged document must enter the lived-memory stream *now*, not remain archival.

---

### 2. **Recommendation (Non-Authoritative)**
**Option: P01-A — “The Record as a Living Wound”**  
Make the forged founding record matter to lived memory by **embedding it in a public correction that forces a character to choose between inherited identity and observed truth.**  

**Concrete execution:**  
- In Book2, a public correction (per DIR-SC1) cites the forged founding record as “authoritative” — but a character (e.g., a keeper, a witness, or a reluctant archivist) recognizes the forgery’s tell (a stylistic anachronism, a missing seal, a contradictory date).  
- The character must decide: **uphold the forged record** (to protect community stability) or **expose it** (to honor lived memory). This choice *is* the falsifier’s advancement (DIR-SC2) — the falsifier’s work becomes personal, not abstract.  
- The forged record’s content must **contradict a specific lived memory** (e.g., a founding date that clashes with a grandmother’s testimony, a name misspelled that a child knows by heart). This makes the forgery *felt* in daily life, not just in archives.  

**Why this works:**  
- It satisfies **DIR-INT2** by making the record active in the present (a correction triggers it).  
- It honors **contested-history** by using the correction as the pressure point, not a side quest.  
- It advances **commitment-falsifier** by giving the falsifier a human consequence — the falsifier’s lie now has a face.  
- It excludes dormant/irrelevant elements: no monastery testimony, no broken lantern.

---

### 3. **Risk & Mitigation**
- **Risk:** The forgery reveal feels like a plot device, not a lived truth.  
  - **Mitigation:** Tie the tell to a sensory, everyday detail (e.g., the ink smells wrong to a character who worked as a scribe; the parchment’s edge is too clean for its claimed age). Make the discovery *embodied*, not intellectual.  
- **Risk:** The public correction (DIR-SC1) becomes repetitive if overused.  
  - **Mitigation:** Use this correction as the *culmination* of Book1’s pressure — not another instance, but the one that breaks the dam.  
- **Risk:** The falsifier (DIR-SC2) remains abstract if the character chooses to expose the record.  
  - **Mitigation:** Even if exposed, the falsifier’s *method* (how the forgery was made) must be partially revealed, leaving a thread for Book2’s later resolution. The choice matters, but the falsifier’s shadow persists.

---

### 4. **Continuity Check**
- **REL-01 (contested-history pressure):** The correction is the pressure’s peak; Book2 inherits and escalates it.  
- **REL-02 (founding-record setup for falsifier):** The forged record is now *used* — the setup pays off in lived memory, not just plot.  
- **ST-F1 (founding-record forged):** Active and central; no violation.  
- **ST-F2 (monastery-testimony):** Not referenced; remains dormant for Book4.  
- **ST-I1 (broken-lantern):** Not referenced; correctly ignored.

---

### 5. **Final Recommendation**
**Proceed with P01-A.** It makes the forged record matter by forcing a lived-memory collision — a character must weigh the comfort of a false founding story against the pain of a true one. This advances both active commitments, respects the trigger, and keeps Book2’s scope tight. No dormant elements are pulled in; no irrelevant threads are touched. The record becomes a wound, not a document.
