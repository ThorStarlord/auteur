Recommendation: witness-account

Why: Two active constraints govern Book 2 planning — contested-history (every Book must expose a consequential conflict between official history and lived memory) and commitment-falsifier (the person who falsified the founding record must be identified). The current planning intent is explicitly "make the forged founding record matter to lived memory," and the active fact founding-record establishes that archive.founding_record is forged. Centering the living witness's account directly satisfies contested-history by staging the conflict as official record vs. lived memory in the most concrete form available — a person's testimony against a forged document — and it serves the planning intent (lived memory) more directly than tracing the cover-up would. It also keeps the falsifier-identification thread live: a witness's account is a natural vector for surfacing who benefited from the forgery, aligning with the series promise ("who profits when history is controlled").

Principal tradeoff: centering the witness advances lived memory and the contested-history constraint immediately, but exposes the witness early — which raises stakes against the series pressure ("every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness"). That exposure risk is real but is the cost of satisfying the current planning intent now rather than deferring it.

What I excluded and why: I did not treat monastery-testimony or broken-lantern as relevant — both are dormant (accepted facts not referenced by Book 2 planning), so they're derived support only, not active constraints, and nothing in the current question calls on them. I also did not fold in cover-up-trace as a parallel or sequenced option since the task asks for a single bounded recommendation among the two presented options; cover-up-trace would keep institutional history central and delay the lived-memory witness, which runs counter to the stated planning intent of making the fraud matter to lived memory now, not institutional history.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create Book Direction, does not resolve the series open question, and does not modify Canonical State.
