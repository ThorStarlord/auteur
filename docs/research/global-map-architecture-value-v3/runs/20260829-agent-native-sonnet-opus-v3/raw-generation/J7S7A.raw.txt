Recommendation: **witness-account**

**Why:** The series commitment "contested-history" requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory — cover-up-trace stays inside institutional history and defers that confrontation, so it doesn't discharge the commitment as directly. The monastery testimony was already established in Book 1 as preserved, not merely referenced — that's a live witness account sitting in accepted history, ready to be centered rather than left inert. Putting the witness against the forged record is also what makes the forged founding record (Book 1's central realization) matter to lived memory specifically, which is the current planning intent — a cover-up trace would make the fraud matter to institutional history instead.

**Principal tradeoff:** Centering the witness account exposes the witness early. Given Book 1's established resistance (custodians erase witnesses) and stakes (publishing too soon destroys witnesses while waiting erases truth), this is not a free choice — it activates the same danger Book 1 built up. That exposure risk is the real cost of this option, not a side effect.

**What I excluded:** The unresolved commitment "commitment-falsifier" (identifying who falsified the record) is adjacent but not decisive here — the question asked is about mattering to lived memory, not about naming the falsifier, so I didn't let that commitment drive the choice between these two specific options, though it remains open and unaddressed by either. I also didn't treat the broken lantern as relevant — it's an accepted fact but nothing in the two options or the question engages it. I did not invent any witness identity, cover-up mechanism, or institutional actor beyond what's accepted (forged founding record, preserved monastery testimony) — the "custodians" who erase witnesses are cited only as the already-accepted resistance force from Book 1's Direction, nothing further about them is assumed.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create a Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
