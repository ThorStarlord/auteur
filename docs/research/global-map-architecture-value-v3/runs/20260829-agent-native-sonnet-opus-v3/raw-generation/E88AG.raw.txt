**Why**

- The monastery testimony (fact: monastery-testimony) is explicitly reactivated for Book 4 planning — it is a current constraint the book must satisfy, not background color.
- The archive is currently in a protected state (fact: archive-protected — treaty protected) and that protection is an active constraint on Book 4, not a dormant or superseded one. Any option that destroys the archive directly violates an active constraint.

That delay is itself usable as Book 4 tension (verification under pressure, who controls the timeline) without breaking continuity.
