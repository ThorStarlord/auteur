Why: The active pressure cluster grouping monastery-testimony (reactivated, preserved) with archive-protected (treaty protected) is the binding current constraint at this horizon. Series pressure ("every public correction gives hidden archivists reason to erase another witness") also means this delay is not free narratively: the verification window itself is a plausible site for archivist countermove, but that is a story opportunity, not a constraint violation.
