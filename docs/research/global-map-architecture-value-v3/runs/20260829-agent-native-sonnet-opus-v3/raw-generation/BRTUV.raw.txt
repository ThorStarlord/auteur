Recommendation: force-council-hearing

Why: The active constraint contested-history requires Book 3 to expose a consequential conflict between official history and lived memory — a public hearing does this structurally, staging the clash on-page rather than resolving it offstage. The named-falsifier fact (accepted, though now marked dormant/irrelevant for direct citation) established the leverage this option depends on — a hearing can only be "compelled" because the falsifier is known and nameable; that causal chain is what makes the option live rather than aspirational. Most importantly, the retraction itself (council.archive_position = retracted admission, superseding the earlier public-admission) is the proximate trigger: the council walked back an admission, so the dramatically and thematically apt response is to force them back into a forum where retraction is harder to sustain, keeping accountability — not just witness survival — central to how Book 3 answers the retraction.

Principal tradeoff: the council controls the forum and timing of any hearing it's compelled into, so this option risks the council re-shaping or stalling the confrontation on its own terms — a real cost, but one that keeps the conflict public and contested rather than removing the witness from the fight.

What I excluded: publish-witness-account, while it protects the witness more directly, sidesteps the council rather than making them answer — it resolves the witness's exposure but not the retraction itself, and doesn't as directly serve contested-history's requirement that the conflict be consequential and mutual rather than parallel/avoidant. I did not rely on founding-record, monastery-testimony, or broken-lantern — all dormant, unreferenced by current Book 3 planning, and not load-bearing for this specific question. I also did not treat commitment-falsifier as an open lever; it's resolved (history support only), so I used it only as background for why named-falsifier exists, not as an active constraint being satisfied here.

This is a planning-level recommendation only — it does not create Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
