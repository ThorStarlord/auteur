Recommendation: **publish-verified-testimony**

Why: The active continuity commitment requires every Book to expose a consequential conflict between official history and lived memory, and monastery testimony is currently preserved and reactivated for Book 4 — it needs to re-enter public memory. At the same time, archive protection is active (treaty-protected), and the planning intent is explicit: return the testimony without breaking the protected archive. Publish-verified-testimony is the option that satisfies both constraints simultaneously — it authenticates and releases the testimony while the protected archive keeps the original secure, so the evidentiary chain stays intact. That matches the series pressure too: public correction is what gives hidden archivists reason to erase a witness, so keeping the original secured while publishing a verified account is the lower-exposure path for the testimony itself.

Principal tradeoff: this path delays release — authentication takes time, and "public memory" arrives later than it would under the alternative.

Why not stage-protected-hearing: it puts pressure on the conflict immediately, but it does so by revealing the archive's strongest records under treaty, which cuts against the current constraint that archive protection is treaty-protected and against the series-level danger already established (public correction → erasure risk for witnesses/records). It's not incompatible with the map, just a worse fit for the "without breaking the protected archive" framing in the current planning intent.

Excluded as not relevant:
- The founding-record forgery and named-falsifier facts — both dormant; Book 4 planning doesn't reference them, and they don't bear on how testimony is reintroduced.
- The falsifier-identity commitment — already resolved pre-Book 4, not live to this question.
- Broken-lantern — superseded by repaired-lantern, and lantern condition (either state) has no bearing on the testimony/archive-protection tradeoff.
- Public-admission — superseded by admission-retracted; even the current state (admission-retracted) is dormant/unreferenced by Book 4 planning, so neither council-admission fact was used.
- Repaired-lantern — accepted current fact but flagged irrelevant to Book 4 planning; excluded on that basis.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not constitute Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
