Recommendation: publish-verified-testimony.

Why: The active constraint cluster at this horizon is monastery testimony (preserved) coupled with archive protection (treaty protected) — the two facts Book 4 planning intent explicitly reactivates and references. The derived relationship in the ledger is explicit and deterministic: archive-protected is incompatible with destroying the archive. That derivation isn't attached to any option's label — it's a standing constraint on the Decision Map itself, and burn-archive falls on the wrong side of it regardless of how the option text frames its tradeoff. publish-verified-testimony is the only presented option that satisfies both halves of the grouped commitment at once: it surfaces the testimony (answering "return to the monastery testimony") while keeping the archive intact as evidence (answering "without breaking the protected archive"). The interpretive note about official history vs. lived memory reinforces this but isn't load-bearing — the hard constraint is the accepted fact pairing itself, not the theme.

Principal tradeoff: publishing carries a delay — release waits until verification/authentication is complete — trading immediacy of public impact for preserving the evidentiary chain intact. That's the cost the series pressure (public correction invites erasure of witnesses) makes worth paying: a rushed, unauthenticated release would itself be a form of exposure without protection.
