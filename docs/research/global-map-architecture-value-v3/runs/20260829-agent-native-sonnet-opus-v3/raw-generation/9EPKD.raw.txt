Recommendation: **witness-account**

**Why:** The narrative horizon shows two Realizations already established from Book 1 — the founding record was forged, and the monastery preserves a testimony (a living witness's account). The series commitment `contested-history` requires every Book to expose conflict between official history and lived memory, not just between official history and another official record. A forged document sitting alone doesn't create that conflict — it takes the preserved testimony being weighed against it to do so. Since Book 1 already recovered the testimony (it's accepted history, not a new invention), Book 2 centering it is the option that actually uses established facts rather than introducing new institutional machinery.

The Book 1 Direction's stakes — publishing too soon destroys witnesses, waiting erases truth — also point this way. The witness-account option's tradeoff (exposes the witness early) is the same risk the series has been building toward since Book 1; it's not a new danger, it's the payoff of the tension already set up.

**Principal tradeoff:** Centering the witness's account makes lived memory the dramatic engine of Book 2, but it exposes the witness to the erasure the custodians are established as willing to commit — raising the stakes on protecting them immediately rather than deferring that risk.

**What I excluded:** The cover-up-trace option isn't unsupported — it's a plausible institutional-history angle — but I set it aside because it delays the lived-memory witness, which cuts against `contested-history`'s requirement to expose the official/lived conflict now, in Book 2 opening, rather than postponing it further. I did not treat the broken lantern as relevant to this choice — it's an accepted fact but has no bearing on whether the fraud should be made to matter through witness or institution; it may matter to something else (e.g. authenticating evidence) but not to this particular tradeoff. I also did not invoke `commitment-falsifier` (identifying who falsified the record) since the question is about lived-memory stakes, not investigative attribution — that commitment stays open regardless of which option is chosen here.

This is a planning recommendation only — it does not create Book Direction and does not modify Canonical State.
