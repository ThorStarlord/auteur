# Story-Instance Relationship Extraction — Research Disposition

Status: suspended by architecture decision, 2026-08-31.

## Decision

Do not start Attempt 10, V1.2/V2, another model-output campaign, another
empirical harness, or production relationship extraction until Detailed
Narrative Architecture V1 is reviewed and a concrete implementation failure
creates a new empirical question.

## Established enough to proceed

- Direction and realization should compose through accepted history, current
  state, dependency, and relevance projections;
- causal traces and pressure/group reasoning deserve architectural seams;
- currentness and relevance are separate;
- richer structure should be selectively projected into current decisions; and
- the narrow repeated Map/Focus mechanism is the strongest current bounded
  continuity evidence.

## Not established

Universal relationship ontology, reliable open-world extraction, promotion of
LLM confidence to authority, cross-story generalization, human usability, and
the need for a graph database are not established.

## Evidence boundary

The V1 ledger is explicitly research-only. The auditable V2 result supports
only narrow single-fixture conclusions: current Map/Focus captured useful
activation/irrelevance/grouping mechanisms, while the richer ledger showed no
additional demonstrated value over that baseline. It does not prove automatic
extraction or authorize a production Global Map. Requested V3 and
Story-Instance Relationship Extraction V1/V1.1 artifacts were not found in
this checkout and are not inferred.

## Next empirical question

None is authorized. A future question must arise from an observed architecture
or vertical-slice failure, state its mechanism and acceptance boundary, and be
approved separately.
