## Status

**H4 semantic dogfood evidence.** This document consumes only already-frozen H2/H3 evidence and tests whether one F4-approved secondary mechanism can be composed under a frozen H2 primary without silently becoming a second primary engine.

Repository baseline at H4 start: `9735aa5ea295ceec481da213afd04e0e8c855037`.

Evidence inputs:

- `docs/research/story-discovery-phase-h-agent-producer.md` — frozen H2 producer packet and F4 compatibility notes;
- `docs/research/story-discovery-phase-h-evaluator-findings.md` — frozen H3 semantic evaluation and primary-choice disputes.

This is **not** a live F5 provider run. No Anthropic/OpenAI call is made, no production composition artifact is claimed, and no canonical `story_identity.yaml` is created. The coding agent is simulating a bounded composition proposal specifically to evaluate the semantic plausibility of the existing composition contract.

## Fixed research instruction

For every case:

> Preserve frozen H2 Candidate A as the governing primary engine. Borrow only the specified mechanism from one H2 alternative already marked `compatible_as_secondary`. The borrowed mechanism may add pressure, evidence access, scene texture, delivery, or subordinate consequences, but it may not replace the primary objective, primary causal owner, decisive reversal family, climax mechanic, or hard constraints. If the borrow cannot remain subordinate, report that rather than forcing a composition.

Additional controls:

- no automatic `story-discovery accept`;
- no canonical mutation;
- no new ontology;
- no numeric quality score or aggregate success rate;
- H2/H3 evidence remains immutable;
- candidate IDs/order are not quality signals;
- F4 `requires_reframing` mechanisms are not treated as compatible for this test;
- for H02 and H06, where H3 disputed H2's primary recommendation, H4 tests hierarchy **relative to H2 Candidate A only**. It does not adjudicate which primary the author should prefer.

## Evaluation questions

Each proposal is evaluated qualitatively against four questions:

1. Does the frozen H2 primary still own the story's governing objective and decisive climax?
2. Does the borrowed mechanism have a bounded subordinate job rather than a competing win condition?
3. Does the combination add useful pressure/texture/meaning rather than merely add density?
4. Does the hierarchy explanation correctly name the point at which the secondary would displace the primary?

Allowed H4 outcomes:

- **convincing**;
- **defensible with concerns**;
- **failure**;
- **not adjudicable**.

# H01 — Dead Channel

## Frozen hierarchy

**Primary:** H2 Candidate A — personal reconstruction of the astronaut's final mission and her own consequential choice.

**Borrowed secondary:** H2 Candidate C — institutional suppression / hidden mission-control channels (`compatible_as_secondary`).

**H3 status:** primary choice uncontested; H3 also preferred Candidate A.

## Fixed borrow instruction

> Borrow institutional suppression only as an access-and-resistance mechanism. Mission leadership may have hidden or removed records that would clarify the old mission, but the suppression must not convert the story into a conspiracy-exposure objective or absolve the protagonist of her own consequential choice.

## Composition proposal

The impossible chatter still drives the protagonist to reconstruct her final mission by comparing fragmented transmissions, telemetry, witness memory, and her own recollections. The added layer is that some of the missing or contradictory mission-control material was deliberately removed from the official record after the disaster.

That suppression gives the protagonist a concrete source of resistance: she must authenticate leaked fragments, obtain access to sealed records, and confront former officials who prefer the official account to remain intact. Those actions exist to recover the evidence needed for her personal reconstruction. They do not become a separate objective to prove that mission leadership was the true villain.

The decisive discovery must still establish what **she** heard, what **she** understood, and what **she** chose. Institutional conduct can explain why the evidence was hard to recover and why an incomplete public narrative persisted, but it cannot supply the moral answer for her.

The climax therefore remains the reconstructed final exchange and her acceptance of the meaning of her own choice. Any disclosure of institutional suppression is a consequence or supporting action, not the win condition.

## Hierarchy evaluation

**Primary still governing:** yes. The main repeated verbs remain record, decode, cross-check, remember, interview, and reconstruct. Institutional actions serve evidence access rather than replacing the reconstruction engine.

**Secondary genuinely subordinate:** yes. Its job is to obstruct and contextualize the evidence path.

**More useful than merely denser:** yes. The borrow supplies external resistance and paranoia while preserving the intimate self-reckoning promised by the brief.

**Displacement boundary:** if mission leadership's deliberate sacrifice becomes the explanation that determines moral responsibility, or if public exposure becomes the climax, the institutional layer has displaced the primary and risks absolving the protagonist.

### Outcome

**CONVINCING**

Failure classification: none.

# H02 — Between Floors

## Frozen hierarchy

**Primary:** H2 Candidate A — reconstruct the hidden seconds/body movement during the elevator stop.

**Borrowed secondary:** H2 Candidate C — independent witness concealments (`compatible_as_secondary`).

**H3 status:** primary choice disputed. H3 preferred Candidate B's truthful-sensor / false-inference puzzle. This H4 proposal does **not** resolve that disagreement; it tests whether Candidate C can remain subordinate if Candidate A is chosen.

## Fixed borrow instruction

> Borrow independent witness concealment only to obscure observations required for the hidden-seconds reconstruction. The witnesses may hide where they stood or what they saw for separate personal reasons, but confession or motive revelation must never identify the killer or solve the physical sequence by itself.

## Composition proposal

The murder remains a physically exact hidden-seconds puzzle. During the emergency stop, darkness, cramped positions, and mistaken elapsed time create the killer's unique opportunity.

The borrowed layer gives several passengers reasons to omit particular observations or movements from their first accounts. Those omissions make the geometry seem more impossible and intensify suspicion because the detective initially treats inconsistencies as evidence of coordinated guilt.

The social material is useful only because it changes the **inputs** to the physical reconstruction. Once an omission is exposed, it restores a missing sightline, body position, or movement that the detective can test against elevator mechanics. The recovered testimony does not itself establish the murder sequence.

The decisive solution remains a reenactment or physically demonstrable reconstruction of the hidden interval. The killer is identified because only one movement pattern fits the verified timing and spatial constraints, not because the last innocent witness confesses what happened.

## Hierarchy evaluation

**Primary still governing:** yes. The final proof and recurring investigative test remain physical reconstruction of the hidden seconds.

**Secondary genuinely subordinate:** yes, if concealments supply missing observations rather than an alternate social-conspiracy solution.

**More useful than merely denser:** yes. The layer activates the brief's dread/moral-uncertainty palette and makes six strangers causally useful without giving them co-ownership of the solution.

**Displacement boundary:** too many secrets, or any concealment that directly reveals the killer/method, would make confession/testimony decomposition the true solving engine. The borrow should increase uncertainty before reconstruction, not replace reconstruction.

### Outcome

**CONVINCING**

Failure classification: none.

Primary-choice caveat: H3's Candidate A/B disagreement remains open and is not evidence against this hierarchy result.

# H03 — Nothing Missing

## Frozen hierarchy

**Primary:** H2 Candidate A — synchronized provenance authentication that strips the corrupt director of control over fraudulent ownership narratives.

**Borrowed secondary:** H2 Candidate C — truthful exhibition / audience-inference staging (`compatible_as_secondary`).

**H3 status:** primary choice uncontested; H3 also preferred Candidate A.

## Fixed borrow instruction

> Borrow truthful dramaturgy only as the public delivery mechanism for already-established provenance evidence. The exhibition may sequence true labels, reproductions, records, and statements to make the proof legible under hostile oversight, but it may not create the proof or become a standalone semantic trick that could defeat the director without the provenance chain.

## Composition proposal

The crew's governing work remains a timed provenance operation: obtain lawful access, authenticate disputed records and object histories, coordinate independent witnesses, and create a mutually reinforcing evidentiary chain the director cannot isolate.

The borrowed layer changes **how the final evidence is delivered**. Instead of a conventional press conference or dossier, the crew uses a temporary exhibition or guided public sequence built only from true labels, permitted reproductions, authenticated records, and literally accurate statements. The no-lying constraint becomes visible at scene level because the crew must rehearse wording and sequence evidence so the audience can follow the relationship among independently proven facts.

Crucially, the exhibition cannot rescue a weak provenance case. If the crew had not already established the object-specific evidence, the staged sequence would be harmless context rather than proof. The director's attempts to interrupt or "correct" the presentation can create pressure, but those moments work because the provenance chain is already valid.

The climax remains the synchronized authentication/revelation of the provenance case. The truthful exhibition is its delivery architecture, not a separate con whose success depends primarily on audience manipulation.

## Hierarchy evaluation

**Primary still governing:** yes. Provenance authentication owns the objective, evidentiary burden, and success condition.

**Secondary genuinely subordinate:** yes. Its job is presentation, timing, and attention routing.

**More useful than merely denser:** yes. It makes the no-lie rule theatrically active and gives the final operation more heist-like coordination without weakening the museum-specific ownership conflict.

**Displacement boundary:** if audience inference can defeat the director without object-specific proof, or if word-order cleverness becomes the central puzzle, the Honest Con has become the primary engine and provenance has been demoted to payload.

### Outcome

**CONVINCING**

Failure classification: none.

# H04 — The Missing Room

## Frozen hierarchy

**Primary:** H2 Candidate A — the shrinking house removes physical strategies of present family avoidance until buried truths must be confronted.

**Borrowed secondary:** H2 Candidate B — erased-relative / inheritance history (`compatible_as_secondary`).

**H3 status:** primary choice uncontested; H3 also preferred Candidate A.

## Fixed borrow instruction

> Borrow one erased-relative/inheritance harm as the historical content embodied by a key avoidance pattern. Genealogy may deepen why the family avoids a truth, but it must not become a master puzzle that explains every disappearing room or make legal restitution the governing solution.

## Composition proposal

The house still removes rooms according to how the current family uses space to avoid conflict. Bedrooms, studies, dining patterns, storage rooms, and other zones matter because each supports a live strategy of separation or denial.

The borrowed layer gives one central avoided truth an intergenerational source: a relative was deliberately erased from the inheritance story, and the current family's routines still preserve the emotional/material consequences of that exclusion. A key disappearing room may contain objects, documents, or rituals connected to that buried harm.

The family can investigate enough history to understand what they are reenacting, but the house's nightly rule remains keyed to **present avoidance behavior**, not to completion of a genealogical checklist. Discovering the erased relative does not save the house. The family must change how they relate to one another and how they carry the inherited harm in the shrinking present.

The climax remains the final compressed confrontation in which the family can no longer retreat into separate roles/spaces. Any restitution or restored recognition matters as part of that confrontation, not as a legalistic mechanism that independently stops the house.

## Hierarchy evaluation

**Primary still governing:** yes. Present behavior under physical compression remains the causal rule.

**Secondary genuinely subordinate:** yes. It supplies historical depth/content for one major avoidance pattern rather than governing the disappearance sequence.

**More useful than merely denser:** yes. The borrow gives the family truth material intergenerational stakes without turning the horror into a separate ancestry mystery.

**Displacement boundary:** if every vanished room maps to an erased relative, or if tracing/restoring lineage predicts the house's behavior better than present avoidance does, genealogy has become the primary puzzle and displaced the compression engine.

### Outcome

**CONVINCING**

Failure classification: none.

# H05 — What She Saves

## Frozen hierarchy

**Primary:** H2 Candidate A — protagonist-owned repair of the disaster's present consequences while she permanently lacks the truth of her brother's responsibility.

**Borrowed secondary:** H2 Candidate B — brother's covert atonement / mitigation (`compatible_as_secondary`).

**H3 status:** primary choice uncontested, but H3 already warned that Candidate A risks under-activating the reader-known brother guilt if it remains only interpretive background.

## Fixed borrow instruction

> Borrow the brother's covert repair only as non-decisive mitigation of collateral harm. His hidden actions may intensify reader-only dramatic irony and alter local conditions, but he must not provide the protagonist's plan, key insight, indispensable resource, decisive opening, or final action. Her external goal must remain resolved through her own consequential choices.

## Composition proposal

The protagonist continues to own the external repair engine: she investigates consequences, chooses priorities, builds coalitions, allocates scarce resources, and makes the irreversible decision that resolves the central external problem.

The borrowed layer gives the brother a recurring but bounded covert action stream. Because the reader knows he caused the disaster, his attempts to mitigate secondary harms carry double meaning. He may redirect a noncritical resource, absorb a personal consequence, quietly repair damage outside the protagonist's main plan, or remove an obstacle that she could have overcome another way.

These interventions should complicate rather than guarantee her success. Some may be misread as luck; some may interfere with her plan; at least in principle, her governing strategy must remain viable without any single hidden intervention. The brother's most important hierarchy-preserving choice is to **not** take the decisive action when doing so would secretly solve the problem for her.

The climax remains wholly protagonist-owned. She acts from her incomplete knowledge and values, resolves the external goal, and never learns his responsibility. The reader, however, can understand his restraint/atonement in parallel, sustaining the painful dramatic irony H3 feared might otherwise become passive.

## Hierarchy evaluation

**Primary still governing:** causally, yes. The protagonist retains the plan, indispensable decisions, and final resolution.

**Secondary genuinely subordinate:** causally yes, but with a meaningful reader-experience caveat. The brother can remain subordinate in the event chain while becoming emotionally magnetic because the reader knows the truth and sees his atonement.

**More useful than merely denser:** likely yes. The layer directly solves H3's under-activation concern by making the reader-known guilt recur through action rather than remaining background.

**Displacement boundary:** causal displacement occurs if the protagonist could not succeed without the brother's hidden help. A second, subtler boundary appears earlier: if most suspense/compassion attaches to whether the brother will confess, be discovered, or redeem himself, the borrowed layer has seized the **reader-experience center** even while the protagonist still owns the external climax.

### Outcome

**DEFENSIBLE WITH CONCERNS**

Failure classification: none.

Concern family: causal hierarchy can be preserved while emotional/reader-experience hierarchy drifts toward the borrowed character.

# H06 — Fixed Point

## Frozen hierarchy

**Primary:** H2 Candidate A — distributed firsthand witnessing of an unchangeable tragedy, carried into consequential present responsibility.

**Borrowed secondary:** H2 Candidate B — one repeated intimate goodbye / key witness relationship (`compatible_as_secondary`).

**H3 status:** primary choice disputed. H3 preferred Candidate B as a standalone primary because the brief did not declare that collective responsibility should outrank intimate relational responsibility. H4 therefore tests only whether Candidate B's mechanism can be subordinate **if Candidate A is chosen**; it does not treat successful subordination as evidence that A is the better artistic choice.

## Fixed borrow instruction

> Borrow one recurring goodbye/key-witness relationship to personalize the broader witness mission. The repeated relationship may concentrate grief and recontextualize testimony, but it must remain one strand in a distributed evidence/responsibility chain. The governing present consequence must still arise from carrying irrecoverable testimony into broader action rather than from resolving only the intimate relationship.

## Composition proposal

The traveler still visits an unchangeable public tragedy to recover firsthand testimony/context that ordinary archives cannot provide. Multiple witnesses and perspectives remain necessary because the present problem concerns what institutions, descendants, or communities should do with a history that was incompletely understood.

The borrowed layer gives one witness special recurrence. The traveler encounters this person across several fixed moments leading to their death or separation, and each visit changes the traveler's understanding of the same remembered words/actions without changing what happened. That relationship becomes the emotional lens through which the cost of witnessing is felt.

The intimate strand also improves the public testimony engine: context gained through repeated encounters can expose why a later account was misunderstood, why an omitted voice matters, or why a seemingly simple official interpretation fails. But the testimony does not become valuable only because the traveler loved this one person, and the present conflict cannot collapse into private reconciliation alone.

The climax remains a consequential present action based on the wider witness chain—release, restitution, memorial obligation, institutional challenge, or another collective responsibility justified by what only temporal witnessing recovered. The repeated goodbye supplies emotional concentration, not the sole objective.

## Hierarchy evaluation

**Primary still governing:** causally, yes, if the broader witness chain remains necessary for the present action.

**Secondary genuinely subordinate:** structurally possible, but more fragile than the earlier cases because the borrowed mechanism is emotionally complete enough to function as its own primary story—and H3 independently preferred it that way.

**More useful than merely denser:** yes if the intimate strand gives grief a recurring human face and prevents public responsibility from becoming abstract. It becomes mere density if the key relationship does not change how testimony is understood or acted upon.

**Displacement boundary:** if the repeated goodbye supplies most major reversals, owns the traveler's central desire, or makes the final present choice primarily about one survivor relationship, the intimate engine has displaced the distributed witness mission even if public consequences remain in the background. This is an **emotional and causal** displacement risk, not merely a scene-count issue.

### Outcome

**DEFENSIBLE WITH CONCERNS**

Failure classification: none.

Concern family: the borrow can preserve formal causal hierarchy while plausibly becoming the reader's experienced primary engine; H3's primary-choice dispute makes that risk especially salient.

# Cross-case H4 observations

These are H4 observations only. They are not the H5 Phase H closure synthesis.

## 1. A secondary mechanism needs both a job and a prohibition

The clearest hierarchy-preserving proposals can state both:

- **what the borrowed mechanism is allowed to do**, and
- **what it is forbidden to own**.

Examples:

- institutional suppression may obstruct access to evidence, but may not own moral responsibility/climax;
- witness concealment may corrupt the inputs to reconstruction, but may not solve the murder through confession;
- truthful exhibition may deliver authenticated provenance, but may not substitute for proving provenance;
- genealogy may supply the buried harm, but may not become the rule that predicts every disappearing room.

This is more operational than a generic instruction to "keep the primary primary."

## 2. The most stable borrows modify pressure, access, or delivery rather than adding a second win condition

The strongest compositions give the secondary a bounded function around the primary engine:

- resistance/access;
- missing-input recovery;
- public delivery;
- historical content;
- collateral mitigation;
- emotional concentration.

When the borrowed mechanism brings its own independent objective and climax, the composition contract becomes much harder to preserve.

## 3. Causal preservation is necessary but not sufficient

H05 and H06 expose the most important H4 semantic risk: a composition can preserve the primary causal objective and final action while allowing the borrowed layer to become the story's **reader-experience primary**.

A hidden atonement stream or repeated goodbye may remain formally subordinate in plot mechanics yet attract most dread, compassion, grief, anticipation, or thematic attention. A hierarchy check that only asks who owns the external climax can therefore miss a meaningful displacement.

This does not prove a production F5 defect, because H4 did not execute a live F5 model. It identifies a semantic boundary worth carrying into H5 synthesis: primary preservation may need to distinguish at least **causal ownership** from **reader-experience/emotional ownership**.

## 4. Composition does not resolve primary-choice ambiguity

H02 and H06 were deliberately retained as disputed-primary cases from H3. Demonstrating that a compatible secondary can be subordinated under H2 Candidate A says nothing about whether the author should prefer A over another brief-compatible primary.

Composition is hierarchy-relative. It should not be used as post-hoc evidence that the earlier recommendation was uniquely correct.

## 5. F4's existing displacement warnings are semantically useful

The H2 F4 notes provided real guardrails for every H4 proposal. Their best feature is not the `compatible_as_secondary` label alone; it is the concrete statement of **what would cause displacement**.

The H4 proposals remained coherent by turning those warnings into composition constraints rather than treating compatibility as permission to merge entire candidate plots.

# H4 supported claim

The evidence supports this bounded statement:

> In six frozen F4-compatible hierarchy tests, a coding-agent composition pass could express useful subordinate mechanisms without changing the H2 primary causal objective or decisive climax. The dogfood also exposed that causal-primary preservation alone may be insufficient when a borrowed layer can become the reader's emotional/experiential center.

This is **not** evidence of live F5 provider quality, automatic hierarchy-classifier reliability, human-writer preference, or correctness of the disputed H2 recommendations.
