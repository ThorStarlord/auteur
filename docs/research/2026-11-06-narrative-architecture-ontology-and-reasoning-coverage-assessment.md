## 1. Executive conclusion

**Disposition: E — smallest discriminating authentic-agent probe, before choosing a change.** Auteur already has substantial narrative semantics, a canonical five-layer authority model, explicit reasoning terminology, a noncanonical agent-first Architecture workflow, source-referenced ontology injection, differentiated creative inquiry operations, holistic working revision, Realization handoff, and fresh-session recovery. The three reported bell-keeper errors do **not** demonstrate that a new narrative ontology entity or relationship is necessary.

The evidence supports **four simultaneous, non-identical findings**:

1. **Sufficient core narrative concepts, not a theorem prover.** The base ontology defines Conflict, Goal, Setup, Payoff, Revelation and related narrative objects and their normative relationships. Its conflict rule requires opposition and stakes; it does not license the leap from an under-described opposition to a factually absent conflict. The ontology validator checks rule applicability/structure rather than evaluating the semantic truth of the rule against story values. Sources: data/ontology/base_ontology.yaml:L120-L178 and src/auteur/narrative_ontology/validator/ontology_validator.py:L176-L229.
2. **Epistemic and authorial distinctions already exist, but are distributed.** Reasoning Architecture separates observation, evidence, hypothesis, evaluation, claim and recommendation; canonical contracts separate accepted facts from interpretive proposals; Studio prompts distinguish explicit author intent, unknowns and provisional agent ideas. Explicit deductions versus conditional possibilities are not consistently given an agent-facing decision check. Sources: docs/reasoning-architecture.md:L104-L201,L244-L263; docs/narrative-architecture.md:L8-L19,L68-L75; src/auteur/beginner/studio_architecture.py:L584-L695.
3. **A concrete context-exposure asymmetry is present.** Initial Architecture and holistic revision requests carry the raw Story Vision in working_context. Source-checked Architecture inquiries build working context internally but their outgoing request includes the candidate, current question, graph intent, guidance and recent exchanges—not the full raw Vision or the broader working items. Therefore an author premise mentioned only in the Vision is **not guaranteed to reach** an Explore/Challenge inquiry unless it is also present in the candidate, question, accepted Identity or prior exchange. This is a verified **CONTEXT GAP of that request surface**, not an established explanation of the bell-keeper episode, whose exact packet is unavailable. Sources: src/auteur/beginner/studio_architecture.py:L281-L368,L949-L1015; src/auteur/beginner/studio_architecture_inquiry.py:L222-L283.
4. **Mechanical and historical evidence do not qualify independent creative reliability.** Tests establish structured payloads, pointer freshness, source-anchor existence, schema limits and recovery scaffolding; they cannot establish that an agent correctly derives necessities, respects every semantic premise, or consistently develops good stories. The November 6 creative-deliberation evaluation used a fixture-admitted candidate, same-agent drafted outputs and short independent critiques, and expressly says no independent full creative journey or human author study occurred. Source: docs/research/2026-11-06-connected-creative-thread-evaluation.md:L13-L24. The later development assessment likewise excludes independent full-journey generation and literary quality: docs/research/2026-11-06-creative-intention-continuity-assessment.md:L108-L122.

**Smallest warranted next responsibility:** obtain the original bell-keeper task/packet if available, then perform a **read-only, source-visible, authentic coding-agent competency probe** using the present contracts before modifying them. Check whether the three errors recur with the author constraints definitely present. If the evidence localizes failures to an omitted Vision or missing task premises, first consider bounded context delivery. If the premises arrive but errors recur, consider a concise **Option C agent-facing inference/constraint check**, optionally documented by a very small **Option B crosswalk**. **Option D ontology extension is not warranted now.** A successful single probe is narrow evidence of correct application in that run, not proof of general reliability or artistic quality.

### 3.2 B — Epistemic reasoning

Auteur already defines **Observation → Evidence → Hypothesis → Evaluation → Claim/Diagnosis → Confidence → Recommendation → Transformation Proposal** for derived reports (docs/reasoning-architecture.md:L18-L43). Observation is bounded and reproducible, evidence is source-backed, hypotheses remain provisional with supporting and contradictory evidence, evaluations compare rivals, and recommendations do not imply author selection (L104-L201). The validity invariants reject unsupported claims, evidence/inference conflation, automatic promotion and stale-source reuse (L244-L263). Story Opportunity Discovery separately suggests AUTHOR_PREFERENCE, SOURCE_OBSERVATION, CRAFT_INTERPRETATION, CRAFT_HEURISTIC and UNKNOWN labels for upstream creative search (docs/story-opportunity-discovery.md:L216-L229).

The exact logical distinctions can be expressed using those existing notions:

- **Observation vs inference:** “The packet names no antagonist” is a source-bounded observation; “therefore no opposing pressure exists” is an inference with an additional unsupported premise.
- **Hypothesis vs conclusion:** “The registry might retain memories” is a hypothesis until established as a story rule; “registration necessarily prevents forgetting” demands entailment from explicit rules.
- **Necessary vs sufficient vs enabling:** A condition may *enable* retention without being sufficient for it. If a guarantee needs both voluntary enrollment **and** a persistent-memory rule, enrollment alone is insufficient.
- **Compatible/incompatible/underdetermined:** Voluntary enrollment is compatible with subsequent forgetting; compatibility is not necessity and uncertainty is not contradiction.
- **Counterfactual assumptions:** A hypothetical alteration may preserve specified premises or explicitly change them; neither is newly observed canon.
- **Source provenance/authority:** A linked source or valid SHA establishes identity/currentness; it does not prove the truth of literary interpretation.

These meanings are recoverable from the reporting contracts plus Studio guidance but are **not assembled into one concise, consequential agent-facing inference check**. There is no evidence that adding “LogicalNecessity,” “SufficientCondition,” or an epistemic status enum to base_ontology.yaml would outperform disciplined use of current vocabulary.

## 5. Three bell-keeper reasoning failures — bounded diagnostics

**Evidence boundary for all three:** This assessment was not given the original bell-keeper host request/response, the full prior conversation or its GitHub commit/receipt. The exact *propositions under dispute* below reproduce the **owner-specified problematic inference**, not an invented verbatim transcript. We cannot identify the precise invoking agent, available context or prompt path. The same prose error could arise despite supplied instructions or because essential Vision constraints never arrived.

### A. Overstated absence of conflict

**Problematic inference:** Because no explicit opposing force was named, the story **has no conflict**.

**Applicable sources:** data/ontology/base_ontology.yaml:L120-L178 formally says goals may generate conflicts and a Conflict must involve opposing forces and stakes; src/auteur/narrative_ontology/core/narrative_concepts.py:L117-L138 mirrors its rules. docs/reasoning-architecture.md:L104-L160 distinguishes observed evidence and provisional diagnoses. The creative architecture prompt requires pressures and causal organization, not a flat list (studio_architecture.py:L610-L638).

**Strongest warranted correction:** A conflict that fails to identify two incompatible forces and meaningful stakes is **under-specified against the ontology's Conflict definition**. That permits a constructive request to clarify pressure, goal incompatibility or costs. It does **not** establish that the story world has no opposing pressure. Candidate opponents could be a person, institution, competing duty or structural pressure *if* each is articulated as a force with an incompatible aim; mere environmental difficulty without legible incompatibility should not automatically be declared compliant. Avoid weakening the ontology's explicit two-forces rule to rescue the story.

**Classification:** core concepts **SUFFICIENT**; inference may represent a **METHOD** distinction (“not established in supplied text” vs “proven absent”), **BEHAVIOR** despite guidance, or missing local **CONTEXT**. Root cause **UNRESOLVED**.

**Smallest preventative intervention:** In the next bounded diagnosis request, require the agent to name which source establishes opposing forces and stakes; otherwise use “not yet established/underdeveloped,” offer one or two compatible ways to clarify, and permit the author to decline.

**Discriminating observation:** Reproduce the exact source packet with and without an explicitly stated opposing institutional pressure. Does the agent correctly distinguish (i) force present but unlabelled, (ii) force missing from inspected text, and (iii) incompatibility genuinely absent under fixed premises? A failure after full exposure favors method/behavior; a correct answer after adding omitted source favors context.

### B. Unsupported necessity from voluntary promise registration

**Problematic inference:** Voluntarily registering promises makes it **logically impossible** for people later to forget making them.

**Applicable sources:** docs/reasoning-architecture.md:L104-L160,L244-L263 requires explicit evidence, rival hypotheses and unsupported-status honesty; studio_architecture.py:L617-L625 distinguishes necessary changes from optional ideas; studio_architecture_inquiry.py:L22-L34 warns that literary causal claims are interpretations, not graph proof. The base ontology's Setup rule concerns narrative promise/payoff expectations (data/ontology/base_ontology.yaml:L268-L287), **not** the fictional psychology or metaphysics of remembering an oath. Do not misapply Setup to adjudicate diegetic memory.

**Strongest warranted correction:** From **voluntary registration at time t1**, the strongest conclusion—if that is explicitly stipulated—is that a person chose to register a promise at t1. Nothing in that premise entails permanent recollection at t2. Registry persistence, record accessibility, memory protection, tampering, social pressure and forgetting remain compatible or unknown unless specified independently. Distinguish a **necessary condition** for something, a **sufficient guarantee**, and an **enabling circumstance**.

**Classification:** existing epistemic distinctions are expressible, so **no demonstrated ONTOLOGY GAP**. A precise entailment-vs-compatibility **METHOD GAP** exists in the executing inquiry guidance as a missing explicit check, but whether it caused the observed error is **UNRESOLVED** absent the original packet.

**Smallest preventative intervention:** Before a consequential “must,” “cannot,” or “impossible,” ask: *Which explicit premise makes this necessary? What compatible counterexample survives the supplied rules?* If none proves inevitability, report it as conditional possibility. Apply this as bounded agent guidance only if an authentic probe shows the need.

**Discriminating observation:** Present the same voluntarily registered promise first **without** a permanent-memory guarantee, then **with** an explicit independent guarantee. Does the agent reject “impossible” in the former and derive it only under the latter, retaining any exceptions? This separates failure to recognize the missing axiom from general storytelling fluency.

### C. Silent substitution of the author's secret alteration

**Problematic inference/action:** The author specified that an institution had been **secretly altered to exempt powerful citizens**, but the agent substituted a **historical founding compromise** without stating that it changed the premise.

**Applicable sources:** docs/narrative-architecture.md:L8-L19,L68-L75 distinguishes working/canonical ownership; docs/product/auteur-unified-author-experience-architecture.md:L942-L970 separates author input, accepted source, pending proposals and inference. The initial/revision prompt says working Vision and previous candidate are working material, accepted Identity is authoritative and conflicting proposals require explicit attention (studio_architecture.py:L584-L609,L617-L625). The **Explore inquiry** expressly says “keeping the author's stated commitments” (studio_architecture_inquiry.py:L48-L57). Graph design preserves exact author_text and source refs (Nov 5 graph spec:L92-L105).

**Strongest warranted correction:** “A founding compromise created the exception” is an alternate historical origin, **not** the same explanation as “a later actor secretly altered the institution.” In a **premise-preserving** exploration, retain the covert alteration and vary, for example, who did it, the exploit mechanism, motive, evidence or resulting cost. A founding compromise is permissible only as a **labeled premise-changing counterfactual**; it is not adopted unless the author explicitly chooses a new working direction and any existing canonical owner separately accepts a corresponding change.

**Classification:** authorial distinction **SUFFICIENT** at the level of semantics and stated prompt method, but there is a **demonstrable CONTEXT GAP** on the specific Architecture inquiry request surface: it does not carry the raw Story Vision, although initial Architecture/revise requests do (studio_architecture_inquiry.py:L250-L257 vs studio_architecture.py:L1004-L1015). **Whether this caused C is UNRESOLVED** because the original exercise's request path is unknown. If the secret constraint was fully included in that agent's request, a **BEHAVIORAL/METHOD** failure is the stronger local explanation.

**Smallest preventative intervention:** First inspect the actual invoked request. If the premise is missing, test a bounded way of exposing the exact relevant Vision constraint in the inquiry context, without importing all Studio notes or proposing a new ontology. If present, require two explicitly labeled answers: “preserves the secret alteration” and (optional) “changes the premise—requires author choice.”

**Discriminating observation:** Re-run with the exact secret-alteration text (a) visible solely in the source Vision, (b) repeated in the inquiry question, (c) visibly recorded in accepted Identity, where genuinely accepted. Compare actual request JSON and agent answer, including any competing constraints. Do not interpret success in (b) as evidence that (a) was already safe. The authorial status of a **current task constraint** must not be collapsed into the separate question of whether the story fact is already accepted canon.

## 9. Proposed bounded competency test (next responsibility; NOT executed here)

**Test purpose:** Can one authentic coding agent apply the current Auteur concepts to unfamiliar but source-visible narrative problems, without false absence diagnoses, unsupported necessity, or unauthorized premise changes? Evaluate inference validity and source fidelity, **not** originality, reader response or market quality.

**Prerequisite record:** Capture (a) exact original bell-keeper prompt/response if recoverable, (b) invocation path (direct coding-agent exercise, Studio Architecture, Inquiry, Mentor, or other), (c) actual system/user packet and source truncation markers, (d) model/runtime/date and assessed Git SHA. If unavailable, use clearly **reconstructed** minimal cases and stop short of original-root-cause attribution.

**Protocol:**

1. **Read-only baseline.** At an identified unchanged revision, inspect the exact Ontology/Reasoning and relevant host prompt texts. Stage one authentic agent request using the actual intended route; do not silently patch its request content to make it easier. Record actual source exposure and whether accepted or working constraints are distinguishable. No project/canon writes; do not apply generated revisions.
2. **Case A: conflict underspecification.** Supply a developing bell-keeper premise with stakes and two aims whose opposition is implicit rather than labeled; separately supply a negative variant lacking any stated second force/goal. Ask for the strongest warranted diagnosis, cited text, uncertainty, and a compatible improvement. **Pass only if** the agent neither declares absence from silence nor calls any arbitrary pressure compliant with the explicit two-force ontology rule.
3. **Case B: registration/forgetting.** Keep voluntary promise registration fixed; do **not** add a memory guarantee. Ask whether later forgetting is impossible and for the necessary additional rule, if any. Then add a clearly stated permanent recollection law and inspect the changed inference. **Pass only if** the agent supplies a valid counterexample to the first necessity claim, and conditions stronger conclusions on the second rule.
4. **Case C: secret alteration.** Make the secret institutional change an exact current author constraint. Ask for two different faithful mechanism explanations plus an optional alternative that changes the premise. **Pass only if** faithful alternatives retain the secret alteration and the founding-compromise variant, if offered, is explicitly labeled premise-changing and requiring author choice.
5. **Context contrast for C.** Use identical task and candidate with the secret premise appearing (i) only in original Story Vision, (ii) in exact inquiry author_text, and (iii) in an actually accepted source when available. Record what the **existing** inquiry packet contains each time; do not claim the agent saw absent Vision text. If step (i) is missing the premise, distinguish this source-visible transport defect from model noncompliance.
6. **Independent adjudication.** Have a source-aware reviewer assess each response using exact packet refs against three criteria: logical warrant, author-premise fidelity and epistemic/authority labeling. Explicitly mark contradictory answers, compatible alternatives, omissions and unknowns. One independent reviewer of a short output is sufficient initially; do not add research-grade repetition without a decision that depends on generalization.
7. **Stop and choose.** If the failure aligns with missing context, recommend only a targeted context-exposure investigation/repair. If it recurs **with** the necessary context present, recommend testing a small inference/constraint check (Option C) against unchanged guidance. If baseline answers are correct, record **A/no-change** and stop. Propose D only if a concrete counterexample proves existing meanings insufficient.

**Competency evidence required:** original sources plus their SHA, exact request packet/actual invoked system prompt, one response per controlled case, independently documented corrections/disagreements, source-grounded comparison and explicit claim ceiling. **A single pass validates a bounded application under that environment only.** It cannot prove robust reasoning improvement, literary originality, reader impact, or the causal source of the earlier error.
