**Conditional incompatibilities:** For the *same pinned decision, feasible option set and value definition*, the strong claim “the local choice was warranted and best” (H17) conflicts with the strong claim “a different option would clearly have been more valuable” (a specific H8 error). But a well-reasoned local choice can coexist with poor provenance (H13), framing limitations (H1) or a later opportunity to reenter strategy (H15). A missing strategy from one supplied context (H2) and correct interpretation of it after demonstrable delivery (H5's alternative) are distinguishable states, not philosophical contradictions. No recorded historical trace resolves these alternatives.

### 2.1 Required overlap distinctions and evidentiary limits

- **H1 vs H12:** *Candidate set formation* is upstream of the executor; *mission authorization/promotion* is downstream. The Stage 4G menu is visibly bounded and Stage 4H was separately assigned. Neither fact proves the executor should have ignored its task or invented a new mission.
- **H2 vs H5:** The current/archived strategy file proves repository **availability**; only an actual choice-point context/receipt supports **delivery**. Accurate paraphrase can evidence **interpretation**; decision changes linked to that source are necessary to infer **use**. No transcript establishes historical internal uptake.
- **H7 vs H8:** An omitted mission alternative points toward option-generation/framing limits, *not* evidence that the same agent undervalued a candidate it never considered. Re-run comparison under a fixed, complete alternative set to isolate valuation.
- **H10 vs H15:** A poor mode choice given current evidence differs from a good initial choice that was not revisited after new evidence. Include a follow-up observation with a genuine decision-change opportunity.
- **H13 vs H14:** Structural completeness can support auditing. A shallow exclusion string or invented comparison can satisfy record checks; the judge needs separate evidence of real alternate consequences and decisions, not more detailed private reasoning traces.

## 3. Coverage gaps, non-failure explanations and problem boundaries

**No consequential *new top-level causal family* is missing from the divergent space.** The following refinements are important for subsequent evaluation but are already representable within its conceptual coverage:

1. **Objective validity vs objective continuity.** A perfectly preserved `GoverningObjective` can itself be misidentified, stale, ambiguous or no longer authorized. H2/H3 cover delivery/priority loss; the candidate ontological `SubordinateObjective`, `ObjectiveRevision` and authority relations cover validity *conceptually*. Convergent Evaluation must ask whether an operation can **verify which objective actually governs**, not simply carry a string forward. A new H-number or schema is not warranted merely to name this refinement.
2. **Problem discovery vs alternative generation.** C2 and C7 already contemplate discovering/reformulating the **question**, while C4 enumerates **actions for a supplied question**. H1/H7 involve candidate scarcity; the historical case does not isolate a missing *problem-framing* operation. Later evaluation should not count 12 alternative tasks as progress if all answer the wrong problem.
3. **Evidence availability vs interpretation vs uptake.** H2/H5 and C3 distinguish them, but only point-of-use provenance and outcome-linked reasoning could separate them empirically. A source read or copied citation cannot prove attention or causal influence; no hidden chain-of-thought access is required to state that limitation.
4. **Actor accountability and authorized goal change.** H12/H16, the five ownership alternatives, `MISSION.md` and the [Architecture Constitution](../architecture-constitution.md) cover who may change work scope or story canon. A legitimate owner-approved pivot must **not** be scored as objective drift.
5. **Learning vs repeated artifacts.** H15/C13 cover reassessment; E6 proposes evidence-return observations. No independently established cross-episode learning or improvement currently exists. This is an **evidence gap**, not a missing concept.
6. **Non-failure states remain material.** H17 (a local action was globally worthwhile), H18 (existing concepts suffice with better integration), H16 (scope/cost constraints) and `NO_CHANGE`/STOP/owner escalation prevent the analysis from defining “better autonomy” as “always widen search.” These are genuinely decision-changing counterexamples.

**Coverage limit:** The historical G→H trace is a single, heavily framed continuing-context research exercise. It can expose a missing *visible comparative warrant*, but not the probability of general-agent failure, hidden deliberation, the optimal alternative under equal conditions, or a causal effect of an ontology. The report is sufficient to evaluate hypotheses, **not** to confirm one.

Repurposing those operations would require an explicit decision and evidence of behavioral need.

## 6. Redundancy, compatibility and architectural ownership boundaries

### 6.1 Functional overlaps: what is additive, and what may be redundant?

| Candidate relationship | Coverage assessment | Risk if collapsed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **C2 problem discovery → C4 option generation → C5 comparison** | Complementary operations at distinct stages; each can fail while others work | A large option set for the *wrong question* appears successful; a good comparison of supplied options is misread as autonomous discovery |
| **C1 goal continuity + C3 context uptake + C7 reformulation** | Often complementary; objective correct/current, evidence delivered/used, and problem reconsidered are different claims | A prominent mission quote stands in for genuine adaptive choice |
| **C5 value evaluation + C6 cross-horizon effects + C14 reversible build** | C6 can inform predicted value; C14 can generate evidence and product value, not automatically substitute for comparison | Abstract planning wins by default or construction wins by inertia |
| **C11 event provenance + PR #543 checker + C9 challenge** | Different purposes: trace what occurred, notice declared omissions, test semantic strength | A complete form is treated as a real causal explanation or a quality pass |
| **C8 mode control + C13 feedback/reentry + C15 stopping** | Temporally complementary: current mode, return after evidence, admissible exit | A mandatory global re-check or infinite research loop defeats cheap useful action |

| **O3/O4/O5** | Some overlap in general reasoning policy; hybrid separation could be compatible with reusing an existing owner | Two competing ontologies, task routers or authority maps become inconsistent |

## 9. Explicit coverage sufficiency judgment

**Disposition: `SUFFICIENT FOR CONVERGENCE`.**

**Reasoning:**

1. The divergent report spans the consequential causal chain from objective representation, problem formation and source access through option search, evaluation, coordination, observability and learning, *plus* serious non-failure alternatives.
2. The strongest overlaps are separable by particular observations. None demands inventing unseen Stage 4G reasoning.
3. The apparent seven ontology choices are partly **orthogonal** design dimensions; the report already retains minimal/no-new-schema alternatives, Sensemaking reuse and capability-first paths. Misclassifying their compatibility, rather than missing a brand-new architecture, is the main danger to convergence.
4. Conceptual gaps (objective legitimacy, problem-first discovery, source uptake and outcome learning) are expressed sufficiently for evaluation. No concrete source shows that an additional persistent ontology entity is already required by a consuming operation.
5. Current implementation coverage is **much narrower than conceptual coverage**: the one genuine responsibility-frame checker is an optional structural advisory interface; observed automatic mission selection, substantive semantic comparison and decision-quality gains are *not established*. This uncertainty informs subsequent choices without invalidating the breadth of the alternatives.

**What this disposition does not mean:** any hypothesis is proven, any strategy was actually used at the historical choice, an autonomous agent chose badly, the right owner is identified, a new ontology is justified, the current checker is broken/useless, all future evidence is sufficient, or the next stage may implement code.

**No additional Divergent Exploration is required on the evidence currently inspected.** A later genuinely new source could reveal a material omitted mechanism; reopening exploration would then require naming the decision it changes.

## 10. Precisely bounded handoff to Convergent Evaluation

A subsequent **Convergent Evaluation** (not undertaken here) can use the report as an unranked evidence basis. It should:

- Compare **causal hypotheses separately from solutions**; prevent a documentation-only solution from being credited with problem discovery or a generic shared ontology from being credited with improving Auteur's creative judgment.
- Qualitatively evaluate what each candidate actually *changes*: the problem detected, options generated, relevant evidence used, consequences forecast, work authorized, or learned revision—not the number of schema fields, checks, or files.
- Retain **both** decision domains: Auteur's long-form creative architecture and the potential general autonomous-agent control layer; compare ownership/duplication only after each candidate's intended consumer is explicit.
- Keep incompatible location choices distinct while recognizing O1/O6/O7 and several C-capabilities as potentially compatible. Prefer no new persistent ontology when no consuming operation is established; **do not treat this as an implementation decision**.
- Test each evaluation **against counterexamples**: local clearly best; broad reentry worthwhile; current problem false; evidence incomplete; correct STOP/owner decision; superficial compliant rationale; legitimately revised governing objective.
- Identify the **one or few decision-changing unknowns** whose resolution would genuinely alter the later direction, and the cheapest credible observations; do not launch a behavioral qualification simply because it is imaginable.
- Produce an explicit comparative evaluation with evidence ceilings and unresolved ownership/authority questions. **Do not** silently adopt a roadmap, authorize new types, change story canon, or enter Architecture Synthesis/Implementation.

**Scope completed here:** one source-grounded coverage document, one documentation-only branch/PR and focused source/document-integrity verification. No additional artifact is needed to record the same analysis. Stop after Coverage Assessment and leave subsequent convergence to separately authorized work.

### Governing distinctions

1. It is not the count of hypotheses; it is actually whether competing causal mechanisms remain visible and distinguishable.
2. It is not concept vocabulary and is not operational integration; it is actually demonstrated knowledge-to-decision behavior that would justify a new capability.
3. Coverage readiness is actually permission to **evaluate alternatives**, not evidence that any alternative has already won.
