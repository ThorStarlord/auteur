A twelve-Book optimistic series about a monastery that copies books whose authors are unknown. The monks must decide, generation after generation, whether an anonymous book can be authoritative. The intended experience is earned trust in incomplete knowledge: authority is extended before it can be verified, and the series should make that extension feel both necessary and dangerous. The series must not resolve its central question by declaring anonymity either fatal or irrelevant. The ending, whenever it comes, must be chosen rather than exhausted.
