An optimistic seventy-Book series about a civilization that discovers its founding hero was a fraud. The institutions built on that story - the oath, the archive of merit, the hereditary office - still work, and still do real good. Each generation inherits a different piece of the truth: a ledger, a letter, a witness, a forged coin. The series asks whether an institution founded on a lie can be made honest without being destroyed, and whether it is worth keeping once it is honest. The intended reader experience is earned hope: every Book should make honesty cost something that the previous Book made the reader value. Never resolve the series' central question by declaring the argument over. The ending, whenever it comes, must be chosen rather than exhausted.
