An optimistic thirty-Book series about a court of translators who must render a peace treaty between two languages that cannot express the same obligations. One language has a word for a promise that can be inherited; the other has a word for a promise that cannot be forgiven. Every generation of translators inherits a treaty whose meaning has drifted. The intended experience is earned hope about being understood: comprehension is costly, partial, and worth the cost. The series should never resolve its central question by declaring that translation is impossible, and it should never resolve it by declaring that translation is easy.
